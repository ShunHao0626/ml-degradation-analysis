# Enhancement of Stability by Applying HAT-CN for Hole Modification With Good Water Resistance and Hole Extraction

ng Chen, Kai-Li Wang, Xing-Juan Ma, Chong Dong, Yue Zhang, Xiao-Mei Li, and Zhao-Kui Wang

Abstract—A creative application of using HAT-CN as a hole modification layer (HML) in positive perovskite solar cells (PSCs) is demonstrated herein. This measure successfully improves the performance of devices in many aspects. Although HAT-CN owns a similar position of Fermi level with   $  MoO_{3}  $  , there have been proved to be a much smoother interface. The devices using HAT-CN had a higher   $  V_{oc}  $   at 1.10 V over 1.06 V that was shown by devices using   $  MoO_{3}  $  . Optimized devices remained PCE over 18% for 188 h resulting from compressing recombination better for owning higher hydrophobicity. The measure that using HAT-CN for modification proposed in this work was proved that HAT-CN provides a better ability of extracting holes and owns better water-resistance to be more stable.

Index Terms—HAT-CN, hole modification layer,  $ \mathrm{MoO}_3 $ , stability.

# I. INTRODUCTION

PEROVSKITE solar cells possess low cost, simple manufacture, and high power conversion efficiencies and so on advantages [1]. The most outstanding achievement of PSCs is greatly rapid improvement in power conversion efficiency (PCE) within several years [2], such as the PCE up to 25.2%, which was achieved by MIT(Massachusetts Institute of Technology) and KRICT(Korea Research Institute of Chemical Technology) in 2019 [2]. It can be attributed to its superior ability of light absorption and excellent charge-transporting properties [3]. However, the lack of stability stops promoting it into commercialization. The hole transporting layer (HTL) in PSCs pushes forward an immense influence on transporting holes and stopping electrons so that the charge recombination is suppressed, further improving efficiency and lifetime [4]. The HTM

used most widely is Spiro-OMeTAD(2,2',7,7'-tetrakis(N,N-di-p-methoxyphenylamine)-9,9'-spirobifluorene), which mixed with Li additives achieves relatively high PCE after applying to devices [3]. The hole transporting material (HTM) which can contribute to high efficiency and stability requires a suitable energy level and high hole mobility, the interface is very important [5]–[7]. For example, Back et al. applied PSSH modified PEDOT:PSS to increase the uniformity at the interface [8], and Li et al. applied graphene oxide and PEDOT:PSS as hole extracting layer in order to reduce recombination and get efficient hole transporting [9].

In this work, HAT-CN and   $  MoO_{3}  $   were applied to devices respectively as HML, while keeping other layers same. HAT-CN works as hole injection layer in organic electroluminescence (OLED), achieving Ohmic contact at the interface [3], [10]. After experiments, it was proved that the modification efficiently improved the stability and PCE of devices via modifying the morphology of them interface between.   $  MoO_{3}  $   and HAT-CN were both deep LUMO materials and behave as strong electron acceptors, since the difference between the Fermi levels is quite small, the influence caused by energy level could be ignored [10]. This work confirms the feasibility and the unique superiority of HAT-CN material over the area of PSCs, simultaneously optimizing the thickness to 10 nm. According to the optimizing factors above, PSC devices were able to remain PCE over 18% for 188 h.

# II. EXPERIMENTAL SECTION

# A. Materials

Titanium tetrachloride (TiCl $ _4 $ ) was purchased from Sigma-Aldrich, 4-tert-butylpyridine (tBP) and Lithium bis (trifluoromethanesulfonyl) imide (Li-TFSI) were bought from 1-Material Ltd. Lead iodide (PbI $ _2 $ , 99.99%), Methylammonium iodide (CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _2 $ , 99.5%), Chlorobenzene and Spiro-OMeTAD were provided by Alfa Aesar Ltd. MAI:PbI $ _2 $  (1 mmol/ml:1 mmol/ml) was dissolved in mixing solvents of 1,4-butyrolactone (GBL) and dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) (7:3, v/v) to get MAIPbI $ _3 $  precursor solution. Spiro-OMeTAD solution was prepared by adding Li-TFSI and tBP in Spiro-OMeTAD dissolving in chlorobenzene solution. Then MoO $ _3 $  and HAT-CN were obtained from Xi'an Polymer Light Technology Corp for evaporation.

# B. Device Fabrication

FTO after standard washing was pretreated by ultraviolet ozone (UVO) for 20 min. Then   $  TiO_{2}  $   was deposited at 70 °C in solution of   $  TiCl_{4}  $   and water. The perovskite solution was spin-coated at 4000 r/min (20 s) and rapidly added chlorobenzene with 2000 r/min for 40 s. Anneal at 100 °C for 6 min and the Spiro-OMeTAD solution was spin-coated at 4000 r/min for 40 s. then   $  MoO_{3}  $   and HAT-CN were evaporated with 10 nm thickness each. Then Ag was evaporated with thickness of 100 nm as the electrode.

# C. Measurements

Current density-voltage (J–V) characteristics were conducted under 1 sun illumination using Keithley 2400 source meter under AM 1.5G solar irradiation at   $  100 \, mW/cm^{2}  $  . The scan electron microscopy (SEM) images were from Quanta 200 FEG, FEI Co. A contact angle instrument was used to measure the water invasion. The steady-state photoluminescence PL spectra of different HMLs were collected by Horiba Jobin-Yvon LabRAM HR800 with a 550 nm excitation wavelength. A UV-vis spectrophotometer PerkinElmer Lambda 750 was used to obtain the UV-Vis absorption spectra of perovskite films with HTL and HML over them. The devices were stored in dark with foil-wrapped to evaluate the stability, keeping the humidity 10% and the temperature   $  25 \, ^{\circ}C  $  .

# B. Film and Electrical Characterization

Extra capacitance exists in the modification layer resulting from ion migration which originates from Schottky barrier between the interfaces. Fig. 2(a) indicates the relationship between the plot of  $ 1/C^2 $  and  $ V $  in the linear region, we can find that HAT-CN would cause elevated built-in electric field ( $ V_{\text{bi}} $ ) and increased amount of charge carriers ( $ N_D $ ) [13]. The width of the depletion region in the active layer was increased for HAT-CN devices, resulting in a higher open circuit voltage ( $ V_{\text{oc}} $ ) for better charge separation [14]. To evaluate the hole mobility essentially, under dark conditions the space-charge-limited current (SCLC) model was applied to the device structure [15]. The results are shown in Fig. 2(b), and the device with HAT-CN exhibits higher mobility which is  $ 2.111 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}^2\text{V}^{-1}\text{s}^{-1} $  whereas that of MoO $ _3 $  was only  $ 6.436 \times 10^{-5} \text{ cm}^2\text{V}^{-1}\text{s}^{-1} $ . This result is consistent with the above device performances, which can be ascribed to the improved hole extraction ability and better Ohmic contact at the interface between spiro-OMeTAD and Ag electrode. To better understand the origin of enhancement of  $ V_{\text{oc}} $  in the optimized devices, Fig. 2(d) illustrates the corresponding energy levels herein. MoO $ _3 $  and HAT-CN are n-type materials and the interlayers modify the energy levels of the anode and wide HTMs when inserted between these two layers. Due to this ability, these materials can serve as efficient hole injectors and form Ohmic contact at the interface.
