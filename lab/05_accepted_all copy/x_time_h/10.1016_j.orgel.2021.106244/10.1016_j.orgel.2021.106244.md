# Introduction

Perovskite solar cells have turned into the most promising technique among the next generation photovoltaic devices. The power conversion efficiency of the PSCs in the laboratory has reached 25.6%, nearly approaching its theoretical Shockley-Queisser limit around 30% [1,2]. An exciton separation driving force above 0.3 eV is generally needed by other sort of solar cell devices, such as organic cells or dye-sensitized solar cells, while the exciton binding energy of the PSCs is less than 0.025 eV [3,4]. Despite the fact that the photo-generated excitons can easily be dissociated into free charge carriers in the perovskite bulk at room temperature, an efficient extraction, transport and collection of the photo-generated holes requires a layer of high-performance HTM [5]. 2,2',7,7'-tetrakis (N, N'-di-p-methoxyphenylamino) -9,9'-spirbiuorene (spiro-OMeTAD) was invented by J.Salbeck in 1997, and being applied as THM in the solar cells by Bach et al., in 1998 [6,7]. Then, Spiro-OMeTAD became the most widely used HTM since Grätzel and Snaith et al. introduced it into PSCs in 2012 [8,9]. Nevertheless, the low hole mobility of the intrinsic spiro-OMeTAD calls for a doping procedure, which in most cases is deleterious to the long-term stability of the PSC devices due to the moisture-absorbing properties of the p-dopants [10]. Developing novel dopants instead of using LiTFSI is one of the effective ways to improve the device stability [11]. Nevertheless, producing efficient dopant-free HTM is a simpler alternative technique

Dopant-free hole-transport material (HTM) is essential to the long-term stability of the perovskite solar cells (PSCs). This study has introduced a novel carbazole-based dopant-free HTM via the simple ring-closing reaction without using the expensive catalyst. It has found that the merger of a higher rigid conjugated ring into the molecular geometry of HTM is an effective way to improve its charge extraction ability, hole mobility and phase thermal stability. Consequently, a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of  $ 16.4\% $  as well as a much-improved long-term stability have been achieved based on the as-prepared C302 dopant-free HTM.

[12-15].

Designing “donor-acceptor” molecule structure can reduce the bandgap of HTM, improving the chance of energy level transition processes, thus enhancing the charge carrier concentration and hole mobility   $  [16–18]  $  . Another approach to enhance the hole mobility is to introduce the rigid-conjugated moiety into the molecule structure, therefore increasing its intermolecular   $  \pi-\pi  $   stacking and constructing the charge transport channel   $  [19–21]  $  . Nazeeruddin et al. compared photovoltaic performances of two star-shaped HTMs with a different central unit, one incorporating a flexible triphenylamine (TPA-CN) and another containing a rigid quinolizino acridine (FA-CN). In the end, PSC devices based on the rigid FA-CN obtained a PCE of 18.9%, while devices based on the flexible TPA-CN only yielded a PCE of 17.5% under the same conditions   $  [22]  $  . Tremendous efforts have also been devoted to develop the high efficiency dopant-free HTM through designing a rigid conjugated molecule, showing the effectiveness of this strategy   $  [23]  $  . Among which, ring-closing reaction catalyzed by   $  FeCl_{3}  $   have been proved to be an effective and simple technique for the thiophene-based HTM   $  [24,25]  $  . Nevertheless, strategies about how to build a conjugated ring in carbazole-based HTM under mild condition are still lacking.

Here, we have introduced an efficient carbazole-based dopant-free HTM synthesized in a mild condition via the facile  $ \mathrm{FeCl}_3 $  catalysis reaction for the first time. Furtherly, we have also synthesized a structure-similar C301 as the reference. Compared with C301, the cost to

# 2. Results and discussions

# 2.1. Material physical properties

The “backbone” of C302 is composed of eight conjugated aromatic rings, which are fixed on one plane. While the main body of C301 only possesses two carbazole rings, which are arranged in a twisted configuration judged by the DFT calculations (Fig. 1). Thus, C302 holds a higher conjugation degree as well as a larger delocalized   $  \pi  $  -system, which is in favor of a stronger   $  \pi-\pi  $   stacking effect. UV–Vis absorption spectroscopy and steady-state PL spectra were conducted in dichloromethane solution to determine the bandgap of the as-prepared HTMs (Fig. 2a). The UV–Vis spectra cut-off edge of the C302 showed 40 nm bathochromic shift compared to the C301. Moreover, the PL emission peak of C302 exhibited a small hypsochromic shift with a narrower emission peak width compared to the C301. Deduced from the formula:   $  E_{g}=1240/\lambda_{cut-off}  $   [26], the bandgap of C301 and C302 were determined to be 3.06 eV and 2.79 eV respectively. The narrower bandgap of C302 is consistent with its higher conjugation degree [27,28]. Furthermore, the highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) energy levels of the new HTMs were determined using the cyclic voltammetry (CV) with the three electrodes system [29]. The HOMO energy levels of C301 and C302 were calculated to be -5.40 eV and -5.42 eV respectively (Fig. 2b). The slightly higher HOMO energy level of C302 indicates a relatively stronger electron-donating ability of it. Consequently, the PSC device energy level alignment diagram has been displayed in Fig. 2d. Hole mobility (  $  \mu  $  ) of the HTMs were determined by the space-charge-limited current measurements based on the hole-only device. According to Mott-Gurney equation,   $  \mu_{C301}=5.70E-5\ cm^{2}\ V^{-1}\ s^{-1}  $   and   $  \mu_{C302}=5.73E-4\ cm^{2}\ V^{-1}\ s^{-1}  $   [30]. The higher hole mobility obtained by C302 may come from its higher delocalization degree and stronger   $  \pi-\pi  $   stacking effect, supported by the DFT calculation.

# 2.2. Device Photovoltaic performances

A device configuration of “FTO/c-TiO $ _2 $ /m-TiO $ _2 $ /(FAPbI $ _3 $ ) $ _{0.85} $ (MAPbBr $ _3 $ ) $ _{0.15} $ /HTM/Au” was fabricated to characterize the photovoltaic performances of the as-prepared HTMs. As shown in Fig. 5, devices based on C302 obtained a PCE of 16.4% (with a  $ V_{oc} $  = 1.01 V,  $ J_{sc} $  = 23.3 mA cm $ ^{-2} $ , FF = 69.5%), while devices based on C301 only yielded a PCE of 5.8% (with a  $ V_{oc} $  = 0.73 V,  $ J_{sc} $  = 19.6 mA cm $ ^{-2} $ , FF = 40.6%) under the same conditions (see Table 1). A higher  $ V_{oc} $  indicates a less energy loss of the C302 based device, while a higher  $ J_{sc} $  implies a higher photo-to-charge conversion efficiency of the C302 based device. Incident photo-to-electron conversion efficiency (IPCE) spectra were then conducted, and C302 based device exhibited a higher IPCE over 300–800 nm, agreeing with its higher  $ J_{sc} $ . The integrated IPCE for the C301 and C302 based devices were 18.5 mA cm $ ^{-2} $  and 22.0 mA cm $ ^{-2} $  respectively. The slightly lower value by IPCE integration than  $ J_{sc} $  obtained from  $ J-V $  curve stemmed from the difference in test light source intensity [32].

Steady-state photoluminescence spectra (PL) quenching were performed to probe the photo-generated hole extraction ability of the HTMs. When the bare perovskite film was excited with a 532 nm wavelength light, a strong PL emission peak appeared at around 780 nm. The PL emission peak became 20.1% and 6.3% of its original intensity after being cover by C301 and C302 respectively (Fig. 6 a). The higher quenching efficiency of C302 indicates that it possesses a stronger photo-generated hole extraction ability. EIS was widely applied as a tool to investigate the interfacial charge transport processes in the device. # Conclusion

A facile strategy to introduce the rigid aromatic ring into the carbazole based dopant-free HTM by the  $ \mathrm{FeCl}_3 $  catalytic reaction was proposed. Compared with C301, the cost to create a higher conjugated C302 only increased by  $ 20.8\% $ , while the PCE of PSC device based on

C302 nearly increased by twice. Besides, owing to the improved conjugation effect, phase thermal stability, hole extraction ability and hole mobility of the C302 were greatly enhanced. Without doping the hygroscopic additives, the C302 based PSC device remained 88% of its original PCE after being stored at a relatively humility of 80% (  $  \approx  $   25 °C) for 168 h, while the PSC device based on the widely used doped spiro-OMeTAD only kept 61% of its original PCE under the same conditions. Thus, designing a higher rigid conjugated molecular geometry proved to be an effective technique to synthesize the carbazole-based dopant-free HTMs, which may provide useful information to the related fields.
