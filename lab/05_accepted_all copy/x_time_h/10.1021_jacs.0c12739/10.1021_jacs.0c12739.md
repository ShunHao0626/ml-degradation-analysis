# Single-Layered MXene Nanosheets Doping   $  TiO_{2}  $   for Efficient and Stable Double Perovskite Solar Cells

Zhenxing Li,  $  ^{*}  $   Ping Wang, Chang Ma, Femi Igbari, Yikun Kang, Kai-Li Wang, Weiyu Song, Chong Dong, Yanjie Li, Jiasai Yao, Dong Meng,  $  ^{*}  $   Zhao-Kui Wang,  $  ^{*}  $   and Yang Yang  $  ^{*}  $

Cite This: J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2021, 143, 2593–2600

ACCESS

ABSTRACT: The inorganic lead-free   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   double perovskite structure is the promising development direction in perovskite solar cells (PSCs) to solve the problem of the instability of the   $  APbX_{3}  $   structure and lead toxicity. However, the low short-circuit current and power conversion efficiency (PCE) caused by the low crystallization of   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   greatly limit the optoelectronic application. Herein, we adopt a simple strategy to dope single-layered MXene nanosheets into titania (  $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $  ) as a multifunctional electron transport layer for stable and efficient   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   double PSCs. The single-layered MXene nanosheets significantly improve the electrical conductivity and electron extraction rate of   $  TiO_{2}  $  ; meanwhile, the single-layered MXene

nanosheets change the surface wettability of the electron transport layer and promote the crystallization of the   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   double perovskite in solar cell devices. Therefore, the PCE went up by more than 40% to 2.81% compared to that of a   $  TiO_{2}  $   based device, and the hysteresis was greatly suppressed. Furthermore, the device based on   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   showed the long-term operating stability. After storing the device for 15 days under ambient air conditions, the PCE still remained a retention rate of 93% of the initial one. Our finding demonstrates the potential of   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   in electron transfer material of high-performance double PSCs.

# INTRODUCTION

Since the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   perovskite was first introduced into dye-sensitized solar cells (DSSCs) in 2009, the perovskite solar cell (PSC) has made great progress through the rapid development in the recent 10 years.  $  ^{1}  $   The power conversion efficiency (PCE) of the devices based on   $  APbX_{3}  $   (A = formamidinium (FA), methylammonium (MA), or Cs; X = Cl, Br, I) PSCs has increased from 3.8% to 25.5%.  $  ^{2}  $   However, there are still two main problems in the commercialization of   $  APbX_{3}  $   PSCs: the instability of the   $  APbX_{3}  $   structure and the lead (Pb) toxicity.  $  ^{3,4}  $   A series of studies have revealed that the thermodynamic stability and moisture resistance of perovskite can be significantly improved by substituting cesium for   $  MA^{+}  $  or   $  FA^{+}  $  at site A.  $  ^{5}  $   However, the toxicity of lead still hinders the development of PSCs. As a consequence, the development of inorganic lead-free perovskite materials with nontoxicity and high stability has become the focus of future research. The current research on an inorganic lead-free perovskite structure mainly focuses on Pb replacement.  $  ^{6}  $   For instance,   $  Pb^{2+}  $   is replaced by homologue element   $  Sn^{2+}  $   or   $  Ge^{2+}  $ . However,   $  Sn^{2+}  $   or   $  Ge^{2+}  $   has poor stability and is easily oxidized to   $  Sn^{4+7,8}  $   or   $  Ge^{4+9,10}  $   which is still a great challenge to the development of homovalent elements substitution perovskite devices. As a new type of lead-free perovskite material,   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   replaces two divalent   $  Pb^{2+}  $   with a pair of heterovalent metal cations (  $  Ag^{+}  $

The energy offsets at the PSC interfaces determine the charges extraction and holes transfer.  $  ^{22}  $   Specifically, the electron transfer process occurs at the interface between the ETL and perovskite layer. Therefore, ETL is an important reason in determining the performance of a PSC, which has attracted extensive attention from researchers. ETL materials used in PSC are generally metal oxides, including   $  TiO_{2}  $  ,   $  Al_{2}O_{3}  $  ,   $  SnO_{2}  $  , ZnO, etc.  $  ^{23-25}  $   Among them,   $  TiO_{2}  $   has become the most commonly used and most studied electron transport layer because of its suitable conduction band, various deposition methods, good stability, and low cost. Many PSC based on the   $  TiO_{2}ETL  $   generally use a high-temperature sintering process. Although the PCE of the device prepared in this way is very high, it cannot be applied to a large area of flexible equipment. Compared with the anatase   $  TiO_{2}  $  , the rutile   $  TiO_{2}  $   and the perovskite layer have good lattice matching and higher electron mobility. Currently, the rutile   $  TiO_{2}  $   electron transport layer prepared by the chemical bath deposition (CBD) method and heat-treated at the temperature below 100 °C can be applied to flexible PSC devices, but processing at the lower temperature may cause defects in the electron transport layer, which causes a decline in PEC.

The two-dimensional (2D) material MXene is a new type of transition metal carbide/carbonitride, which is obtained from the ternary ceramic material MAX phase material by hydrofluoric (HF) acid selective etching of the A layer (where M and A represent a transition metal element and a group III or group IV element, etc., respectively; X represents B, C, and N). Not only can MXene show higher electrical conductivity and larger specific surface area but also the surface groups (-O, -OH, -F) of MXene can adjust the electronic properties,  $  ^{26,27}  $  which provides an opportunity for MXene application in PSCs. Here we adopt a handy strategy to synthesize a multifunctional ETL  $  \left(\mathrm{Ti}_{3}\mathrm{C}_{2}\mathrm{T}_{x}@\mathrm{TiO}_{2}\right)  $  via slowly dropping single-layered MXene nanosheets into  $  TiCl_{4}  $  solution during the deposition of dense titania  $  \left(\mathrm{TiO}_{2}\right)  $ . The high crystallization of  $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $  film was obtained on this ETL by the solvent method with annealing in the air. It is notable that the incorporation of single-layered MXene reduces the accumulation of electrons in the interface due to the increase of ETL conductivity. Meanwhile, the MXene nanosheets change the surface wettability of the ETL, which promotes the growth of crystals and inhibits the generation of pinholes in double perovskite film. The resulting  $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $  PSC exhibits a PCE of up to 2.81%. In addition, the device exhibits excellent stability, and the PCE still has a retention rate of 93% of the initial PCE, after the device was kept in the environment without encapsulation for 15 days.

# EXPERIMENTAL SECTION

Synthesis of MXene. A 1 g portion of LiF (98.5%, Sigma-Aldrich) was added to 20 mL of 6 M hydrochloric acid (HCl, Alfa Aesar) slowly, and the solution was stirred until completely dissolved. Then, 1 g of   $  Ti_{3}AlC_{2}  $   powder (  $  Ti_{3}AlC_{2}  $   was synthesized according to previous reports  $  ^{[28]}  $  ) was added to the above LiF/HCl mixture, and an ice bath was used to absorb the excess heat generated in the etching process to avoid the overflow of the mixed solution due to the rising temperature. After stirring at 50 °C for 24 h, the excess LiF was washed with 1 M HCl solution and then with deionized water until the pH value of the upper part of the solution was close to 6. After

sonication in deionized water for 10 min, the mixture was centrifuged at 3000 rad/min for 10 min. The supernatant was collected, and the previously collected supernatant was freeze-dried to prepare MXene. The resulting solution was freeze-dried for 3 days and then collected.

Synthesis of   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $  .   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   was synthesized by using a previously reported method. 213 mg of CsBr (99.9%, Sigma-Aldrich), 225 mg of   $  BiBr_{3}  $   (99.99%, Sigma-Aldrich), and 94 mg of AgBr (99.9%, Alfa Aesar) were immersed into 25 mL of 47% hydrobromic acid (HBr, Alfa Aesar), stirring the solution at 120 °C to evaporate the solvent until red crystals crystallized out of the solution. The stirring was turn off, and the mixture was slowly cooled at a rate of 5 °C/h. Next, the mixture was allowed to stand overnight; then the precipitated red crystals were filtered and collected, then washed in ethanol. The solid product was dried at 60 °C under vacuum to obtain   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $   crystals.

Device Fabrication. In an ultrasonic bath, the pre-patterned fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO) glass substrates (15 Ω/sq, You Xuan Company) were washed for 20 min with deionized water, acetone, and ethanol in sequence and then placed in an oven at 100 °C to dry for 30 min. TiCl₄ (4.5 mL, 99.8%, Sigma-Aldrich) was slowly dropped into 200 mL of ice water to prepare a TiCl₄ solution. FTO was immersed in the TiCl₄ ice-water mixture for 60 min at 70 °C. The FTO was then rinsed with ultrapure water and ethanol and dried at 100 °C for 8 h. 40 mg of MXene was added to the TiCl₄ aqueous solution to prepare Ti₃C₂Tₓ@TiO₂ (0.2 wt %o) using the same process, and the MXene content was changed to prepare a series of Ti₃C₂Tₓ@TiO₂ substrates. 532 mg of Cs₂AgBiBr₆ powder was dissolved in dimethyl sulfoxide (1 mL, DMSO, 99.9%, Sigma-Aldrich) to prepare a 0.5 M Cs₂AgBiBr₆ precursor solution. 100 μL of Cs₂AgBiBr₆ precursor solution was spin-coated on the surface Ti₃C₂Tₓ@TiO₂ layer at 5000 rad/min for 1 min. The resulting film was annealed at 280 °C for 5 min to completely form a double perovskite phase. After the Cs₂AgBiBr₆ films were cooled to room temperature, a 0.074 M 2,2,7,7-tetrakis (N,N-di-p-methoxyphenylamine)-9,9-spirobifluorene (Spiro-OMeTAD, You Xuan Company) (in chlorobenzene) solution doped with 1.81 M Li-TFSi (in acetonitrile) and TBP was spin-coated on the absorber layer, rotating at 5000 rpm for 60 s. Finally, 10 nm MoO₃ thin film and 100 nm Ag electrode were deposited by the shadow mask method, respectively. The effective area of the solar cell is 0.09 cm².

Characterization. Scanning electron microscope (SEM) images of the samples were taken on a ZEISS Supra 55 (Germany), and transmission electron microscope (TEM) images were obtained with a JEM 2100 LaB6 (Japan). X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was obtained on the PHI5000 Versaprobe system (Japan) equipped with an Al Ka X-ray source. The binding energies were calibrated to the C 1s peak at  $ 284.8\mathrm{eV} $ . The work function of  $ \mathrm{Ti}_{3}\mathrm{C}_{2}\mathrm{T}_{x}@\mathrm{TiO}_{2} $  was measured by UV electron spectroscopy (UPS). The X-ray diffractometer (XRD) pattern was obtained by the Bruker D8 Advance Instrument (Germany), irradiated with monochromatic 4Cu Kα, and the wavelength was  $ 1.5406\mu $ . A PerkinElmer ultraviolet-visible (UV-vis) spectrophotometer was used to measure the ultraviolet-visible absorption spectrum. The time-resolved PL and steady-state photoluminescence (PL) were measured on the Horiba Science FluoroHub single-photon counting controller. Under simulated conditions, the current density-voltage  $ (J - V) $  curves were obtained on a Keithley semiconductor parameter analyzer.

Meanwhile, the high-resolution XPS spectrum of F element in Figure 2e shows that F element exists on or near the surface of   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   film. The F 1s spectrum is convoluted into 684.2 and 683.6 eV, which are corresponding to Ti-F and surface oxyfluoride,  $  ^{29}  $   respectively. The existence of oxyfluoride is related to the drying process of   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   film. During the heating and drying process of   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   film, the F on the MXene surface is decomposed into   $  TiO_{2}  $   surface or near surface via heating treatment. In addition, the ion radius (0.133 nm) of fluorine is close to that of oxygen (0.132 nm) and has stronger electronegativity, which makes a F atom relatively easy to replace the O atom and form a Ti-F bond.

The energy level and work function of   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   was also investigated, and the ultraviolet photoelectron spectroscopy (UPS) was conducted on the   $  TiO_{2}  $   and   $  Ti_{3}C_{2}T_{x}@TiO_{2}  $   film spin-coated onto FTO substrates (Figure 3a–d). Therefore, the surface functional groups of MXene can modify the electronic structure of the   $  TiO_{2}  $   surface. To further understand the improved photocurrent hysteresis, the electron only devices with a structure of FTO/Ti $ _3 $ C $ _2 $ Ti $ _x $ @TiO $ _2 $ /Cs $ _2 $ AgBiBr $ _6 $ /PCBM/Ag were fabricated. The electron trap state density of the perovskite absorber was determined from the dark  $ I-V $  curves of the electron only devices based on pristine and doped TiO $ _2 $  ETLs presented in Figure S10. The linear portion of the curve at lower bias voltage shows ohmic contact. A further increase in bias voltage results in a kink point, followed by a nonlinear increase in current. The kink point indicates completely filled trap states. The trap state density can be computed from the trap filled limited voltage ( $ V_{\text{TFL}} $ ) based on eq 2

$$
V _ {\mathrm{TFL}} = \frac {e n _ {t} L ^ {2}}{2 \varepsilon \varepsilon_ {0}}
$$

(2)

where e is the charge on the electron,   $  n_{t}  $   is the trap state density, L is the perovskite film thickness,   $  \varepsilon  $   is the relative

dielectric constant (for   $  Cs_{2}AgBiBr_{6}  $  ),  $  ^{36}  $   and   $  \varepsilon_{0}  $   is the vacuum permittivity. # CONCLUSIONS

In summary, a novel and multifunctional ETL (Ti $ _3 $ C $ _2 $ T $ _x $ @ TiO $ _2 $ ) was demonstrated to improve the efficiency and stability of Cs $ _2 $ AgBiBr $ _6 $  PSCs, and the Ti $ _3 $ C $ _2 $ T $ _x $ @TiO $ _2 $  is synthesized via slowly dropping single-layered MXene nanosheets into TiCl $ _4 $  solution during the deposition of dense titania. The single-layered MXene nanosheets can not only improve the electron extraction rate and electrical conductivity of TiO $ _2 $  but also modify the surface wettability of ETL and promote the crystallization of the Cs $ _2 $ AgBiBr $ _6 $  double perovskite structure. Therefore, the device efficiency was improved significantly from 2.0% of the original TiO $ _2 $  to 2.81% of the Ti $ _3 $ C $ _2 $ T $ _x $ @TiO $ _2 $ , and the hysteresis was also greatly suppressed. In addition, the device based on Ti $ _3 $ C $ _2 $ T $ _x $ @TiO $ _2 $  exhibited the high long-term operating stability. Compared with the device based on TiO $ _2 $ ,
