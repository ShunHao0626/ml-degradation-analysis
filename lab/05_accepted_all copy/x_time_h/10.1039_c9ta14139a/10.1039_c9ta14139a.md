# Abstract

Low photovoltaic performance of 2D perovskites which is caused by the high exciton binding energy and wide band gaps limited their further development and applications. Here, we adopted a new-type strategy to obtain high-quality 2-hydroxyethylamine (HEA) based quasi-2D perovskites films by incorporating halogenated second spacer cation. Due to the effective passivation of halogenated benzylammonium cations with enhanced electronegativity, the mixed quasi-2D perovskites displayed superior crystallinity and reduced internal defects, leading to enhanced charge transfer and suppressive carrier recombination. As a result, significantly improved power conversion efficiency (PCE) from  $ 15.19\% $  to  $ 18.75\% $  with enhanced open-circuit voltage  $ (V_{\mathrm{oc}}) $  and fill factor (FF) was achieved. After aging at  $ 85^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $  for  $ 200\mathrm{h} $  and  $ 45\pm 5\% $  relative humidity (RH) for  $ 1500\mathrm{h} $ , the unencapsulated optimal devices can retain  $ 81\% $  and  $ 90\% $  of

# Introduction

Organic-inorganic metal halide perovskites (such as   $  MAPbX_{3}  $  ), which displaying excellent optical and electrical properties, have achieved huge success in the field of photoelectric device and the certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) has exceed 25%.  $  ^{1-5}  $   Although the PCE of three-dimensional (3D) perovskite solar cells (PSCs) is rapidly improving, the instability under humidity, oxygen, heat and light is still a challenge which has hindered their widespread use in practical devices.  $  ^{6-11}  $

In recent years, researchers have made a lot of work to solve the instability problem of PSCs.  $  ^{12-16}  $   Particularly, two-dimensional (2D) perovskites have been widely studied due to its dramatically superior stability in contrast to 3D perovskites and become promising photoelectric absorption materials.  $  ^{17-25}  $   Up to now, 2D perovskites based on different organic ammonium salts have been successively studied, such as:   $  (\mathrm{BA})_{2}(\mathrm{MA})_{2}\mathrm{Pb}_{3}\mathrm{I}_{10}  $   Ruddlesden-Popper (R-P) 2D perovskite,  $  ^{17}  $     $  (\mathrm{PhC}_{2}\mathrm{H}_{5}\mathrm{NH}_{3})_{2}(\mathrm{CH}_{3}\mathrm{NH}_{3})_{2}\mathrm{Pb}_{3}\mathrm{I}_{10}  $   2D layered perovskite,  $  ^{18}  $     $  (\mathrm{IC}_{2}\mathrm{H}_{4}\mathrm{NH}_{3})_{2}(\mathrm{CH}_{3}\mathrm{NH}_{3})_{n-1}\mathrm{Pb}_{n}\mathrm{I}_{3n+1}  $   mixed-dimensional (MD) perovskite,  $  ^{19}  $   TEA-based 2D/3D perovskite and (PDMA)FA  $  _{2}  $  Pb  $  _{3}  $  I  $  _{10}  $   Dion-Jacobson (D-J) 2D perovskite.  $  ^{20,21}  $   Despite these 2D perovskites have displayed high stability, they presented poor photovoltaic performances due to their poor crystallinity, low charge transport and high trap density. Hence, it remains a great challenge to improve the photovoltaic performance of 2D perovskite devices for further commercial application.

At present, many approaches have been applied to enhance the PCE of 2D PSCs, including additive assistant, hot-casting and solvent engineering. $ ^{17,26-30} $  Li et al. obtained high-performance layered RP-based (BA) $ _{2} $ (MA) $ _{3} $ Pb $ _{4} $ I $ _{13} $  PSCs with a PCE of 14.9% by doping Li $ ^{+} $ . $ ^{31} $  Via synergistic exploitation of NH $ _{4} $ Cl and DMSO, Liang and his co-workers prepared the highly efficient R-P 2D perovskite (PEA) $ _{2} $ MA $ _{3} $ Pb $ _{4} $ I $ _{13} $  with significant PCE up to 13.41%. $ ^{32} $  Liu's group demonstrated a R-P/3D heterostructure PSCs combined with molecule passivation delivering a PCE as high as 20.62%. $ ^{33} $  However, most of these methods are devoted to improve the PCE of butylammonium BA or phenylethylammonium PEA based 2D PSCs, thus, it is still necessary to explore new strategies to achieve high-efficiency 2D perovskite devices based on other potential ammonium salts.

Herein, we employed a new strategy to improve the PCE of low-efficiency 2-hydroxyethylamine (HEA) based quasi-2D perovskite materials (n=9) by incorporating halogenated 4-fluorobenzylammonium (FBA) and 4-chlorobenzylammonium (CBA) as second spacer cation. The superior crystal orientation, smoother surface, remarkably prolonged carrier lifetime and lower trap density for  $ (\mathrm{HEA}_{0.9}\mathrm{CBA}_{0.1})_2(\mathrm{Cs}_{0.1}\mathrm{FA}_{0.9})_8\mathrm{Pb}_9(\mathrm{I}_{0.95}\mathrm{Br}_{0.05})_{28} $  (0.1CBA) and  $ (\mathrm{HEA}_{0.9}\mathrm{FBA}_{0.1})_2(\mathrm{Cs}_{0.1}\mathrm{FA}_{0.9})_8\mathrm{Pb}_9(\mathrm{I}_{0.95}\mathrm{Br}_{0.05})_{28} $  (0.1FBA) perovskite films indicate the excellent passivation effects of the halogenated benzylammonium-based aromatic cations. The results reveal that the 0.1CBA based quasi-2D PSCs achieve a champion PCE of  $ 18.75\% $ . More importantly, the humidity stability of 0.1CBA and 0.1FBA perovskite devices are prominently enhanced, which can retain  $ 90\% $  of initial values after  $ 1500\mathrm{h} $  for aging under  $ 45 \pm 5\% $  RH. This work demonstrated that the halogenated benzylammonium-based aromatic cation can play a unique alternative cation to passivate the defects and the strategy can generally applicable for other quasi-2D perovskite optoelectronic devices.

# Results and Discussion

In this study, we employed the component of   $  (\mathrm{HEA})_{2}(\mathrm{Cs}_{0.1}\mathrm{FA}_{0.9})_{8}\mathrm{Pb}_{9}(\mathrm{I}_{0.95}\mathrm{Br}_{0.05})_{28}  $   (denoted as HEA, 2-hydroxyethylamine) quasi-2D perovskite system as an absorber layer. The 2D perovskite structure of   $  (\mathrm{HEA})_{2}\mathrm{PbI}_{4}  $   has been in the literature and our previous work.  $  ^{34,35}  $   Then, as the second spacer cations, the halogenated benzylammonium-based aromatic cations 4-fluorobenzylammonium (FBA) and 4-chlorobenzylammonium (CBA) were respectively used to improve the performances of quasi-2D perovskite solar cells (PSCs) based on the general formula of   $  [(\mathrm{HEA})_{1-x}(\mathrm{A})_{x}]_{2}(\mathrm{Cs}_{0.1}\mathrm{FA}_{0.9})_{8}\mathrm{Pb}_{9}(\mathrm{I}_{0.95}\mathrm{Br}_{0.05})_{28}  $  . When x is 0.1, the component is labeled as 0.1A (A=CBA or FBA).

We carried out X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns to study the changes in crystal structure of quasi-2D perovskites. Fig. 1a shows the XRD patterns of HEA, 0.1CBA and 0.1FBA perovskite films. The XRD patterns of all the perovskite films exhibit the characteristic peaks of 3D perovskite, corresponding to (110), (202) (220), (310) crystal planes. In addition, new diffraction peaks arise at about  $ 8.9^{\circ} $ , implying the formation of 2D perovskite phase.[20-22] The HEA perovskite film displays a strong characteristic peak of  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  near  $ 12.7^{\circ} $ , declaring the existence of unreacted  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  in HEA film, which will reduce its light absorption. However, after incorporating the second spacer cation to partially replace  $ \mathrm{HEA}^+ $ , the intensities of 3D perovskite characteristic peaks significantly increase and the intensities of characteristic peak for  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  dramatically decrease. From the magnified XRD patterns of HEA, 0.1CBA and 0.1FBA perovskite films in Fig. 1b and c, it can be observed that the peaks of 0.1CBA and 0.1FBA perovskite films display obvious shift to large angles, owing to the larger ionic sizes of CBA and FBA lead to slight changes in the lattice constants of perovskite crystal. # Conclusion

In summary, we reported highly efficient and stable quasi-2D perovskite devices by introducing halogenated 4-fluorobenzylammonium (FBA) and 4-chlorobenzylammonium (CBA) as second spacer cation into the  $ (\mathrm{HEA})_2(\mathrm{Cs}_{0.1}\mathrm{FA}_{0.9})_8\mathrm{Pb}_9(\mathrm{I}_{0.95}\mathrm{Br}_{0.05})_{28} $  perovskite. As a result, owing to the passivation effects of CBA and FBA, the quasi-2D perovskites showed excellent properties with better crystallinity, smoother surface and higher light absorption ability, leading to effectively reduced trap densities and increased charge transfer. The mixed quasi-2D perovskite devices displayed improved PCE and environmental stability. In particular, the 0.1CBA quasi-2D perovskite device exhibited the highest PCE of  $ 18.75\% $  and maintained  $ 90\% $  of initial values after  $ 1500\mathrm{h} $  for aging under  $ 45\pm 5\% $  RH. Our work provides an effective and meaningful method to further improve the PCE and stability of quasi-2D perovskite materials.
