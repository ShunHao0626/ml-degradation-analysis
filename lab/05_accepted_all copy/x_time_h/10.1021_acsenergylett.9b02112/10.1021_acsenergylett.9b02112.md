# Abstract

Hole transport layer (HTL) plays a crucial role in the efficiency and stability of perovskite solar cells (PSCs). The most efficient PSCs based on spiro-OMeTAD (Spiro) generally have stability problems. Here,   $  NiO_{x}/Spiro  $   HTL has been designed and implemented by combining the advantages of these two films. The results indicated that device based on   $  NiO_{x}/Spiro  $   HTL has faster hole extraction ability and better energy alignment than that of pure Spiro device, thus improving the PCE from 19.4% to 21.66%. Compared with the 60% initial efficiency of Spiro-based device, the   $  NiO_{x}/Spiro  $   bilayer devices have higher stability and maintain 90% initial efficiency over 1200 hours. In this work,   $  NiO_{x}  $   is applied to perovskite devices with N-I-P configuration, which provides a possible mitigation strategy to reduce   $  V_{OC}  $   deficit for efficient and stable devices.

Organic-inorganic hybrid lead halide perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have attracted extensive research due to their unique properties, such as large absorption coefficient, high charge carrier mobility, long carrier lifetime etc.  $  ^{1-6}  $   In less than ten years, and the power conversion efficiency (PCE) of PSCs has been greatly improved from 3.8%  $  ^{7}  $   to 25.2%.  $  ^{8}  $   So far, many efficient PSCs have adopted regular structure (N-I-P), in which organic hole transport layer (HTL) could be 2,2',7,7'-tetrakis(N,N-dimethoxyphenylamine)-9,9'-spirobifluorene (Spiro), poly (triarylamine) (PTAA), and poly(3-hexylthiophene)(P3HT).  $  ^{9-11}  $   In order to improve the mobility of charger carrier, tBP and Li-TFSI are essential in Spiro HTL. However, due to interdiffusion at the interface, Li-TFSI is easy to absorb moisture and then cause instability of devices. The hygroscopicity and instability of these organic HTL materials are not benefit to improve the stability of PSCs, which seriously hinders the development of PSCs.

In recent years, low-cost inorganic materials such as   $  NiO_{x}  $  ,   $  CuGaO_{2}  $  ,   $  CuCrO_{2}  $  , and CuSCN have been used to improve device stabilities.  $  ^{12-20}  $   Chen et al. reported an inverted PSCs with NiOx layer without annealing, which demonstrated a PCE of 20.9%.  $  ^{13}  $   Zhang et al. designed an inorganic HTLs (  $  CuCrO_{2}  $  ) for inverted PSCs resulting in a PCE of 19.0%.  $  ^{21}  $   Yang et al. demonstrated the planar P-I-N PSCs based on the   $  CuCrO_{2}  $   with PCE of 20.54%.  $  ^{22}  $   Mali et al. reported that   $  NiO_{x}/CuSCN  $   double HTL

The structure of planar PSCs in this study is ITO/SnO $ _2 $ /(FAPbI $ _3 $ ) $ _x $ (FAPbBr $ _3 $ ) $ _{1-x} $ /NiO $ _x $ /Spiro/Au, as shown in Figure 2(a). Two-step spin coating method was used to deposit perovskite layer. First, PbI $ _2 $  layer was coated on the ITO/SnO $ _2 $  substrate, then FAI (organic salts formamidinium iodide) mixed with MABr (methylammonium bromine) and MACl (methylammonium chloride) were spin-coated on PbI $ _2 $  layer. Finally, the perovskite phase was formed by annealing. NiO $ _x $  was then spin coated on the top of the perovskite layer and annealed at 100°C for 10min. The preparation of NiO $ _x $  micellar solution has an essential relationship with the amount of OA. When the amount of OA in chlorobenzene is less than the Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC), the OA could not aggregate into nanometer micelle, and will not be able to be dispersed NiO $ _x $  into non-polar solvent chlorobenzene. When the concentration of OA reached the CMC, OA spontaneously aggregated into micelles in chlorobenzene. NiO $ _x $  can be encapsulated in micellar cavity to disperse nanomicelles NiO $ _x $ . In summary,   $  NiO_{x}  $   nano-micelle solution has been successfully prepared and applied to N-I-P planar perovskite solar cells. The results show that the addition of   $  NiO_{x}  $   layer improves the extraction ability of hole carriers. As a result, the   $  V_{OC}  $   of devices is improved, and the champion efficiency of the solar cells reaches 21.66%. Compared with pure Spiro device, the stability of   $  NiO_{x}  $   devices is increased by four times. These results indicate that the solution treatment of   $  NiO_{x}  $   is expected to further improve the stability and efficiency of perovskite solar cells.
