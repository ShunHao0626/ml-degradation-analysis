# Multifunctional CuSCN interlayer in carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic perovskite solar cells for boosting the efficiency and stability

Jieqiong Liu, Miao Lei, Wei Zhang, Guiqiang Wang*

School of New Energy, Bohai University, 121007 Jinzhou, China

* Corresponding author, email: wgqiang@bhu.edu.cn

Abstract: Carbon electrode-based all-inorganic perovskite solar cells (PSCs) without hole-transport materials are attracting extensive interest due to their low cost, simple fabrication process, and high stability. Nevertheless, the conversion efficiency of carbon electrode-based PSCs is far from satisfactory owing to serious charge recombination at the inorganic perovskite/carbon interface, which mainly derives from the mismatched energy level between inorganic perovskite film and carbon layer. Herein, a hydrophobic CuSCN film is introduced into carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs as a multifunctional interlayer between  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite film and carbon electrode to form a favorable interfacial energy level alignment and protect  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite from ambient moisture. It is found that introducing CuSCN interlayer can not only enhance the hole extraction and suppress the charge recombination in carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs, but also improve the stability of the cell. Moreover, the very strong interaction between  $ \mathrm{SCN^{-1}} $  and  $ \mathrm{Pb^{2+}} $  remarkably reduce the surface defects of  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite film. Consequently, the device with CuSCN interlayer displays an improved power conversion efficiency of  $ 7.30\% $  in comparison with  $ 5.19\% $  for the device without CuSCN interlayer as well as an excellent long-term stability under the ambient

# Introduction

Organic-inorganic hybrid perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have attracted an ever-increasing research interest and considered as one of the most promising candidate for low-cost, new-generation photovoltaic technology due to their advantage of booming efficiency and simple solution-processed technique   $  [1-4]  $  . However, the intrinsic instability of organic-inorganic hybrid perovskite materials under ambient stresses severely impedes the large-scale commercial application of hybrid PSCs   $  [5]  $  . In addition, the state-of-the-art hybrid PSCs usually utilize the expensive organic hole-transport materials and noble metal back electrode to achieve fast hole extraction and transport. This inevitably impose a serious financial worry on the practical application of hybrid PSCs. Therefore, it is highly desirable to develop highly stable PSCs using low-cost materials and facile fabrication process. This urgent requirement promotes the development of carbon electrode-based all-inorganic PSCs owing to their high cost-effectiveness, simple fabrication process, and excellent stability   $  [6-12]  $  .

Among all-inorganic perovskite materials being suitable for solar cell application, cubic phase   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   perovskite with a bandgap of 1.73 eV is considered as the best choice for PSCs in the point of view of the light harvest [13-16]. Unfortunately, cubic phase   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   perovskite is unstable and easily transform into non-perovskite phase with poor photovoltaic properties under the ambient condition. By contrast,   $  CsPbBr_{3}  $   perovskite displays a superior phase stability, but it has a wide bandgap of 2.3 eV,

which certainly confines the light absorption edge and limits the photocurrent density of the corresponding device [17-20].  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $ , a mixed halide perovskite, shows a narrower bandgap than  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $  and a higher stability than  $ \mathrm{CsPbI_3} $ . Thus,  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  becomes a good choice for all-inorganic PSCs in view of its well-balanced bandgap and phase stability features [6, 21-24]. In recent years, carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs with a architecture of  $ \mathrm{FTO / TiO_2 / CsPbIBr_2 / carbon} $  have been attracting wide-spread attention [25-28]. In 2018, Zhu et al. reported a aged perovskite-precursor-solution strategy to fabricate carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs and achieved a conversion efficiency of  $ 6.55\% $  [25]. Zhang et al. demonstrated a light-processing method to prepare high-quality  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite film [28]. The assembled carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs displayed a power conversion efficiency of  $ 8.60\% $  with a high open-circuit voltage of 1.283V. Recently, Zhu et al improved a power conversion efficiency of carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSC over  $ 9\% $  through an intermolecular exchange strategy [27].

In the carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs, carbon electrode functions as both the hole-transport material (HTM) and the back electrode, and thus the holes are directly extracted by carbon layer from perovskite film. Unfortunately, the energy level mismatch at the  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite  $ (\mathrm{E_{VB}} = -5.72\mathrm{eV}) / \mathrm{carbon} $   $ (\mathrm{W_F} = -5.0\mathrm{eV}) $  interface greatly limits the charge separation and hole extraction. In addition, the direct contact of  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite film with carbon layer usually lead to the energetically favorable electron transfer from perovskite film to carbon layer.

All these result in serious charge recombination and largely limit the photovoltaic performance of carbon electrode-based  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  all-inorganic PSCs. To Insert an inorganic interlayer with a suitable energy level matching both the valence band of perovskite and the work function of carbon at the perovskite/carbon interface is a workable strategy to address above problems without compromising device stability [29,30].

CuSCN is an inorganic p-type semiconductor material with appealing properties of high hole mobility and excellent stability and has been investigated as the HTM for organic-inorganic hybrid PSCs [31-36]. CuSCN possesses a wide bandgap with a valence band of -5.3 eV, which remarkably match the energy level of   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   perovskite and carbon. Moreover, CuSCN thin film can be obtained via a solution-processed route at low temperature. These favorable features make CuSCN ideal for interfacial interlayer application in carbon electrode-based   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   all-inorganic PSCs. Herein, CuSCN interlayer prepared by a solution-processed method was inserted at the   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   perovskite/carbon interface for fabricating high-efficiency carbon electrode-based   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   all-inorganic PSCs. The presence of CuSCN interlayer forms a favorable energy level alignment for enhancing hole extraction and blocking electron transfer at the   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   perovskite/carbon interface, which remarkably suppress the charge recombination in carbon-based   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   PSCs. Moreover, introducing CuSCN interlayer could also protect   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   perovskite from the ambient moisture and enhance the stability of carbon electrode-based   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   all-inorganic PSCs due to the hydrophobicity of CuSCN

Although rarely being discussed in detail, carbon electrode-based inorganic PSCs usually exhibit obvious hysteresis effect in the J-V plot due to the unbalanced charge extraction at the electron-transport layer/perovskite interface and the perovskite/carbon interface, which derives from the sluggish hole extraction at the perovskite/carbon interface [25,42]. # Conclusions

In summary, the hydrophobic CuSCN interlayer has been utilized as the multifunctional interlayer between   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   perovskite film and carbon electrode for boosting the efficiency and stability of carbon electrode-based   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   all-inorganic PSCs. The introduction of CuSCN interlayer between   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   preovskite film and carbon electrode promotes the hole extraction from perovskite, suppresses charge recombination, and protects   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   perovskite film from ambient moisture simultaneously. Consequently, the carbon electrode-based   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   all-inorganic PSC with CuSCN interlayer achieves a gratifying PCE of 7.30% as well as a superior long-term stability at ambient condition. This work provides a facile and promising strategy to improve the efficiency and stability of carbon electrode-based all-inorganic PSCs.

FTO substrates were cleaned by sequential sonication treatments in deionized water, acetone, and isopropanol according to the procedures described in our previous works   $  [18]  $  . A compact   $  TiO_{2}  $   layers were deposited on pre-cleaned FTO substrate by spin coating 0.15 M ethanol solution of titanium diisopropoxide bis (acetylacetonate) at 4500 rpm for 45 s and sintered at 450 °C for 30 min. Then, a mesoporous   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer was deposited onto the compact   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer by spin coating commercial   $  TiO_{2}  $   paste (Dyesol18NR-T) diluted in ethanol with weight ratio 15:1 at 4000 rpm for 30 s and subsequently heat-treated at 450 °C for 30 min.

Measurements and characterization.

Scanning electron microscopy (SEM, Hitachi S-4800) was used to characterize the morphology characteristics of the samples. X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns were recorded by a Rigaku Smartlab X-ray diffractometer with Cu Kα radiation. The absorption spectra were measured by a UV–Vis spectrophotometer (UV-3000). The steady-state photoluminescence (PL) measurements were performed on an F900 fluorescence spectrophotometer with an excitation wavelength of 400 nm. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) measurements were carried out on a Solartron 1287/1255 system in the frequency range from   $  10^{6}  $   to 1 Hz under open circuit. The current density-voltage (J-V) curves of cells were measured using a Keithley 2400 source meter under standard AM 1.5 illumination. The external quantum efficiency (EQE) spectrum was measured by employing a 150 W xenon lamp (Oriel) equipped with a monochromator as monochromatic light source. All the fabrication and measurement processes were carried out in the ambient condition.
