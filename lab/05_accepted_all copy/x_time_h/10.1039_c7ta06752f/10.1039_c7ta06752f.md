# Efficient and stable perovskite solar cells based on high-quality  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_{3 - x}\mathrm{Cl}_x $  films modified by  $ \mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O}_x $  additives

Tingwei He   $  ^{a,b}  $  , Zhiyong Liu   $  ^{a,b*}  $  , Kaikai Liu   $  ^{a}  $  , Jiaxiang Wang   $  ^{a}  $  , Yawei Zhou   $  ^{a}  $  , Jien Yang   $  ^{a,b}  $  , Hairui Liu   $  ^{a,b}  $  , Yurong Jiang   $  ^{a,b}  $  , Heng Ma   $  ^{a,b}  $  , Mingjian Yuan   $  ^{c}  $

a Department of Physics and Materials Science, Henan Normal University, Xinxiang 453007, China

b Henan Key Laboratory of Photovoltaic Materials, Xinxiang 453007, China  
c Department of chemistry, Nankai university, Tianjin 300071, China

# Abstract

The method of preparing perovskite films in ambient air is of great importance to commercialize high-performance planar perovskite solar cells (PSCs). Herein, an efficient and stable inverted planar PSC has been prepared by a simple solution method in ambient air. Suitable vanadium oxide  $ (\mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x}) $  aqueous solution is mixed with  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_{3 - x}\mathrm{Cl}_x $  precursor solution, which results in improvement in  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_{3 - x}\mathrm{Cl}_x $  film. Perovskite films with large crystal sizes could be obtained through  $ \mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x} $  additives. The improvement in the performance of perovskite film is conducive to the separation and transmission of charge carriers. The highest power conversion efficiency (PCE) of  $ 16.14\% $  of PSCs has been obtained by controlling the proportion of  $ \mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x} $  additives in ambient air. It is physics mechanism that  $ \mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x} $  particles are dispersed in the perovskite crystal boundary, and the heterogeneous structure between  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_{3 - x}\mathrm{Cl}_x $  and  $ \mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x} $  is formed. In addition, the slow migration of iodide ions and the stable perovskite films are also achieved. What merits more attention is that the PSCs with  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_x\mathrm{Cl}_{3 - x}:\mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x} $  (2.5 wt%) still maintains a higher PCE after being stored for  $ 1000\mathrm{h} $ , more than  $ 70\% $  of the initial one.

Keywords: Perovskite solar cells; Ambient air;  $ \mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O_x} $  additives; Heterogeneous structure; High stability

# 1. Introduction

It has been found that organic-inorganic hybrid perovskite materials have excellent semiconductor physical properties. For example,   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   with a forbidden band width of 1.55 eV and good absorption in the full range of visible light is the first material widely used to prepare PSCs.  $  ^{1,2}  $   The exciton binding energy of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   is less than 50 meV, indicating that the exciton of the material can be easily converted to free carriers at room temperature.  $  ^{3-5}  $   In addition, the mobilities of electron and hole of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   are   $  24 \pm 7 cm^{2}V^{-1}S^{-1}  $   and   $  105 \pm 35 cm^{2}V^{-1}S^{-1}  $   respectively, and diffusion length of charge carriers reaches 100-1000 nm.  $  ^{6,7}  $   Another hybrid perovskite material is   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $ , and its diffusion length of electrons and holes reaches 1   $  \mu m  $ .  $  ^{8}  $   High-efficient PSCs has been prepared by using this hybrid perovskite material.  $  ^{9,10}  $   Since 2009, the organic-inorganic hybrid perovskite, as a light-absorbing material, has been used to prepare solar cells.  $  ^{11}  $   An alarmingly rapid increase in the power conversion efficiency (PCE) of perovskite solar cell (PSC) has aroused widespread concern.  $  ^{12-16}  $   The efficiency of 22.1% of the PSC device had been achieved in 2016,  $  ^{17}  $   which was considered as one of the most efficient devices. The efficiency and stability are the main performances of PSC devices, however, some devices with better stability do not exhibit a high efficiency.  $  ^{18-20}  $   We still have a long way to go to prepare high-efficiency and simple-preparation PSC devices. For example, some researchers embark on the fabrication of PSCs under ambient air.  $  ^{21,22}  $

The crystal structure of the perovskite material is particularly susceptible to other factors, such as water, oxygen, ultraviolet light, temperature and external conditions. $ ^{23,24} $  The impact of ambient air on perovskite materials and devices has been one of the focuses of the studies. What's more, some reports suggest that the water also plays an important role in improving performance of PSCs. $ ^{25,26} $  The method of producing high-performance devices in the air environment is critical to the commercialization of PSCs. In addition, it has amply demonstrated that the grain size of the perovskite film is more important than its crystallinity for the performance of inverted planar PSCs. $ ^{27} $  The large perovskite grains have been obtained by using some

It has been widely believed that the water additives can increase the crystallinity of perovskite films and improve photoelectric performance of PSC devices. On this basis, the   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   aqueous solution treated by hydrogen peroxide is added to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor.  $  ^{[28,29]}  $   The vanadium (IV) oxide has a high capacity and excellent cyclability.  $  ^{[30]}  $   Although   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives, as the interface modification layer, is a good hole extraction material, it has not been used to improve the quality of the perovskite film with   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $  . Here, we have introduced a method that the efficient and stable PSCs are prepared by adding   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives under ambient air. By controlling the ratio of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives, high-quality and large-size perovskite grains can be easily obtained, improving effectively the photoelectric properties of the PSC devices. As a result, the PSCs with   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   (2.5 wt%) show an enhanced PCE of 16.14%, with an open circuit voltage (  $  V_{OC}  $  ) of 0.98 V, a short circuit current density (  $  J_{SC}  $  ) of 21.62 mA cm  $  ^{-2}  $  , and a fill factor (FF) of 75.1%. In comparison, the PCE of device with   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   (0 wt%) is only 14.02% under the same preparation conditions, with a   $  V_{OC}  $   of 0.95 V, a   $  J_{SC}  $   of 20.17 mA cm  $  ^{-2}  $  , and a FF of 73.2%. What's more, the long-term stability of the devices with   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives has also been effectively improved. Under the storage condition of 1 sun light illumination and 35%–40% of humidity, the PCE of the devices has been maintained within a certain range (more than 70% of the initial value) in the ambient air after 1000 h.

# 2.2 Fabrication of the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution

$  V_{2}O_{x}  $  aqueous solutions with different concentrations had been prepared according to previously reported methods.  $  ^{[28]}  $  First,  $  V_{2}O_{5}  $  aqueous solution was manufactured by dissolving  $  V_{2}O_{5}  $  powder into deionized water, with varying proportions (0, 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 and 4.5 wt%) and churning the mixture at room temperature for 30 min. Then,  $  H_{2}O_{2}  $  aqueous solution (30%) was added to  $  V_{2}O_{5}  $  aqueous solution, and the mixture was stirred for 10 min and let set for 10 h or more. Thus,  $  V_{2}O_{x}  $  aqueous solutions with different mass fractions were prepared. Mixed DMF solvent (the volume ratio of DMF to  $  V_{2}O_{x}  $  aqueous solution is 1000: 15) was prepared via adding different  $  V_{2}O_{x}  $  aqueous solution into anhydrous DMF.  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $  precursor solution was fabricated by solubilizing  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $  and  $  PbCl_{2}  $  in aqueous DMF solvent with a molar ratio of 3:1,  $  ^{[32,33]}  $  and agitating the compound at 60 °C for 12 h in ambient air. The solutions were filtered with a 0.45 μm Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) filter before device fabrication.

# 3. Results and discussion

Previous reports have confirmed that suitable water can improve the crystal quality of perovskite films in glove box or air environment. The dense and uniform perovskite films could be obtained by adding suitable   $  H_{2}O  $   in ambient air.  $  ^{[25,38]}  $   In order to eliminate the interference of water, we dope the same amount of water into the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}  $   precursor solution, and regard the device with 1.5%   $  H_{2}O  $   as reference device. As shown in Fig. 1, PSCs with   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   composite films were prepared by adding   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives in ambient environment with   $  \sim40\%  $   relative humidity. On the basis of conventional inverted PSCs, we added   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   aqueous solution to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution, fabricating the efficient and stable PSCs with the structure of ITO/PEDOT:PSS (40 nm)/   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   (300 nm)/PCBM (40 nm)/Bphen (10 nm)/Ag (100 nm). The cross-section FESEM image of PSC prepared in ambient air (  $  \sim40\%  $   relative humidity) has been shown in Fig. S1.

# 3.2 The role of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives

The   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   composite films with different concentration of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   display different surface morphologies. Fig. 4a-f shows the surface FESEM images of the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   films with different concentration of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   solutions annealed in ambient air. As we know, the long-strip perovskite films (0 wt%   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $  ) (Fig. 4a) are easily formed in air atmosphere. The long strip of perovskite crystals are closely connected to each other and form an interlaced network structure, resulting in more holes in the perovskite film. When 0.5 wt% of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   solutions is added to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution, interestingly and remarkably, a large grain with the size of 300-600nm is found in some areas of perovskite films. Furthermore, with the increase of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   concentration, the grains become larger. When 4.5 wt% of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   solution is added to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution, a large grain with a size of 2 – 4 μm has been displayed in perovskite films (Fig. 4f). This suggests that   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives can contribute to obtaining large-sized perovskite grains and play a crucial role in the crystal growth. Perovskite films with large crystal sizes can be obtained by controlling the nucleation sites and crystal growth processes.  $  ^{50}  $   The perovskite with larger grains could reduce the trap charge density due to smaller total grain boundary areas.  $  ^{51}  $   However, the gap between grains gets larger with the increase in the size of grain, which might result in shorted cells.  $  ^{27}  $   The dense perovskite film can effectively suppress the diffusion of oxygen and moisture, which would contribute to improving the stability of perovskite films in ambient conditions.  $  ^{52}  $   Therefore, large grain and dense film have their own advantages, and the performance of the device with large grain and dense film is the best.

There is no doubt that   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives promote the growth of perovskite crystals.

In the annealing proceeding of perovskite film with   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives, the boundaries of the grains will be migrated, and some small grains will grow and infiltrate into each other,  $  ^{56}  $   making grain larger. In short, the reason why   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives could make perovskite grains larger remains to be further studied.

# 3.3 Stability of devices

One of the biggest challenges faced by researchers is to maintain the stability of PSC devices during the period from experiment to application.  $  ^{58}  $   The highest-efficiency-inverted PSC devices usually show a poor stability.  $  ^{59}  $   To analyze the effect of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives on the stability of devices, degradation tests of the unencapsulated PSCs that are stored for 360 h under 1 sun light illumination have been conducted. And the identical and stable atmosphere conditions (the temperature in the range of 25-30°C, and the humidity in the range of 35%-40%) are controlled. The stabilities of normalized   $  V_{OC}  $  ,   $  J_{SC}  $  , FF and PCE values, as a function of time, are shown in Fig. 7a-d. # 4. Conclusion

In summary, we have systemically analyzed the effects of   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives on inverted planar heterojunction PSCs prepared in ambient air. The   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives that exist in   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution contribute significantly to the augment in the size of perovskite crystals. The high-quality   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   (2.5 wt%) film could effectively improve the optoelectronic performance of PSC devices. The devices obtain PCE of 16.14%, 15.3% higher than that of the device without   $  V_{2}O_{x}  $   additives. In addition, after being stored in the air for 1000 h, devices with   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{x}Cl_{3-x}:V_{2}O_{x}  $   (2.5 wt%) still remains a high efficiency, more than 70% of the initial value. It has been universally acknowledged that the stability of the devices prepared in ambient air could exert a profound influence on the commercialization of PSCs.
