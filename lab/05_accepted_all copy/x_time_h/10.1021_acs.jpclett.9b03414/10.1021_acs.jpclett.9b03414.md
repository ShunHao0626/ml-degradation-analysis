# Abstract

Insertion of organic spacers into halide perovskite slabs have offered a trade-off between efficiency and stability of perovskite solar cells (PSCs). The layered structure of diammonium-intercalated cesium lead halide perovskites is virtually unexplored, in contrast to several works on monoammonium system. In this report, we find that perovskite with 1,4-butanediammonium (BDA) and cesium cations can only form n = 1 and n = 2 layered isologues defined by the chemical formula of (BDA)Cs $ _{n-} $  $ _{1} $ Pb $ _{n} $ (I $ _{0.7} $ Br $ _{0.3} $ ) $ _{3n+1} $ , while the n = 3 ~ 4 ones will self-constructed into unique heterostructures comprising separated quantum wells (QWs, n = 1 ~ 2) and 3D (n = ∞) perovskites. Low-dimensional (LD) metal halide perovskites possess wide tunability on optical bandgap and electric properties, regarding their structural versatility and internal-ligand flexibility.  $  ^{1-4}  $   Photoabsorbers based on LD perovskites have triggered a research prosperity in recent years, as the layered architecture constructed by inserted organic ligands can successfully overcome the structural instability of three-dimensional (3D) perovskites.  $  ^{5,6}  $   However, such ascendancy is earned at the expense of their optoelectronic performance, such as widened bandgap and increasing barriers for carrier transport. Accordingly, steric screening on ligands,  $  ^{7-9}  $   additive strategy,  $  ^{10}  $   and optimized processing  $  ^{4,11}  $   are raised to address these issues, which leads to high-efficiency photovoltaics ever approaching the 3D counterparts.

Currently, most designs of LD perovskite absorbers are focusing on methylammonium lead halide (MAPbX $ _3 $ , X = halide), while a critical problem is that MA cannot endure thermal stress in real operation. $ ^{12,13} $  As an alternative, cesium was used to fully substitute MA, of which the thermal and light stabilities are significantly enhanced. $ ^{14} $  To reduce the moisture sensitivity of cesium lead halide perovskites (CsPbX $ _3 $ ), ethylenediammonium

In summary, we have gained insights into the phase segregation induced heterostructures of diammonium-intercalated cesium lead halide perovskites. Atypically, the heterogeneous composition does not lead to terrible photovoltaic performance for low-member perovskites, yet improves their structural stability under stimulus. The band alignment among 2D/3D QWs allows initial charge extraction and transfer inside the film, which takes full advantage of wide-bandgap 2D phases. Though the efficiency remains to be improved, the findings in this work provide another solution for low-dimensional design on perovskite photovoltaics, that is, heterogeneity should not always be avoided.

# Synthesis of iodide salts

To obtain pure diamino iodide salts, BDA were added into the ethanol solution of HI in round-bottomed flask with a molar ratio of 1:2. The reactions were kept at 0 °C in nitrogen atmosphere for 2 h. Then the white precipitates were obtained by rotation evaporation at 50 °C for 1 h and washed with diethyl ether for three times. Finally, the precipitates were dried at 60 °C in vacuum oven overnight.
