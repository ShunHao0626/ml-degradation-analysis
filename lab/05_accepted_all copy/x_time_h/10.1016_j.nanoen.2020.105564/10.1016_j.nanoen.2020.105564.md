# Abstract

Interface engineering is of great signification for high-performance of organic-inorganic halide perovskite solar cells (PSCs). Herein, we report a dual interface modification strategy for constructing effective and stable PSCs by simultaneously incorporating the europium ions doped  $ \mathrm{WO}_{\mathrm{x}} $  nanorods (Eu- $ \mathrm{WO}_{\mathrm{x}} $ ) into electron transport layer (ETL) and hole transport layer (HTL). A significant hysteresis-free power conversion efficiency (PCE) with  $ 22.08\% $ , and much better UV light/long term-stability of PSCs are achieved as compared to  $ 19.03\% $  for the reference one. Systematic investigations elucidate that Eu- $ \mathrm{WO}_{\mathrm{x}} $  nanorods significantly contribute to the improved conductivity, carrier mobility of both  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  ETL and Spiro-OMeTAD HTL, and the crystalline quality of the perovskite film with the large grain size. Meanwhile, because of the perfect energy band alignment of Eu- $ \mathrm{WO}_{\mathrm{x}} $  nanorods, the electrons/holes extraction efficiencies at perovskite/ETL and perovskite/HTL interfaces in PSCs are remarkably boosted. This finding highlights that Eu- $ \mathrm{WO}_{\mathrm{x}} $  nanorods as the interface modifier provide a versatile avenue to construct efficient and stable PSCs for practical applications.

# Introduction

Thin film photovoltaics (PVs) based on hybrid organic-inorganic perovskite have become the most promising candidate for the next-generation solar devices due to their rapidly developing power conversion efficiency (PCE).[1-4] Recently, the planar-type perovskite solar cells (PSCs) based on the  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  electron-transport layer (ETL) have attracted extensive attentions, owing to the simple, low-cost, and low-temperature fabrication process.[5-9] However, the best PCE of  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $ -based PSCs (23.32%) is still lower than that of mesoporous-type PSCs based on  $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $ ,[10] which is mainly limited by the low conductivity, interfacial defects and imperfect energy band alignment.[11, 12] Apart from the PCE, the trap-assisted nonradiative recombination at  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $ /perovskite interface also dramatically affects the hysteresis and stability of devices.[13-15] In addition, 2,2,7,-7-tetrakis(N,N-di-p-methoxyphenylamine)-9,9'-spirobifluorene (Spiro-OMeTAD) as the most common hole-transport layer (HTL), suffers from the low hole mobility, possible degradation in moisture and air, and the metal migration from the electrode easily diffusing into the HTL, which restricts the device performance and long-term stability for commercial development.[16-18] Therefore, the interfacial modification of ETL and HTL is one of the main challenges for highly efficient and stability of PSCs.[19-21]

Substoichiometric tungsten oxide (WO $ _x $ ) possesses variable bandgaps, high electron mobility, superior electrical conductivity, high light transmittance, low material cost, superstability, and adequate tolerance to oxygen vacancies.[22-26] which furnish great prospects to promote the PCE and stability of PSCs. Previous works have tried to employ the WO $ _x $  as the ETL or HTL for constructing PSCs.[27-31] However, such PSCs normally show low PCEs with less than 20%, and high hysteresis due to the imbalanced charge transport at the ETL/perovskite or perovskite/HTL interfaces.[28] Recently, a few works incorporated the WO $ _x $  into ETL or HTL to improve the PCEs and/or stability of PSCs, attributed to the enhanced charge extraction, hindered electron-hole recombination, increased hole transport, and/or the protecting perovskite layer against the migration of Au.[20, 25, 26, 32] Thereby, WO $ _x $  has a great potential to modify the interfaces of HTL and ETL for high-performance PSCs. However, the previous ETL interfacial modification engineering with WO $ _x $  was mainly for TiO $ _2 $  based PSCs, rarely reported for that of SnO $ _2 $ . Meanwhile, the dual interfacial modification for both ETL and HTL with WO $ _x $  has not been reported yet.

Moreover, inspired by the previous encouraging works, the doping (eg. Ta, Nb, Ti etc.) has been considered as a promising avenue to boost the electronic and optical properties of  $ \mathrm{WO_x} $ , which increases conductivity, decreases defect density, and optimizes energy band structure etc..[20, 24, 27, 33] Among the families of doping metal ions, the lanthanide ions have been evidenced as the most appropriate candidates for enhancing the performance of PSCs when doped them into  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  ETL or perovskite etc.[34-38] In particular, lanthanides ions own the unique 4f electronic configuration and spectroscopic properties. Thus, the electronic and optical properties of  $ \mathrm{WO_x} $  can be further optimized and expanded through lanthanides ions doping, thereby improving the electronic/interfacial properties and performance of related devices.

Herein, we employ the rare earth  $ (\mathrm{Eu}^{3+}) $ -doped  $ \mathrm{WO}_x $  nanorods to modify the dual interfaces of carrier transport layers (CTLs) for efficient planar- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  based PSCs. When methylammonium

MAI-capped Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods were introduced between the SnO $ _{2} $  and perovskite layers to improve the performance of PSCs. A schematic diagram of complete device structure of the heterojunction PSCs is displayed with a structure of SnO $ _{2} $ /Eu-WO $ _{x} $ /perovskite/spiro-OMeTAD/Au cells in Figure 2a. The ultraviolet photoelectron spectroscopy (UPS) was employed to characterize the energy levels of the SnO $ _{2} $  and Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods, as shown in Figure 2b. The work function (WF) of SnO $ _{2} $  and Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods is determined to be 4.86 eV and 4.51 eV, respectively. The valence band (VB) of SnO $ _{2} $  and Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods was calculated from the binding energy onset to be 8.23 eV and 7.78 eV. Combining the absorption spectra (Figure S2 and Figure S3), it can be deduced that the conduction bands (CB) of SnO $ _{2} $  and Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods are 4.39 and 4.24 eV, respectively. The energy band alignment of different layers in the PSCs is illustrated in Figure 2c. It can be seen that the CB of Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods is the middle value between SnO $ _{2} $  and perovskite, which is more favorable for the electron extraction, collection and transport. The statistical parameters of perovskite solar cells employing ETL incorporated with different contents of Eu-WO $ _{x} $  under AM1.5G illumination demonstrate that the optimized concentration for Eu-WO $ _{x} $  in the SnO $ _{2} $  for the highest performance of PSCs is 5 wt% (Figure S4 and Table S1). As displayed in Figure 2d, the cell without the Eu-WO $ _{x} $  achieves a PCE of 19.03% with  $ J_{SC} = 23.32 $  mA cm $ ^{-2} $ ,  $ V_{OC} = 1.11 $  V,  $ FF = 73.38\% $  under the reverse scan, and a PCE of 17.19% under the forward scan. The hysteresis index defined by (PCE $ _{\text{reverse}} $ -PCE $ _{\text{forward}} $ )/PCE $ _{\text{reverse}} $ , determined to be 0.097.[15] As a comparison, the champion PCE with adding 5 wt% Eu-WO $ _{x} $  into SnO $ _{2} $  reaches to 21.25% with  $ J_{SC} = 23.74 $  mA cm $ ^{-2} $ ,  $ V_{OC} = 1.14 $  V,  $ FF = 78.52\% $ . The forward scan obtains a PCE of 20.86% with a negligible hysteresis effect (0.018). From the above results, the PSCs with the higher PCE and the improved  $ J_{SC} $ ,  $ FF $  and  $ V_{OC} $  are realized through incorporating the Eu-WO $ _{x} $  into SnO $ _{2} $  ETL. It should be highlighted that the performance of PSCs decreases with adding WO $ _{x} $  into SnO $ _{2} $  (Figure S5), which is mainly attributed to the large size of WO $ _{x} $  nanorods and not well energy band alignment compared to the Eu-WO $ _{x} $  nanorods (Figure S6). The incident photon-to-current efficiency (IPCE) spectra of SnO $ _{2} $ /perovskite and SnO $ _{2} $ /Eu-WO $ _{x} $ /perovskite based devices show that IPCE of SnO $ _{2} $ /Eu-WO $ _{x} $ /perovskite device is larger in the entire visible light wavelength range than that of control device. The integrated current values calculated by the IPCE are 23.06 mA cm $ ^{-2} $  and 23.48 mA cm $ ^{-2} $ , respectively, consistently with that obtained from the  $ J-V $  characterization (Figure 2e). Maximum power point tracking is performed to evaluate the stabilized power output of PSCs (Figure 2f). The PCEs of the champion devices using SnO $ _{2} $  and SnO $ _{2} $ /Eu-WO $ _{x} $  stabilize at 18.87% and 21.10% with photocurrent densities of 21.45 mA cm $ ^{-2} $  and 22.69 mA cm $ ^{-2} $ , respectively, which are close proximity to the values measured from the  $ J-V $  curves. These exceptional photovoltaic performances demonstrate the beneficial effect of SnO $ _{2} $ /Eu-WO $ _{x} $  on PSCs.

# Conclusions

In this work, we simultaneously engineered the ETL and HTL via incorporating  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods, achieving high device performance and stability of PSCs. Firstly, the MAI modified  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods with uniform size were fabricated by the typical hot injection method, followed by the ligand exchange. Then, the optimized device yields a PCE of  $ 21.25\% $  after doping the  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods into  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  ETL, attributed to the effective electron extraction and the reduced recombination loss at perovsktie/ETL interface. Furthermore,  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods in the HTL layer amplify the hole mobility and conductivity of HTL, the hole extraction perovsktie/HTL, and along with protecting perovskite layer against the migration of Au. Finally, a champion device achieves the PCE of  $ 22.08\% $  with negligible hysteresis though dual interface engineering. More importantly, the long-time ambient stability and light stability of PSCs devices are significantly improved. This work suggests that  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods can serve as a promising interface modifier for the efficient and stable PSCs.

# Highlights

1) The excellent electronic and optical properties of  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods significantly contributes to the improved conductivity, carrier mobility of both  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  electron transport layer and spiro-OMeTAD hole transport layer, and the crystalline quality of the perovskite film with the large grain size.

2) Due to the perfect energy band alignment of  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods, the electrons/holes extraction efficiencies at perovskite/ETL and perovskite/HTL interfaces in PSCs are remarkably boosted.

3) Benefiting from the dual interface modification of  $ \mathrm{Eu - WO_x} $  nanorods, the champion PCE of the planar PSCs reaches  $ 22.08\% $  with negligible hysteresis, in comparison to  $ 19.03\% $  for the control PSCs.

4) More importantly, the long-time ambient stability and light stability of PSCs devices are significantly improved.
