ABSTRACT: All-inorganic cesium lead bromide (CsPbBr $ _3 $ ) halide has attracted growing attentions for carbon-based perovskite solar cells (PSCs) owing to its inherent stable lattice in thermal and/or moisture ambient. The main drawback for carbon-based CsPbBr $ _3 $  PSCs is the low power conversion efficiency (PCE) caused by serious charge recombination at perovskite surface and/or device interfaces. To address this problem, an interface engineering strategy by modifying a polyvinyl acetate (PVAc) polymer with carbonyl group at the interface of CsPbBr $ _3 $ /carbon is implemented to passivate perovskite surface defect states and also to improve energy level alignment between valence band of CsPbBr $ _3 $  and work function of carbon, suppressing charge recombination and accelerating charge separation. By introducing graphene oxide (GO) layer for further promoting hole extraction and decreasing energy level difference, the PSC with an architecture of FTO/c-TiO $ _2 $ /m-TiO $ _2 $ /CsPbBr $ _3 $ /PVAc/GO/carbon achieves a champion PCE as high as 9.53%, yielding an improvement by 44.0% compared with 6.62% for the original device. Furthermore, the optimal device free of encapsulation exhibits remarkable long-term stability under high humidity, high temperature and continuous illumination in air. This work provides a new polymer as interface modification material for reducing defect states as well as enhancing energy level alignment and suggests an effective approach for fabricating efficient and stable carbon-based CsPbBr $ _3 $  PSCs.

KEYWORDS:  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $  perovskite solar cells; Interface modification; Polyvinyl acetate; Graphene oxide; Charge extraction

# INTRODUCTION

The power conversion efficiency (PCE) of organic-inorganic hybrid perovskite solar cells (PSCs) has been drastically developed from 3.8% for the first prototype to certified 25.2% due to the implementation of composition engineering, interface engineering, preparation technique, device structure optimization and so on, which show little difference from the PCE of monocrystalline silicon solar cells.  $  ^{1-5}  $   However, the performances of organic-inorganic hybrid PSCs are still difficult to meet the requirements of commercial applications owing to their serious moisture and thermal instability.  $  ^{6-7}  $   In addition, the high cost of precious metal electrodes such as gold, silver and hole-transporting materials (e.g. Spiro-OMeTAD) is also an obstacle to the commercialization of PSCs. The recent works in this field demonstrate the substitution of inorganic cerium ions (Cs  $  ^{+}  $  ) to organic   $  [CH_{3}NH_{3}^{+}/HC(NH_{2})_{2}^{+}]  $   ions to prepare   $  CsPbBr_{x}I_{3-x}  $   (x = 0, 1, 2 or 3) perovskite photoactive layer is considered to be promising solution to improve the stability under natural conditions.  $  ^{8}  $   Among various   $  CsPbBr_{x}I_{3-x}  $   perovskites, full bromine   $  CsPbBr_{3}  $   with high carrier mobility is the most stable all-inorganic perovskite materials because of its long-term tolerance to air and moisture.  $  ^{9}  $   And   $  CsPbBr_{3}  $   solar cells with a standard architecture of FTO/c-  $  TiO_{2}  $  /m-  $  TiO_{2}  $  /  $  CsPbBr_{3}  $  /carbon free of precious metal back electrodes and hole transport materials has become a research hotspot due to its environmental stability, low cost and simple preparation process.

These results indicate that polymer interfacial modification offers a promising route for reducing defect states and coordinating energy level matching as well as enhancing performance of carbon-based PSCs.

# EXPERIMENTAL

Materials.   $  PbBr_{2}  $   (Aladdin; purity > 99.0%), CsBr (Aladdin; purity > 99.9%), graphite (Aladdin; purity > 99.95%), Poly(vinyl acetate) (Macklin;   $  M_{w} = 100,000  $  ) and other reagents were all obtained from commercial suppliers and used directly.

Preparation of   $  TiO_{2}  $   Colloid. The   $  TiO_{2}  $   paste was synthesized using a sol-hydrothermal method.  $  ^{[25]}  $   Specifically, slowly dripped titanium tetrabutanolate (10 mL) into stirring ultra-pure water (100 mL), and continue to agitate at 25 °C for 20 minutes to get the dry filter powders. Then, glacial acetic acid (10 mL) and nitric acid (0.8 mL) were slowly added dropwise to the obtained powder. After agitating in a water bath for 20-25 minutes at 80 °C, added 160 mL of ultra-pure water and stir until the solution

Preparation of GO Particles. The GO particles used in the experiment were prepared by a simple Hummer method. $ ^{26} $  In detail, added 2 g of graphite powder and 1 g of  $ \mathrm{NaNO}_3 $  to 46 mL of  $ \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4 $  (98%) and agitated for 15 minutes using an ice water bath. Subsequently, 6 g of potassium permanganate (temperature below 20 °C) was gradually added to the dispersion solution, continuously agitated for 5 minutes in an ice water bath, and heated the mixture to 35 °C for 30 minutes. After the reaction, slowly add 92 mL of ultra-pure water and stir continuously for 15 minutes. After dilution with 80 mL of ultra-pure water (60 °C), hydrogen peroxide was introduced to remove residual potassium permanganate until the bubbles in the reaction disappeared. The mixture was washed repeatedly with deionized water and lyophilized to obtain the product.

Assembly of PSCs. Zinc powders and 2.0 M HCl were used to etch the FTO conductive glass with the desired pattern, and then fully washed the FTO with deionized water and ethanol. # CONCLUSIONS

In summary, an interface engineering study on modifying PVAc at  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $ /carbon interface to reduce surface defect states intensity of perovskite and to enhance energy level matching between VB of  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $  and WF of carbon is performed here, successfully achieving the reduction of charge recombination and energy loss. By further introduction of GO to promote hole extraction and reduce energy level difference, the  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $  PSC with an architecture of FTO/c- $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ /m- $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ / $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $ /PVAc/GO/carbon achieves a PCE up to  $ 9.53\% $ , which is increased by  $ 44.0\% $  compared with  $ 6.62\% $  for controlled FTO/c- $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ /m- $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ / $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $ /carbon device. Furthermore, the unencapsulated  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $  PSC shows a remarkable stability after being attacked by long-term moisture, high temperature and continuous illumination. Although this work is far from optimized, the reduced defect states, enhanced energy level alignment and charge separation by interfacial modifying of PVAc and GO may offer new opportunities for high efficient and stable  $ \mathrm{CsPbBr_3} $  PSCs.
