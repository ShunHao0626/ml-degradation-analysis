# ABSTRACT

In order to explore the attractive structural and semiconductive properties of two-dimensional bismuthene, exquisite heterojunctions with less interfacial mismatch between bismuthene and   $  SnO_{2}  $   nanoparticle are coincidentally architected by a low-temperature procedure, based on an unique self-adaptive attribute of two-dimensional structure of bismuthene, in combination with a lattice-matching attribute of adjacent lattice-spacing between bismuthene and   $  SnO_{2}  $  . Applied in perovskite solar cells as electron transport layer, the bismuthene-  $  SnO_{2}  $   composite layer turns smoother and transparenter, and endows higher crystallinity for upper perovskite layer. Relying on highly-conductive bismuthene along with semiconductive bismuthene-  $  SnO_{2}  $   heterojunctions, the energy band of integral composite layer is upshifted and the interfacial resistance between composite layer and perovskite layer is reduced, effectively accelerating the electron-extracting without declining the hole-blocking, in comparison with pure   $  SnO_{2}  $   layer. The higher statistical average power conversion efficiency of 18.75% is achieved, compared to the counterpart of 17.35%, and it also maintain high stability almost 80% of its initial efficiency even in ~5% relative humidity environment more than 800 hours, contrastly the counterpart just maintains 50% efficiency retention.

KEYWORDS: bismuthene-  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  heterojunction, perovskite solar cells, electron transport layer, lattice-matching, electron extraction

As an attractive photovoltaic device, the organic-inorganic hybrid halide perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have attracted extensive attention, due to its wide absorption spectra range, high carrier mobility, and small exciton binding energy.  $  ^{9-12}  $   Since the first reported power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 3.8% in 2009,  $  ^{13}  $   the PCE has risen to certified 25.2%, approaching its theoretical value of 33.5%.  $  ^{14}  $   In PSCs, the electron transport layer (ETL) plays very important role by promoting electron transfer and suppressing recombination of electron-charge pair. In addition, the ETL with high optical transmittance property can effectively deliver more illumination to subsequent perovskite layer in a common upright device structure.  $  ^{15}  $   Compared to conventional   $  TiO_{2}  $   ETL, it is demonstrated that the   $  SnO_{2}  $   ETL owning more advantages such as excellent electron mobility (240 cm  $  ^{2}  $   V  $  ^{-1}  $   s  $  ^{-1}  $  ) and low temperature processability (  $  <= 200\ ^{\circ}C  $  ).  $  ^{16-20}  $   The PSCs with   $  SnO_{2}  $   ETL have shows high PCE of 21.6% with almost free of hysteresis.  $  ^{21}  $   To achieve the high PCE, the perovskite layer besides the   $  SnO_{2}  $   layer is subtly fabricated with precise stoichiometry, especially the   $  PbI_{2}  $   contents on device, and it is carried out by harsh processing procedures such as thermal annealing in ambient air condition with 30-40% humidity. These mean complex technologies and expensive processing environment. In addition, a PCE of 20.63% with only 0.03 hysteresis has also been received with   $  SnO_{2}  $   ETL in our previous work.  $  ^{22}  $   To achieve high PCE, a dropwise addition method is employed to subtly fabricate perovskite layer for achieving larger grain size. These still need complex technologies. In order to achieve the PSCs for applicable exploration, a relatively simple method is still required.

# RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

With a solution method, the bismuthene is successfully introduced into the   $  SnO_{2}  $   layer, as described in experimental part and sketched in Figure 1. With different volume content of Bi dispersion solution relative to 2.67 wt%   $  SnO_{2}  $   solution (0, 18, 24, 30 and 36 V: V%), the resultant samples are named as sample-A, -B, -C, -D and -E, respectively. The sample-A without Bi content just indicates the pure   $  SnO_{2}  $ . The resultant films are investigated by scanning electron microscopy (SEM) in top-view. Without Bi additive, the surface of sample-A is uniform, as shown in Figure 2a. With increasing Bi content, the dark spots appear and then change to the larger size with more amount, as shown in Figure 2b-e. In the magnified image of sample-C in Figure 2f, the spots are clearly attaching to the surface. Here, the sample-C is employed as the key sample because of the highest PCE which will be discussed in the following sections. In addition, as shown in Figure 2c, the dark spots reveal the aggregates of bismuthene on the surface. This indicates that the bismuthene is not well dispersed with   $  SnO_{2}  $   on the surface of sample-C, because the 2D materials are tend to bond each other by weak Van der Waals forces.  $  ^{37, 38}  $   Further, targeted at Figure 2g, the energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS) analysis of sample-C is measured and shown in Figure 2h-j. Here, the sample-C is fabricated with thick functional layer in order to achieve more obvious Bi signal for analysis, which is fabricated by repeatedly spin-coating the precursor for 20 times on silicon substrates. It is observed that the Sn, Bi and O elements are all uniform distributed, because the X-ray in the EDS analysis can penetrate the sample in a depth.

# CONCLUSION

In summary, through compositing the bismuthene into   $  SnO_{2}  $   with appropriate content by spin-coating and anneal in low temperature, a bismuthene-  $  SnO_{2}  $   composite structure is generated based on a unique self-adaptive attribute of 2D bismuthene, in combination with a lattice-matching attribute of adjacent lattice-spacing between bismuthene and   $  SnO_{2}  $  . As an ETL in PSCs, the bismuthene-  $  SnO_{2}  $   composite layer endows smooth and less hydrophobic surface, contributing larger gain size of upper perovskite layer. The light transmittance of the ETL is improved and the interfacial impedance is reduced, achieving more effective carrier transport. Compared with pure   $  SnO_{2}  $   ETL (sample-A), the optimal bismuthene-  $  SnO_{2}  $   ETL (sample-C) offers the statistical average PCE of 18.75% in upright device architecture. The PCE of sample-C-based devices can also maintain almost 80% of its initial performance for more than 800 hours in ~5% relative humidity (RH) environment, but the counterpart comparatively degrades below 50% of its initial performance.

# EXPERIMENTAL SECTION

Materials and reagents. The   $  SnO_{2}  $   colloid solution (15 wt%, ~5 nm), isopropanol (IPA, >99.5%), and acetonitrile (>99.8%) are obtained from Alfa Aesar. Bi powder (Bi, 99.999%, 1-6 mm), ammonium peroxodisulphate (  $  (\mathrm{NH}_{4})_{2}\mathrm{S}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{8}  $  , >98.5%) are purchased from Macklin, Shanghai, China. Formamidinium iodide (FAI, >99.5%), methylammonium chloride (MACl, >99.5%), methylammonium bromine (MABr, >99.5%), and 2,2',7,7'-tetrakis-(N,N-dipmethoxyphenylamine) 9,9'-spirobifluorene (Spiro-OMeTAD, >99.5%) are purchased from Lumtec, Taiwan, China. Lead (II) iodide (  $  PbI_{2}  $  , 99.9%), N,N-dimethylformamide (DMF, >99.8%), and dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO, >99.9%) are purchased from Sigma-Aldrich. Li-bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (Li-TFSI), 4-tert-butylpyridine (TBP), ethanol, and acetone are received from Aladdin. All the chemicals and reagents are directly used without further purification.

Preparation of bismuthene. The 300 mg of Bi powder and 1.50 g of   $  (\mathrm{NH}_{4})_{2}\mathrm{S}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{8}  $   were mixed in a 20 mL flask, followed by 10 mL of concentrated   $  H_{2}SO_{4}  $   (98%) and 2 mL 30%   $  H_{2}O_{2}  $  . The mixture was sealed and reacted at room temperature for 12 hours, and then washed with absolute ethanol at least three times to remove unreacted   $  H_{2}SO_{4}  $  .⁶⁴ The prepared powders were dried in vacuum and stored in a nitrogen glove box. Then, 30 mg of resultant Bi powder and 1 mL of deionized (DI) water were slowly added into a reagent bottle in sealed environment. After sonicated for 3 hours and shook for 3 hours, the solution was converted into a gray color. Then the Bi dispersion solution was obtained after the unpeeled bulk Bi was removed by filtration.
