# Abstract

ZnO as a promising electron transport layer (ETL) to   $  TiO_{2}  $   for perovskite solar cells (PSCs) has achieved a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 18.9%, but still lower than   $  TiO_{2}  $  -based PSCs (higher than 20%). Herein, we report the demonstration of a high-efficiency methylammonium (MA) and Cs co-alloying formamidinium (FA) triple cation perovskite based ZnO PSCs via delicate control of the cation compositions and annealing temperature. By virtue of structural, morphological, spectral and electrochemical characterizations and analysis, we find that incorporation of MA and Cs into FA perovskite enables to form high-crystallinity black phase perovskite with reduced surface roughness, inhibit charge recombination and promote electron transfer at the ZnO/perovskite/spiro-OMeTAD interfaces, and hence improve the cell   $  J_{sc}  $   and FF. As a result, the ZnO PSC based on   $  MA_{0.1}FA_{0.75}Cs_{0.15}PbI_{3}  $   being annealed at 110°C achieves a PCE as high as 20.09%, exceeding the previous highest efficiency recorded for ZnO ETL based PSCs. The optimized   $  MA_{0.1}FA_{0.75}Cs_{0.15}PbI_{3}  $   is demonstrated to possess excellent reproducibility and long-term cell durability under ambient condition within 1000 h. Particularly, incorporation of a small portion of Br into the triple cation perovskite i.e.   $  MA_{0.1}FA_{0.75}Cs_{0.15}PbI_{2.9}Br_{0.1}  $   leads to a further enhancement in PCE up to 20.44%, comparable to the best-performing MA and Cs-containing FA-based lead halide   $  TiO_{2}  $   PSCs.

# 1. Introduction

Organic-inorganic perovskite solar cell (PSCs) as an emerging star photovoltaic technology, have drawn enormous attention owing to their high power conversion efficiency (PCE) and simple fabrication process as well as low manipulation cost.  $  ^{1-5}  $   Organic-inorganic perovskite materials have an ABX  $  _{3}  $   structure, typically comprising of an organic cation, A = methylammonium (MA) CH  $  _{3}  $  NH  $  _{3}^{+}  $  ; formamidinium (FA) CH  $  _{3}  $  (NH  $  _{2}  $  )  $  _{2}^{+}  $  , a divalent metal, B= Pb  $  ^{2+}  $  ; Sn  $  ^{2+}  $  ; Ge  $  ^{2+}  $  , and a halogen anion X=Cl  $  ^{-}  $  ; Br  $  ^{-}  $  ; I  $  ^{-}  $  . Formamidinium lead triiodide (FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  ) with a bandgap of 1.48 eV, which is closer to the optimum value of the single junction solar cell, exhibits broad light absorption range (typically peak absorption of ~840 nm), good thermostability and reduced J-V hysteresis.  $  ^{6}  $   Despite these attractive merits, pure FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  -based PSCs have not achieved PCEs as high as conventional MAPbI  $  _{3}  $  -based PSCs. This is because the pure FAPbI  $  _{3}  $   with large radius of the FA cation (2.79Å) lacks structural stability and photoactive black perovskite phase (α-FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  ) can easily transform to photoinactive yellow phase (δ-FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  ) at ambient temperature.  $  ^{7}  $   As demonstrated, the perovskite phase can only stabilize with the Goldschmidt tolerance factor (t) value within 0.8~1.0.  $  ^{8}  $   However, the large radius of the FA cation makes the t value larger than 1 for FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  , resulting in instability of the black perovskite α-phase.  $  ^{7}  $   Alloying with smaller cations such as cesium (Cs) (1.81 Å) or MA (2.70 Å) or Cs plus MA represents an efficient approach to low down the t value and then stabilize the FA-based perovskite structure, and finally resulting in a better optoelectronic performance.  $  ^{7,9-13}  $   Besides, the incorporation of inorganic cation such as Cs and Rb

Up to date, state-of-the-art FA-based PSCs mainly focus on a   $  TiO_{2}  $   nanoparticulate electron transport layer (ETL), and the device PCEs of FA-MA-Cs triple-cation perovskite have exceeded 20%.  $  ^{10,15}  $   However, the fatal disadvantage of   $  TiO_{2}  $   ETL is that a high-temperature annealing procedure (  $  >450^{\circ}C  $  ) or alien element doping or interface modification is often required to enhance its crystallization and electron transport consequence, which severely limits its further commercial application in stretchable cell devices.  $  ^{15,17,18}  $   In contrast, ZnO exhibits several distinct advantages over the commonly used   $  TiO_{2}  $   in aspects of facile low-temperature solution fabrication (  $  <180^{\circ}C  $  ) of various nanostructures, high electron mobility and easy tailoring of electrical and optical characteristics by manipulating its morphology and composition.  $  ^{19-22}  $   Despite these merits of ZnO, the PCEs of ZnO ETL-based PSCs remained lower than 20%.  $  ^{23-25}  $   A recent prominent progress in ZnO PSCs has been made by annealing FA-based triple cation perovskite at 95 °C, with an impressive efficiency of 18.9%. Herein, it is worth noting that an optimal annealing temperature over 100°C for the full crystallization of photoactive FA-based perovskite is experimentally essential, and this also has been demonstrated in high-efficiency   $  TiO_{2}  $   ETL-based PSCs. As such, we are motivated to fabricate high-crystallinity multiple cation perovskite on ZnO ETL at annealing temperature over 100 °C for purpose of attaining a higher PCE comparable to that of   $  TiO_{2}  $   ETL-based PSCs.

All chemicals were used without further purification. The organic cation iodide salts were purchased from Dyesol, and the CsI (99.999%) and   $  PbI_{2}  $   (99.9985%) were obtained from Aladdin. The Spiro-OMeTAD (>99%) were acquired from Dyesol.

# 2.2 PSC device fabrication

The ITO/glass substrate (Xiang Sci. Tech. Co.,   $  8 \Omega \cdot m^{-2}  $  ) was cleaned successively with acetone, detergent, deionized water, and isopropanol, and finally treated under oxygen plasma for 20 min to remove the last trace of organic residues. Compact ZnO layer was deposited onto the ITO substrates by spin-coating   $  [\mathrm{Zn}(\mathrm{NH}_{3})_{4}](\mathrm{OH})_{2}  $   under 2000 rpm for 30s, followed by annealing at 150 °C for 10 min.

The desired solutions of FAPbI $ _3 $ , FA $ _{1-x} $ Cs $ _x $ PbI $ _3 $ , MA $ _y $ FA $ _{0.85-y} $ Cs $ _{0.15} $ PbI $ _3 $  and MA $ _{0.1} $ FA $ _{0.75} $ Cs $ _{0.15} $ PbI $ _{2.9} $ Br $ _{0.1} $  were prepared by dissolution of the FAI, MAI, CsI, MABr and PbI $ _2 $ , stoichiometrically in anhydrous DMSO at 60 °C for 12 h. The perovskite solution was spin coated in a two step program at 1000 and 6000 rpm for 10 and 30 s respectively. During the second step, 100 μL of chlorobenzene was poured on the spinning substrate 3~6 s prior to the end of the program. The perovskite film was then annealed at 150 °C (FA system)/130 °C (FACs system)/110 °C (MAFACs system) for 5 min on a hot plate in a nitrogen filled glove box. The hole transport material (HTM) consisting of 72.3 mg 2,29,7,70-tetrakis-(N,N-di-p-methoxyphenyl-amine)-9,99-spirobifluorene

# 2.3 Device characterization

The morphology of perovskite films and cross sectional view of PSCs were observed on a scanning electron microscope (SEM, JEOL-6701F). An energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS) measurement was performed with an X-ray energy dispersive spectrometer installed on a JEOL-6701F microscope. Atom force microscopy (AFM) height images were characterized with a Bruker Metrology Nanoscope III-D atomic force microscope in tapping mode. The perovskite crystal structure was examined using an X-ray diffractometer (Rigaku, D/max 2500 VB2p/PC) with CuKR radiation ( $ \lambda = 0.15406 $  nm) operated at 40 mA and 40 kV. The light absorption properties of the perovskite/ZnO based films were investigated by UV-visible light absorption/diffused reflectance spectrometry (Lambda 950, Perkin Elmer) in the 300-900 nm wavelength range at room temperature. Steady-state photoluminescence (SS-PL) was characterized at 790 nm after excited at 470 nm with a Xenon lamp. Time resolved photoluminescence (TR-PL) was measured on photoluminescent spectrometer (FLS 980, Edinburgh Instruments), excited with a picosecond pulsed diode laser CW NIR laser at 460 nm as the excitation source and measured at 790 nm. The TR-PL decay curves were fitted with a tri-exponential

Photovoltaic performance of PSCs was characterized by measuring the current-voltage curve under AM 1.5G sunlight with 100 mW cm  $  ^{-2}  $   light output using a solar light simulator (Newport Oriel Sol 3A class, America, calibrated by a Newport reference cell), and the data were recorded with a Keithley 2400 source meter from 1.5 V to -0.5 V with a scan rate of 0.2 V s  $  ^{-1}  $   and a pre-condition of 0.5 s. Firstly, high-crystallization   $  FA_{1-x}Cs_{x}PbI_{3}  $   film (abbreviated as   $  FACs_{x}  $  , x is in percentage of Cs) was obtained by optimizing Cs composition and annealing temperature. Secondly, based on the optimal Cs content, MAI was incorporated into   $  FACs_{x}  $   to form   $  MA_{y}FA_{1-x-y}Cs_{x}PbI_{3}  $   film (abbreviated as   $  MA_{y}FACs_{x}  $  , y is in percentage of MA), and the dependence of MA content and annealing temperature on the optical and electronic properties was investigated to further obtain high-crystallization   $  MA_{y}FACs_{x}  $  .

A series of FA-based perovskites mixed with Cs contents ranging from 0.05 to 0.25 were prepared on the ZnO ETL/ITO substrates being annealed at  $ 130^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $ . Note that pure FA perovskite was obtained being annealed at  $ 150^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $ . Figure 1a shows the XRD patterns of  $ \mathrm{FACs}_x $  film ( $ x = 0 $  to 0.25) on ZnO/ITO substrates. The XRD peaks at 14.05, 19.87, 24.37, 28.19, 31.59, 35.06, 40.23, and  $ 42.79^{\circ} $  are ascribed to the (101), (012), (021), (202), (211), (122), (024), and (131) lattice planes of the trigonal black  $ \alpha $ -phase of  $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $ , respectively. The XRD peak at  $ 2\theta = 11.66^{\circ} $  is from the hexagonal yellow  $ \delta $ -phase of  $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $ . For pure FA perovskite, a mixed phase of yellow  $ \delta $ -phase and trigonal black  $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $   $ \alpha $ -phase can be detected. Besides, a XRD peak from hexagonal  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  at  $ 2\theta = 12.72^{\circ} $  is also found by pure FA perovskite. After only  $ 5\mathrm{mol}\% $  Cs is added, the yellow  $ \delta $ -phase of  $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  peak completely disappears, indicating that alloying a small amount of Cs within  $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  induces complete transformation to the black perovskite phase. Surprisingly,  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  peak starts to decrease upon adding  $ 5\mathrm{mol}\% $  Cs, and finally disappears when Cs content further increases by over  $ 10\mathrm{mol}\% $ . As we know, annealing process is a key to obtaining a high-crystallinity FACs perovskite. High-efficiency FACs perovskite based on   $  TiO_{2}  $   ETL has been attained after being annealed at 100~150 °C for a long period.  $  ^{[16, 27-29]}  $   Thus, the annealing temperatures of 120, 130 and 140 °C are adopted to induce   $  FACs_{0.15}  $   crystallization on the ZnO ETL, respectively, and their XRD patterns are characterized (Figure 1c). Clearly, no   $  PbI_{2}  $   peak can be detected in the three   $  FACs_{0.15}  $   perovskites being annealed at 120-140 °C, and this means a good thermostability for   $  FACs_{0.15}  $   perovskite on the ZnO ETL. Also, there exists hexagonal yellow δ-phase in the   $  FACs_{0.15}  $   perovskite on the ZnO ETL at the annealing temperature of 120 °C. Further increasing annealing temperature to 130 °C, and even higher, a complete black phase   $  FACs_{0.15}  $   perovskite is formed on the ZnO ETL. Based on these results, we select   $  FACs_{0.15}  $   perovskite being annealed at 130 °C for the following spectral and photovoltaic performance characterizations.

# Conclusions

To summarize, a high-crystallinity photoactive black phase MA and Cs co-alloying FA triple cation perovskite film on ZnO ETL has been prepared by delicate control of MA and Cs compositions and annealing temperature. The optimal   $  MA_{0.1}FA_{0.75}Cs_{0.15}PbI_{3}  $   perovskite film being annealed at 110°C achieves a high PCE of 20.09%, which is the top record of the ZnO based PSCs. The outstanding photovoltaic performance based on high-crystallization   $  MA_{0.1}FA_{0.75}Cs_{0.15}PbI_{3}  $   ZnO cell resulting from the enhanced   $  J_{sc}  $   and FF, has been demonstrated to be attributed to reduced surface roughness, suppressed charge recombination and improved electron transfer at the ZnO/perovskite/spiro-OMeTAD interfaces. The optimal   $  MA_{0.1}FA_{0.75}Cs_{0.15}PbI_{3}  $   ZnO PSC also shows excellent reproducibility and excellent long-term cell durability under ambient condition within 1000 h. A further enhanced PCE up to 20.44%, which is very close to the record PCE of 22.1% ever reported for PSCs based on   $  TiO_{2}  $  ,  $  ^{10,15}  $   is achieved by incorporating a small amount of halide Br in the optimal triple cation ZnO PSCs. This work provides a facile and feasible way to attain outstanding ZnO-based PSC cell efficiency and long-term stability together with high reproducibility by delicate control of the perovskite composition and crystallization.
