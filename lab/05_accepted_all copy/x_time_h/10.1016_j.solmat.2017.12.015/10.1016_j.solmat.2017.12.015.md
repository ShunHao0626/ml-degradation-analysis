# 1. Introduction

Solar energy, new renewable energy, has aroused people's extensive interest and attention. In recent years, the efficiency of lead halide perovskite solar cells (PSCs) has been improved rapidly, thereby becoming one of the hottest research areas throughout the world.   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbX_{3}  $   exhibits a strong light absorption of photon whose energy is greater than the forbidden band width (1.5 eV for   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $  , and 2.3 eV for   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbBr_{3}  $  , etc.). What's more, the absorption coefficient of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbX_{3}  $   is closer to that of amorphous silicon [1]. The low exciton binding of the perovskite material can separate photogenerated electron-hole pairs within the material at room temperature [2,3]. With the low recombination rate and high carrier mobility rate in the perovskite material, electron and hole have a considerable diffusion length [4,5], which greatly contribute to the development of the high-efficiency, low-cost and simple-preparation solar cells. The first organic-inorganic lead halide perovskite solar cell with the power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 3.81% was reported in 2009 [6]. Subsequently, the research on PSCs has made remarkable breakthroughs [7–10]. At present, the highest and certified PCE of 22.1% in the PSC device has been achieved [11]. With increasingly improving efficiency of PSCs, top priority has been given to the stability [12,13]. What's more, the

In this work, an efficient and stable inverted planar perovskite solar cell (PSC) with water additive has been prepared in ambient air. Adding moderate water additive to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution leads to growth in quality of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film, because the boiling point of water is lower than that of N,N-dimethylformamide. The annealing temperature of the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film is controlled to 105 °C (slightly higher than the boiling point of water) to obtain the dense perovskite film rapidly in ambient environment with   $  \sim40\%  $   relative humidity. The highest power conversion efficiency (PCE) of PSC with water additive is 14.02%, higher than that (11.17%) of the reference device without water additive. In addition, the aqueous   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   solution is transformed into the stable perovskite film through the method of rapid annealing. The stable   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}\cdot nH_{2}O  $   system can resist the decomposition of perovskite film and weaken iodide ion migration in PSCs, which enhance stability of the device under the atmospheric environment. After being stored in the air for 120 h, the PSC device with water additive still has an PCE of 12.01%, higher than that (1.64%) of the reference device.

In general, the crystal quality of perovskite film is mainly influenced by the solvent choice, precursor composition, deposition temperature, and so on. The morphology and coverage of crystalline perovskite film could be improved by controlling nucleation and growth of the perovskite   $  [15–17]  $  . The crystallinity of perovskites could be enhanced by adding some additives to the precursor solution, which results in a better efficiency   $  [18–20]  $  . High-performance PSCs are conventionally fabricated in a glove box to strictly avoid interference of water and oxygen because the perovskite films would be degraded gradually with time in air   $  [21–23]  $  . A dry atmosphere significantly increases costs of preparation of PSCs, while the ambient air can substantially simplify preparation processes, lower costs and promote commercial production of PSCs. In recent years, increasing researches have been conducted to fabricate PSCs under ambient air, which implies that the moisture plays an important role in the growth of performance of cells   $  [24,25]  $  . Furthermore, many studies have noted that the appropriate content of water can contribute to the crystallization of perovskite during the annealing process   $  [26,27]  $  . # 2. Experimental

# 2.1. Materials and preparation

Chlorobenzene (C $ _6 $ H $ _5 $ Cl), hydrogen iodide (HI) and methylamine (CH $ _3 $ NH $ _2 $ ) were purchased from Shanghai Chemical Industry Co.. Poly (3, 4-ethylene dioxy-thiophene)-poly (styrene sulfonate) (PEDOT:PSS) was bought from Heraeus (Germany). PbCl $ _2 $  (99.999%) and DMF (anhydrous, amine free; 99.9%) were purchased from Alfa-Aesar. Phenyl-C $ _{61} $ -butyric acid methyl ester (PCBM) and 4,7-diphenyl-1,10-phenanthroline (Bphen) powders were produced by Nichem Fine Technology Co., Ltd. (Taiwan). CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ I was synthesized by the chemical reaction of CH $ _3 $ NH $ _2 $  and HI [31]. Aqueous DMF solvent was prepared by adding different volume ratios of deionized water to DMF. CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _{3-x} $ Cl $ _x $  solution was fabricated by solubilizing CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ I and PbCl $ _2 $  in aqueous DMF solvent with a molar ratio of 3:1, and agitating the compound at 60 °C for 12 h in ambient air. The CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _{3-x} $ Cl $ _x $  solutions were filtered by a 0.45 μm Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) filter before fabrication. PCBM solutions were manufactured by dissolving PCBM powder into chlorobenzene and churning the mixture for 12 h in ambient air, with an optimum concentration of 20 mg mL $ ^{-1} $ . Bphen solutions were prepared by lysing Bphen powder into ethanol and rabbling the admixture for 12 h in ambient air, with an optimized concentration of 0.5 mg mL $ ^{-1} $ .

# 3. Results and discussion

In order to study the role of water additive in the crystal growth of perovskite films and the stability of PSC devices in ambient air with  $ \sim 40\% $  relative humidity, we has fabricated a conventional inverted planar heterojunction architecture of indium tin oxide (ITO)/PEDOT:PSS (40 nm)/ $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_{3 - x}\mathrm{Cl}_x $  (270 nm)/PCBM (50 nm)/Bphen (10 nm)/Ag (120 nm) by the method mentioned above. The cross-section FESEM image of PSC prepared is shown in Fig. S1. Bphen film between PCBM and Ag cathode is used as an efficient material of interfacial modification [35]. To analyze the effect of water additives on performance of cells prepared in ambient air, we use the PSCs without water additives as reference device.

The photoelectric performance parameters of PSC device have significantly been improved by adding water additives. Fig. 2a shows the  $ J-V $  characteristics of the PSCs with and without water additives. Reference device has a PCE of  $ 11.17\% $ , short circuit current density  $ (J_{SC}) $  of  $ 18.65\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $ , open circuit voltage  $ (V_{OC}) $  of  $ 0.90\mathrm{V} $  and a fill factor (FF) of 0.69, while device with water additives has a better PCE of  $ 14.02\% $ ,  $ J_{SC} $  of  $ 20.19\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $ ,  $ V_{OC} $  of  $ 0.95\mathrm{V} $  and FF of 0.73. By the method of adding water additives, significant improvements in  $ J_{SC} $  and  $ V_{OC} $  were achieved, which resulted in the increase of PCE from  $ 11.17\% $  to  $ 14.02\% $ . In order to analyze the hysteresis behavior of PSCs, the devices are measured by applying a forward and reverse bias at a scan rate of  $ 0.01\mathrm{V/s} $ .  $ J-V $  curves of devices with and without  $ \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} $  are shown in Fig. S2. Compared with reference device, the devices with  $ \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} $  show a weaker hysteresis. To confirm the enhancement of the  $ J_{SC} $  in the PSCs with water additives, the IPCE spectra of the PSCs with and without water additives is measured (Fig. 2b). The IPCE intensity of PSC is higher than that of the reference device, within a wide wavelength range between 360 and  $ 750~\mathrm{nm} $ . The  $ J_{SC}20.05\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $  of the PSCs with water additives is calculated via IPCE intensity, which is within the allowed error range, compared with  $ J_{SC}20.19\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $  from the  $ J-V $  curve. In order to understand nonradiative recombination in the perovskite films, the steady-state PL spectra and time-resolved PL spectra of perovskite films with and without water additives are shown in Fig. 2c and d, respectively. An enhancement in steady-state PL indicates that the nonradiative decay is significantly suppressed through water additives. Furthermore, time-resolved PL clearly shows that water additives have exerted an important influence on PL lifetime. The perovskite film with water additives gives an increased PL lifetime of about  $ 63.4\mathrm{ns} $ , compared with the perovskite film without water additives (41.1 ns). High performance of PSCs is attributed to high quality of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   films. The quality of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film can be significantly improved by adding suitable water additives to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution. The content of water additives has a crucial influence on the performances of PSCs. The deionized water is added to   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   precursor solution, within doping ratios ranges of 0.5–2.5%. Fig. 3a-f shows the surface FESEM images of the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   films that have varied ratio of deionized   $  H_{2}O  $   and are annealed in ambient air. Although the sizes of perovskite grains are similar, the gaps between the grains with water additives is becoming smaller with the increase in the content of water additives within the range of 0.5–1.5%, which results in a higher surface coverage of the perovskite films. When the content of   $  H_{2}O  $   is increased to 1.5%, gap between grains is the smallest, as shown in Fig. 3d. However, with further increase in the ratio of   $  H_{2}O  $ , more and larger voids between grains are produced, which is because that excessive water leads to the decomposition of perovskite film. In short, the densest and the most uniform perovskite films can be obtained by adding 1.5% of   $  H_{2}O  $ . Dark current measurement is an effective method of evaluating the charge carrier loss and the recombination of free carriers [36]. The J-V curves (Fig. S3) of device with 1.5%   $  H_{2}O  $   and reference device are recorded in the dark conditions. It can be found that the dark current of the device with 1.5%   $  H_{2}O  $   are higher than that of the reference device. The perovskite film with 1.5%   $  H_{2}O  $   could improve the current injection under the condition of forward bias and prevent simultaneously the leakage current under the reverse bias. This is mainly because that the surface coverage of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film with water additives is higher than that of the film without water additives (Fig. 3).

The surface morphology of the perovskite film obtained by spin coating in a glove box is dense and smooth, while the morphology of

the film prepared by spin coating in the air presents the island-like distribution, because the latter has fewer crystal nucleuses than the former. Then, the film prepared in the air is annealed, which restricts the process of formation of new crystal nucleus and leads to a larger gap in perovskite films. The reason is that the evaporation rate of residual DMF solvent is slow. In order to clarify the effect of annealing temperature on the crystallization of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   films, we anneal the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   films with 1.5%   $  H_{2}O  $   at 95 °C, 100 °C, 105 °C and 110 °C respectively. The FESEM images of the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   films are shown in Fig. S4a-d. It can be found that the best-morphology   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film is obtained under the conditions of 105 °C. The annealing temperature (slightly higher than the boiling point of water) of the perovskite film is controlled to obtain rapidly the dense perovskite film in ambient environment. The water has a lower boiling point than DMF, so the rapid evaporation of water can take away part of DMF solvents in the process of annealing. The rapid volatilization of the solvents results in the supersaturation of the perovskite solution and the form of secondary nucleation of the perovskite crystal. More new grains fill the gaps in film, so the island-like perovskite grains formed are connected with each other closely [37,38]. When the annealing temperature is lower than the boiling point of water, slow evaporation of the solvents is more likely to lead to decomposition of perovskite film. However, perovskite films would form a larger void at excessively-high annealing temperature. Perovskite films hardly form a compact network structure, in other words, gaps between grains become bigger and uncovered PEDOT:PSS areas get larger. Fig. 4 shows the J-V curves of devices fabricated by annealing   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film with 1.5%   $  H_{2}O  $   at varied temperatures. The optimal photoelectric property has been achieved at 105 °C annealing temperature.

Remarkably, the PSC devices with water additives exhibit a better stability than reference devices. Fig. 8a-d shows the variation of major performance parameters (namely   $  V_{OC}  $  ,   $  J_{SC}  $  , FF and PCE) of PSCs with and without water additives. The decrease in the   $  V_{OC}  $   of the devices with water

additives is slower than that of devices without water additives within 120 h. The slow decline in   $  J_{SC}  $   results from the very low diffusion in iodide ions of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   film with   $  H_{2}O  $  . The FF of devices with water additives shows a trend—increase and then decline. What's more, the highest FF is achieved after 72 h. The phenomena is highly associated with the light soaking effect in PSCs [56]. The PL studies can provide a direct method that show bulk defects within perovskite film [57]. The photogenerated electrons become major charge carriers in the double-layer PEDOT:PSS/  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   structures. It can be seen that PL intensity increases with the illumination time in the double-layer PEDOT:PSS/  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3-x}Cl_{x}  $   (Fig. S6). This demonstrates the photogenerated electrons are kept in the bulk perovskite, and exhibits an obvious light soaking effect on PL. The photogenerated electrons can effectively occupy the bulk traps and leads to an enhancement in PL under illumination condition. The light illumination may reduce the density of charged bulk defects during measurement. According to the reduced density of bulk traps, it is speculated that continuous light illumination can lead to an increase in the FF. Moreover, it plays a crucial role in slowing decomposition of the perovskite film and weakening ion migration in PSC with   $  H_{2}O  $ , which is very conducive to improving the stability of the PSC device.

# 4. Conclusions

In summary, we have fabricated efficient and stable PSCs by adding water additives in the ambient air. The high-quality perovskite film has been prepared by controlling anneal temperature, slightly higher than the boiling point of water, results in a better PCE of devices. The performances of PSCs have been optimized by controlling the content of water additives. What's more, the best PCE 14.02% has been achieved. PSCs with water additives can resist the decomposition of perovskite and weaken iodide ion migration in moisture circumstance, which leads to higher stability of the device. Optimizing the preparation process of PSCs in the air can promote considerably commercial application of the devices.
