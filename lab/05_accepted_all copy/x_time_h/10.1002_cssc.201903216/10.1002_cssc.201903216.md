# Abstract

Formamidinium-lead-iodide based perovskite (FAPbI $ _3 $ ) is widely used in the field of photovoltaics due to the suitable bandgap (about 1.45eV) and better thermal stability. FAPbI $ _3 $  has two polymorphs (black  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  and yellow  $ \delta $ -FAPbI $ _3 $ ) in ambient temperature. The yellow  $ \delta $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  which has no photoactivity with a chain-like structure is likely to hinder electron transport and reduce the photovoltaic performance. However, pure-phase black  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  without yellow phase is difficult to obtain and the underlying mechanism of phase transition is rarely investigated. Here, we propose a facile bi-additive method (BA method) to eliminate the yellow  $ \delta $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  completely by induction of phase transition from  $ \delta $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  to  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $ . HI and Pb(SCN) $ _2 $  were employed as bi-additives. Based on the investigation of annealing time and temperature, we suppose that the BA method can induce the phase transition and enhance the  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  stability. Due to the enhanced crystallization as well as uniform morphology, the perovskite solar cells (PSCs) made from BA method exhibit an increased power conversion efficiency (PCE). Furthermore, the optimal devices display excellent stability, which can maintain over 80% of initial PCE after 400-hour aging in the air. This work provides us a new insight into fabricating high-quality pure  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  perovskite films and makes the high efficiency photovoltaic devices a reality.

Though PSCs give us hope of making full use of solar energy, the thorns still remain in the road of exploitation.

Formamidinium lead triiodide (HC(NH $ _2 $ ) $ _2 $ PbI $ _3 $  or FAPbI $ _3 $ ) is one of the widely used perovskite materials for PSCs $ ^{[6-8]} $  due to broader range of light response, $ ^{[9,10]} $  appropriate crystal structure $ ^{[11]} $  and increased thermal stability $ ^{[8]} $  when compared to the methylammonium lead triiodide (MAPbI $ _3 $ ) perovskite. Furthermore, FAPbI $ _3 $  based PSCs shows excellent photovoltaic performance owing to distinct phase transition temperature. Usually, FAPbI $ _3 $  exists at room temperature as a non-perovskite, hexagonal yellow phase ( $ \delta $ -FAPbI $ _3 $ ) while the black phase ( $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $ ) is formed at higher temperature. $ ^{[12,13]} $   $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  perovskite phase with a direct bandgap of about 1.45 eV exhibits as a trigonal crystal structure, $ ^{[12]} $  but a recent study suggests that  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  possibly has cubic perovskite structure. $ ^{[14]} $   $ \delta $ -FAPbI $ _3 $  non-perovskite phase appears yellow with indirect bandgap of 2.48 eV. $ ^{[14]} $  It is well-known that FAPbI $ _3 $  suffers spontaneous phase degradation from desired black phase to yellow phase at room temperature. $ ^{[15,16,17]} $  The yellow phase with a chain-like structure is likely to hinder electron transport through the material and reduces the photovoltaic performance, particularly the EQE spectrum. $ ^{[18]} $  To date, many researchers focus on fabrication of high efficiency of MA $ _{1-x} $ FA $ _x $ PbI $ _3 $  and the best cells are fabricated by mixed cations. $ ^{[19-21]} $  Some researchers reported the incorporation of cations with smaller effective radius can adjust the Goldschmidt tolerance factor or relax the

Thus, investigations of the underlying mechanism of controlling phase transition and  $ \alpha $ -FAPbI $ _{3} $  stabilization are essential for further perovskite solar cell performance.

We proposed a facile bi-additive (BA) strategy to fabricate black phase  $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  with no non-perovskite yellow phase. A certain amount of HI and  $ \mathrm{Pb(SCN)_2} $  were used as bi-additives in precursor solution of perovskite. The effect of the concentration of bi-additives on the crystallization and morphology of the films was also investigated. A new intermediate of FAI- $ \mathrm{Pb(SCN)_2} $ -HI-DMF was formed and played important role in the phase transition of yellow phase to black phase. Based on the investigation of annealing time and temperature, we deduced that the intermediate can induce the phase transition and enhance the  $ \alpha $ - $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  stability. The  $ \delta $ - $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  was eliminated completely and high-quality  $ \alpha $ - $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  perovskite film was obtained. As a result, the PCE of the device based on BA film is increased from  $ 14.57\% $  to  $ 20.05\% $  compared with control film. Based on the remarkable  $ \alpha $ - $ \mathrm{FAPbI}_3 $  phase stability, the obtained devices based on BA method shows improved stability with maintaining over  $ 80\% $  initial PCE after being aging for over 400 hours in the air with relative humidity of  $ 35\% $ . # Conclusions

In summary, an effective way to control phase transition and inhibit phase degradation of high quality FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  perovskite layer was introduced. During this process, new intermediate was formed and had effect on phase transition. The crystallization process and degradation process of FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  were investigated by using effective XRD characterization method. When compared with control film, BA film showed better crystal structure and phase stability. Perovskite solar cells based on the BA film showed improving stability in air with 35% relative humidity. These devices still maintained over 81% of initial efficiency after 400 hours storage under unsealed condition.
