# High-Efficiency and UV-Stable Flexible Perovskite Solar Cells Enabled by an Alkaloid-Doped   $  C_{60}  $   Electron Transport Layers

Xiaolong He  $  ^{\dagger}  $  , Cheng Liu  $  ^{\dagger}  $  , Yi Yang, Yong Ding,  $  ^{*}  $   Shuang Ma, Yunzhao Wu, Ye Tao, Xuepeng Liu, Molang Cai and Songyuan Dai  $  ^{*}  $

The inferior stability of perovskite solar cells (PSCs) under ultraviolet (UV) irradiation has been a bottleneck for its further commercialization. Herein, we report a simple strategy to enhance the UV stability of PSCs via employing an alkaloid 1,8-diazabicyclo[5.4.0]undec-7-ene (DBU) doped  $ C_{60} $  electron transport layer (ETL). By improving the electron mobility of ETLs to  $ 2\mathrm{cm}^2\cdot V^{-1}\cdot \mathrm{s}^{-1} $  and optimizing the conduction band position to  $ 4.2\mathrm{eV} $ , the DBU-doped  $ C_{60} $  (DBU- $ C_{60} $ ) ETLs enable the rigid and flexible planner n-i-p PSCs to exhibit PCEs of  $ 20\% $  and  $ 16\% $ , respectively. Most importantly, the devices based on the DBU- $ C_{60} $  ETLs retain over  $ 80\% $  of their initial efficiency after storage either under UV irradiation for  $ 336\mathrm{h} $  or light socks for  $ 1000\mathrm{h} $  in ambient environment. These results indicate that the optimized  $ C_{60} $  is a promising electron transport material to stabilize the PSCs under UV light illumination without the cost of efficiency loss.

# Introduction

Recent research has witnessed unparalleled advances in organic-inorganic perovskite solar cells (PSCs) with the certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) leaping to 25.2%.  $  ^{1-6}  $   The current top-performing n-i-p structured PSCs mainly rely on the use of   $  TiO_{2}  $   as the electron transport layer (ETL) owing to its suitable band alignment and high transmittance.  $  ^{7,8}  $   However, the poor photostability of the   $  TiO_{2}  $  -based PSCs restricts its further commercialization, especially under ultraviolet (UV) irradiation.  $  ^{9-12}  $   Upon UV light exposure, photo generated negative charge accumulation at the   $  TiO_{2}  $  /perovskite interface, which then accelerate the irreversible moisture-driven decomposition of perovskite materials in the ambient conditions.  $  ^{10,13-15}  $

To address this problem, various non photocatalytic materials have been exploited as ETLs, such as inorganic materials   $  (\mathrm{SnO}_{2}, \mathrm{ZnO}_{2}, \mathrm{WO}_{\mathrm{x}} \text{ and La-BaSnO}_{3})^{16-27}  $   and organic materials (ionic-liquids and fullerene-derivative).  $  ^{28-33}  $   Due to the high electron mobility and extreme stability, fullerene   $  (C_{60})  $   has been considered as a promising electron transport material to enhance the UV stability of the PSCs, which benefits from its highest central symmetry in three-dimensional Euclidean space, internal-external double spherical p electron cloud and lack of molecular defects.  $  ^{10, 34-36}  $   Low-temperature solution-processed   $  C_{60}  $   ETLs were successfully used to suppress the continuous

degradation of perovskite devices and remained over 70% of their initial efficiency under hundreds of hours of UV irradiation.  $  ^{9, 12}  $   However, the PCEs of the   $  C_{60}  $  -based n-i-p PSCs are still limited by the energy levels mismatch and charge transport resistance among the carbon spheres.  $  ^{10}  $   Therefore, there is still a lack of an effective and facile route to improve the charge transport of   $  C_{60}  $   ETLs in PSCs, which can make it truly compete with   $  TiO_{2}  $   materials.

Herein, we report a simple method to adjust the energy alignment and enhance the electron transfer property for low-temperature solution-processed  $  C_{60}  $  ETLs via an alkaloid-type n-dopant of 1,8-diazabicyclo[5.4.0]undec-7-ene (DBU). The DBU doping remarkably increased electron mobility of the  $  C_{60}  $  ETLs by 1.5-fold and up-shifted conduction band comparing with the pristine  $  C_{60}  $  film. By adjusting the energy alignment and improving the charge transport, the DBU-doped  $  C_{60}  $  (DBU- $  C_{60}  $ ) ETLs enabled the planner n-i-p PSCs to show an efficiency of 20% and operates stably for over 300 h under UV irradiation. In addition, the flexible PSC based on the DBU- $  C_{60}  $  ETLs exhibits a PCE of 16% and a good mechanical stability. This work confirms that  $  C_{60}  $  is a promising electron transport material to stabilize the PSCs under UV light without the cost of efficiency loss.

# Conclusions

The low-temperature solution-processed DBU- $ C_{60} $  has been successfully used as an effective ETL for PSCs. The PCE of the champion device based on the DBU- $ C_{60} $  ETLs is as high as  $ 20\% $ , which is among the highest values reported so far for  $ C_{60} $ -based n-i-p PSCs. Besides, the flexible PSC based on the DBU- $ C_{60} $  ETLs exhibits a PCE of  $ 16\% $  and a good mechanical stability. The outstanding performance is originated from the high electron mobility and the optimized energy alignment. Particularly, the DBU- $ C_{60} $  ETLs reduces the trap-state density of the films and prolong the carrier lifetime, leading to effectively suppressed hysteresis. Moreover, the DBU- $ C_{60} $  ETLs enable the planner PSCs operates stably for over  $ 300\mathrm{h} $  under UV irradiation and  $ 1000\mathrm{h} $  under light soaking at the ambient environment. The present study points out a new direction to using optimized  $ C_{60} $  ETLs to replace the conventional  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  to enhance the UV stability of the PSCs without the cost of efficiency loss.
