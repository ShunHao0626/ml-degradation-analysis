ABSTRACT:   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have revealed promising application for high-performance photovoltaic devices. Despite high efficiency for regular   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs, poor stability for adverse dopants reflects importance of the inverted. However, the inverted are lagged far for phase transition and inferior   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films. Here, we report an effective post-treatment agent MAPyA to heal and stabilize   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films. MAPyA on   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   can decompose into MA gas and   $  PyA^{-}  $  at 100 °C, and MA could remove pinholes while   $  PyA^{-}  $  gifts effective passivation. In addition, oriented   $  PyA^{-}  $  hinders phase transition and isolate external erosions for long-term stability. The optimized devices show the highest efficiency among the inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs of 16.67%. Besides, phase stability and robustness against external erosions gets considerable promotion. More importantly, unpackaged I-rich inorganic PSCs are rarely reported with long-term MPP in ambient condition and our unencapsulated device still remains 81.31% of initial efficiency after 1800-minutes outdoor operation (RH 30%).

Recently, numerous attentions has been focused on all-inorganic perovskite materials (CsPbX $ _3 $ , X=I, Br, Cl) attributed to their excellent thermal stability and superior photoelectric properties. $ ^{1-7} $  Among inorganic perovskite semiconductors, cesium lead iodine perovskite (CsPbI $ _3 $ ) with suitable light absorption (1.68-1.73 eV) presents the most promising application in high-efficiency photovoltaic devices, which is also the ideal active layer of the top cell in tandem cells. $ ^{8-11} $  However, fabricating stable and efficient CsPbI $ _3 $  perovskite solar cells (PSCs) is still challenge because of the uncontrolled crystalline and phase transition for CsPbI $ _3 $  films. The solution-prepared CsPbI $ _3 $  films easily suffers from the uncontrolled growth for fast crystallization, which leads to inferior and non-dense morphology with massive defects. $ ^{6, 7} $  Additionally, attributed to small-radius Cs $ ^+ $  ions in CsPbI $ _3 $  lattice, unideal tolerance factor ( $ t $ =0.81) gives CsPbI $ _3 $  poor phase stability, and its black phase is easily degraded into non-photovoltaic phase ( $ \delta $  phase) with bandgap of 2.82 eV even in glovebox. $ ^{8, 9, 12} $  Partially replacing I with Br or Cl and reducing dimension (2D) have revealed promotion of phase stabilization. $ ^{13-17} $  Nevertheless, these strategies

Here, we synthesize an effective post-treatment agent of methylammonium pyridine-2-carboxylic (MAPyA) to in situ heal and stabilize   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films for high-efficiency and stable inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs. When annealed at 100 °C, MAPyA on   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   can degrade into methylamine (MA) gas and   $  PyA^{-}  $  ions. The MA gas can repair non-dense   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   with less defects.  $  ^{30, 31}  $   The oriented   $  PyA^{-}  $  anions at grain boundaries (GBs) and surface can strongly interact with   $  CsPbI_{3}  $ , which gifts the effective passivation and act as humidity barriers to isolate moisture erosions. Moreover, the strong interaction with coordination and ionic bonds can suppress the octahedral tilting of   $  [PbI_{6}]^{4-}  $   in   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   lattice to stabilize photovoltaic phase.  $  ^{29}  $

In summary, we have synthesized an effective in situ healing and stabilizing post-treatment agent of MAPyA for high-efficiency and stable inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs. The decomposition product MA gas could remove pinholes with flattened surface while the leftover   $  PyA^{-}  $  ions could suppress the phase transition and isolate moisture erosions, which significantly minimize the trap density and prolong the lifetime of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films. The resulted devices show the champion efficiency of 16.67% with a remarkably enhanced FF and   $  V_{oc}  $ , profiling from less non-radiative recombination and prefect charge transport. Besides, photovoltaic phase stability also get great improvement and target device remains 97.5% of its initial efficiency in glovebox for 120 days. In parallel, tolerance of humidity and heating of the optimized devices also presents significant amelioration. More importantly, we realize the first reported long-term operational stability with unencapsulated   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs under ambient condition and remains 81.31% after MPP tracking under RH 30% ambient condition for 1800 min. Our work has promoted promising application of inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs.
