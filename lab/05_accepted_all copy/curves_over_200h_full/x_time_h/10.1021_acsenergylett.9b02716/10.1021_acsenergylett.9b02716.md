ABSTRACT: Large opening circuit voltage (  $  V_{oc}  $  ) loss and poor moisture stability have significantly hindered the progress of inorganic perovskite solar cells (IPSCs). Here, we report a dual-protection strategy via incorporating monomer trimethylolpropane triacrylate (TMTA) into   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   perovskite bulk and capping surface with 2-thiophenemethylammonium iodide (Th-NI) to ameliorate above issues. Benefiting from growth control and effective suppression of both bulk and surface recombination, the resulted devices show a great improved efficiency from 12.17% to 15.58% with a champion   $  V_{oc}  $   of 1.286 V. Besides, dual-protection strategy endows films with promoted moisture tolerance and the non-encapsulated device remains 83.4% of its initial efficiency after aging at 25% relative humidity for 1540 h. More importantly, the target device shows good operational stability and reveals the unfallen efficiency after maximum power point tracking for 350 h at 45 °C. Our work offers a feasible method to fabricate efficient and stable   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   IPSCs for future commercialization.

Perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have shown great potential as next-generation solar cells attributed to superb photoelectrical properties and low-cost solution fabrication.  $  ^{1-4}  $   The certified efficiency of PSCs has been rapidly improved from 3.9% to 25.2% in the past ten years. However, the stability still lags far behind the requirements for its future commercialization because the organic-inorganic perovskite are endowed with soft crystal and suffer from the volatility of organic component under elevated condition.  $  ^{5, 6}  $   To address thermal instability, substituting volatile organic cations with   $  Cs^{+}  $  ions is a promising channels. All-inorganic   $  CsPbX_{3}  $   (X= Cl, Br, I) perovskite, with superb thermal tolerance and excellent photoelectric properties, have attracted considerable attentions for high-performance and stable PSCs.  $  ^{7-10}  $   Among inorganic perovskite,   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   gifted good phase stability and suitable light absorption (1.82-1.92 eV), gives the most promise for the commercialization of high-efficiency and stable inorganic perovskite solar cells (IPSCs).  $  ^{11, 12}  $

However, large   $  V_{oc}  $   loss and limited moisture stability have significantly hindered the development of   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   based IPSCs. The solution-fabricated   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   always suffers from poor crystallization for the fast crystalline and uneven growth, which results in the inferior film qualify with rough surface.  $  ^{12-14}  $   The non-compact films feature uneven grains size and pinholes, which results in the enrichment of numerous defects and attacked site for external erosions.  $  ^{15}  $   Also, the rough surface causes the poor contact of perovskite/hole (electron) transport layer HTL (ETL) and results in serious recombination at the interface.  $  ^{16}  $   Nowadays,

Toward the goal of realizing efficient and stable IPSCs with the dual-protection strategy, we associate the in-situ GBs passivation with monomer trimethylolpropane triacrylate (TMTA) and surface modification by capping with 2-thiophenemethylammonium iodide (Th-NI). While high-temperature annealing (HTA), TMTA can in-situ crosslink to form the shields to passivate GBs and insulate the external erosions.  $  ^{31, 32}  $   To cap the unstable surface, Th-NI is spin-coated to terminate the surface with moisture resistance. Benefiting from the dual-protection strategy, the issues of   $  V_{oc}  $   loss and moisture stability get significant amelioration in n-i-p normal devices. The devices with dual-protection show considerable efficiency enhancement from 12.17% to 15.58% with the champion   $  V_{oc}  $   up to 1.286 V, which is one of high-performance   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   based IPSCs (Table S1). In addition, the device can contain 83.4% of its initial efficiency after 1540 h aging under 25% relative himidity (RH) ambient condition at room temperature. Moreover, the operational stability also gets great improvement and shows no reduction under maximum power point (MPP) tracking with 100 mW/cm  $  ^{2}  $   LED illuminated at 45 °C for 350 h. The   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   films are fabricated with a two-step annealing process (40 °C (LTA) for 80 s and 180 °C (HTA) for 10 min). The crystallization process of the LTA films holds the key point to develop high-quality   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   films.  $  ^{12, 33}  $   TMTA molecules containing carbonyl groups can interact with   $  Pb^{2+}  $   to control grains growth during LTA.  $  ^{34, 35}  $   Fourier transform infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy are conducted on the deposited films to investigate the interactions (Figure S1). The shift to larger wavenumber of C=O signal of TMTA after introduced into the LTA film reveals the interaction between TMTA and   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   caused by the coordination with Lewis acid   $  Pb^{2+}  $  .  $  ^{34}  $   The delayed crystallization process by TMTA is also investigated (in Figure S2a) and TMTA doped film (BT) shows slower growth than the bare one (Ref), which indicates the better quality of the BT films.  $  ^{36}  $   To evaluate the LTA films, the X-ray diffraction patterns are taken into comparison (Figure 1a). In summary, we have demonstrated that dual-protection strategy via bulk additive with TMTA and surface modification with Th-NI is enabled to realize efficient and stable   $  CsPbI_{2}Br  $   IPSCs.
