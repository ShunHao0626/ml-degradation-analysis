# Polymer-Based Anti-Solvent Engineering to Fabricate Stable and Efficient Triple-Cation Perovskite Solar Cells

Amel Muhson Naji,  $  ^{*[a]}  $   Olfat A. Nief,  $  ^{[b]}  $   Sahira Hassan Kareem,  $  ^{[c]}  $   and Mustafa K. A. Mohammed  $  ^{*[d,e]}  $

Perovskite materials, due to their excellent photovoltaic features, have aroused the researchers' admiration as a photon-absorption film for the next-generation photovoltaics. The interface tailoring of perovskites and hole-transport layer (HTL) is one of the critical approaches to achieve effective and stabilized perovskite solar cell (PSC). Hence, poly[4,8-bis[(2-ethylhexyl)oxy]benzo[1,2-b:4,5-b']dithiophene-2,6-diyl][3-fluoro-2-[(2 ethylhexyl)carbonyl]thieno[3,4-b]-thiophenediyl] (PTB7) as a polymeric organic semiconductors was employed for doping of anti-solvent solution to modify the perovskite film. Results showed with PTB7 dopant; grain boundaries (GBs) of the perovskites are reduced. It suppressed carriers' trap centers in the perovskite film and decreased recombination processes

# 1. Introduction

Nowadays, solid-state perovskites solar cells (PSCs) have pulled considerable interest, and are regarded to be a promise candidate for next-generation photovoltaics (PVs) thank to their distinguished features, including high light harvesting coefficient, high charge-carrier diffusion length, low-cost, etc.  $  ^{[1]}  $   The solid   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   and   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbBr_{3}  $   perovskites originally used for dye-sensitized-based solar cell working as sensitizers.  $  ^{[2]}  $   In this content, all PSCs with high PCE were fabricated in last year by substituting the liquid electrolyte with Spiro-OMeTAD.  $  ^{[3]}  $   Subsequently, efficiency values greater than 20% was reported

Compared to single-cation methylammonium lead iodide, mixed cation perovskite has been revealed to be stronger against exposure to harsh environments.  $  ^{[5]}  $   Therefore, conventional PSCs with organic hole transport layer (HTL) with ambient-fabricated perovskite out of the glovebox have been obtained, which fulfill the commercialization requirements of stable PVs.  $  ^{[6]}  $   In date, best PSCs employ a combination of methylammonium (MA) and formamidinium (FA) as a mixed cation.  $  ^{[7]}  $   However, FA/MA cations are sensitive to heat and humidity environments because their structural instability. The perovskites often contain unwanted defects and grow with less crystallinity describing the large variability reported in the literature.  $  ^{[7b,8]}  $   With incorporating cesium (Cs), the developing mixed-cations (MA/FA/Cs) are more resistance to thermal degradation, have less defects and are less susceptible to fabricating conditions. This allows higher reproducible PSC performance to obtain a high PCE.  $  ^{[9]}  $

As well-known the perovskite layer quality is very important to their optoelectronic merits. Several techniques have been utilized to fabricate a high-quality perovskites film with less impurities phases and defects. Many fabrication approaches have used, include one-step deposition, sequential spin-coating, flash infrared annealing, etc.  $  ^{[10d]}  $   Since its introduction in 2015, the anti-solvent approach has been well-used for one-step fabrication and has become an universal way for depositing a highly crystalline perovskite.  $  ^{[11]}  $   The anti-solvent approach allows quick nucleation for subsequent grain development, resulting in continuous and uniform layer after rapid heat treatment.  $  ^{[12]}  $   Unfortunately, some undesired phase or

# 2.1. Results and discussion

As demonstrated in Figure 1, PTB7 is a commonly employed organic with long carbon chains, which can be utilized as an anti-solvent additive to treat the perovskite properties. PSCs were fabricated with a n-i-p structure of PSC, in which the perovskite crystallized using CB/PTB7 anti-solvent.

The optical absorbance presented in Figure 2a revealed that the perovskite layer modified with PTB7-doped antisolvent showed a higher absorbance in comparison the unmodified film. A moderate amount of 4% PTB7 would cause a significant enhancement in the absorption spectrum of the perovskites within the visible range, implying an improved crystal quality.  $  ^{[19a]}  $   As reported, the lower absorption band in the unmodified perovskite layer was account on the non-perovskite phase of FAPbI  $  _{3}  $   (  $  \delta  $  -FAPbI  $  _{3}  $  ),  $  ^{[20]}  $   which disappeared after the PTB7 modification (Will discuss further by XRD results). However, at an excess amount of the PTB7 (8%) concentration, would result in the decreasing of the absorption of the perovskites. With addition the PTB7, the PSC can absorbs light over the entire visible range, which is significant to the enhancement of J  $  _{SC}  $  .  $  ^{[6b]}  $   Furthermore, the Tauc plots in Figure 2b present a typical magnitude of the bandgap (Eg) of the perovskites about 1.57 eV, which in full agree with the recorded one for the theoretical single junction perovskite with a Eg of 1.55 eV.  $  ^{[21]}  $

# 2. Conclusions

In conclusion, we have constructed a one-step spin-coating processed FTO/ETL/perovskite/HTL/Spiro-OMeTAD/Au devices under air conditions, using organic PTB7 additive in CB antisolvent. The PTB7 treatment enhanced the absorbance of visible light of the perovskite film and contributed to increased photo-generated carriers. The PSCs with the untreated perovskite suffered from recombination issues, which limited the PSC performance to 15.32%. The perovskite-treated perovskite with the optimized additive revealed a dense and pinhole-free highly crystalline perovskite layer. Consequently, the PTB7-based PSC shows high PV performance, which can be associated to the effective charge injection at the perovskite interfaces. The PSC based on PTB7 with 4% concentration yielded a PCE of 19.18%. Additionally, because of the humid-preventing merits of PTB7, the stability of the PSCs was also improved under air conditions without encapsulation. We believe that our work will catalyses the development of new fabrication method, and realize the promising potential of PTB7-based perovskite in the future development of triple-cation perovskite-based solar cells.
