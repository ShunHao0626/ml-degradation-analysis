# Abstract

Full-inorganic  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  optoelectronic devices has received huge attentions in energy technology owing to its prime photovoltaic properties and promising thermal stability. However,  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite films obtained by conventional spin coating always have bad morphology, poor crystallinity and thin thickness, which subsequently reduced the device performance. In this work, an assistant solution, isopropanol solution containing different organic iodide salt (FAI), was successfully introduced during the spin coating process, where isopropanol solution acted as the effective anti-solvent and FAI was used as the surface passivator. A film with suitable thickness (500 nm) with pure phase perovskite film having an average grain size of  $ 600~\mathrm{nm} $ , high coverage, and being pinhole-free, could be created by further controlling and optimizing the  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  crystals. The high-quality  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  film resulted in greatly impeded charge recombination and increased charge transfer and extraction efficiency. Therefore, a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of about  $ 9\% $  was obtained, which is  $ 110\% $  enhanced than that of the pristine device. In addition, the proposed  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite solar cells (PSCs) shown excellent long-time stability by encapsulation, which could show a  $ 10\% $  initial PCE loss after storing in the ambient environment for  $ 1000\mathrm{h} $ . This work indicates that the adopted strategy is an effective and huge potential technology to improve the photovoltaic performance, which can be further applied in other inorganic photovoltaic field.

# 1. Introduction

Since being applied to the photovoltaic field, devices derived from organic-inorganic hybrid materials have achieved unprecedented improvements in a few short years due to its excellent optical traits, outstanding carrier mobility and reasonable band gap structure.  $  ^{1-5}  $   The highest power conversion efficiency (PCE) certified till now has been 25.2%. However, perovskite solar cells (PSCs) based on the mixed halide perovskite materials have natural drawbacks of thermal instability and easy decomposition in moisture environment.  $  ^{6-8}  $   For enhancing the stability of PSCs and finding a solution to this problem, the approach of full replacement of the organic groups   $  [\mathrm{HC}(\mathrm{NH}_{2})_{2}^{+}(\mathrm{FA}^{+})  $   and   $  \mathrm{CH}_{3}\mathrm{NH}_{3}^{+}(\mathrm{MA}^{+})]  $   in hybrid perovskite to develop full-inorganic materials has been mooted. Due to the removal of sensitive organic groups, the full-inorganic PSCs exhibit significantly improved moisture and thermal stability.  $  ^{9-14}  $

Among the interested full-inorganic perovskites, mixed inorganic halide perovskites (CsPbI $ _{X} $ Br $ _{3-X} $ ) have attracted increasing attention. Compared to the easy phase transfer of CsPbI $ _{3} $  even in a nitrogen atmosphere and the unfavorable large bandgap of CsPbBr $ _{3} $  (2.3 eV). $ ^{15-18} $  CsPbI $ _{X} $ Br $ _{3-X} $  light absorb layer exhibits adjustable band gap and satisfactory stability features. Therein, CsPbIBr $ _{2} $  materials show great development prospects because of its reasonable bandgap (2.05 eV). So far, some researches on inorganic CsPbIBr $ _{2} $  PSCs have made unprecedented progress. For example, Lau et al., for instance, to fabricate CsPbIBr $ _{2} $  PSC, used spray-assisted deposition indicating a stabilized PCE of 6.3% at 300 °C for 0.25 hours with good

# 2 Experimental

# 2.1 Device Fabrication.

Before using, the residual organic matter was removed after the Fluorine-doped Tin Oxide (FTO) coated glass substrates were treated with ultraviolet ozone for 30 minutes after cleaning ultrasonically in acetone, isopropanol, deionized water, and ethanol for 10 minutes, respectively. Dissolving 0.4428 g of   $  PbBr_{2}  $   and 0.3117 g of CsI (molar ratio 1:1) in 1 mL of DMSO the preparation of the   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   precursor solution was done. To prepare the   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer the preparation of the 0.15 M titanium disopropoxide bis-(acetylacetonate) solution (75 wt % in isopropanol, dissolved in 1-butanol) was carried out. To obtain a compact thin   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer (c-  $  TiO_{2}  $  , 30 nm), the   $  TiO_{2}  $   precursor solution was sintered at 500 °C for 30 minutes after being spin-coated on the FTO glass which was pre-prepared at 2500 rpm for 30 seconds. About 80 μL of   $  CsPbIBr_{2}  $   precursor solution was dropped onto FTO/c-  $  TiO_{2}  $   substrates with 600 rpm for 5 seconds and 3500 rpm for 45 seconds, respectively, for the synthesizing of the perovskite layer. After the passage of 20 seconds when the last spinning step started, 400 μL of IPA containing inorganic iodide salt formamidinium iodide (FAI) was added. Subsequently, with the protection of a nitrogen-filled glove box the substrates were annealed at 100 °C for 1 hour. In the next step, in a nitrogen-filled atmosphere, 2 mL Spiro-OMeTAD solution with 100 mg of Spiro-MeOTAD, and 45 μL of 4-tert-

# 4. Conclusions

In summary, we first introduced IPA solution containing FAI during the fabricated process of the  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite in order to obtain the high-quality full-inorganic PSCs. By adjusting the amount of FAI in the IPA solution, the grain size of the  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  film could be optimized in the film morphology. Our work showed that the IPA solution could guide the nucleation and growth of the  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  perovskite films to achieve full coverage and pin-hole free  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  film, and FAI surface passivator could further assist the  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  film to gain larger grain sizes, higher crystallinity interface defects, reduce the carrier recombination at grain boundaries. Consequently, compared to that of the pristine device, the optimal  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  PSC attained the PCE of  $ 8.65\% $ , which was enhanced by  $ 110\% $ . A favorable long-time stability (by retaining after aging for 1000 hours,  $ 90\% $  of the initial PCE) was displayed by the encapsulated optimal  $ \mathrm{CsPbIBr_2} $  PSCs significantly.
