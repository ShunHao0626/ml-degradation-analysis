# ABSTRACT

Enhancement of open-circuit voltage (Voc) is an effective way to improve power conversion efficiency (PCE) of the perovskite solar cells (PSCs). Theoretically, work function engineering of   $  TiO_{2}  $   electron transport layer can reduce both the loss of Voc and current hysteresis in PSCs. In this work, two-dimensional   $  g-C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets were adopted to modify the compact   $  TiO_{2}  $   layers in planar PSCs, which can finely tune the work function (  $  W_{F}  $  ) and further improve the energy level alignment at the interface to enhance the Voc and diminish the hysteresis. Meanwhile, the quality of perovskite films and charge transfer of the devices were improved by   $  g-C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets. Therefore, the PCE of the planar PSCs was champed to 19.55% without obvious hysteresis compared with the initial 15.81%, mainly owing to the remarkable improvement of   $  V_{OC}  $   from 1.01 to 1.11 V. In addition, the stability of the devices was obviously improved. The results demonstrate an effective strategy of   $  W_{F}  $   engineering to enhance Voc and diminish hysteresis phenomenon for improving the performance of PSCs.

# 1 Introduction

Halide perovskite materials have outstanding photoelectric characteristics, such as suitable direct bandgap, high absorption coefficient, high charge carrier mobility, and long charge diffusion length   $  [1-3]  $  . In particular, perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have gained significant attention because of the boosting power conversion efficiency (PCE), low-cost production and simple fabrication process   $  [4-6]  $  . Typically, PSCs can be divided into mesoporous and planar configuration   $  [7]  $  . Due to the simplified configuration, the planar PSCs is beneficial for commercial applications. In n-i-p planar solar cell, perovskite layer is sandwiched between electron transport layer (ETL) and hole transport layer (HTL)   $  [8, 9]  $  . The ETL plays a vital role to collect and transfer electrons. Importantly, the ETL has an important influence on the crystallization of the perovskite films.

Nowadays, great efforts have been devoted to exploiting effective ETL for efficient n-i-p planar PSCs. Undoubtedly,   $  TiO_{2}  $   is the shared ETL material because of excellent photoelectric properties, appropriate electronic band structure and superior chemical and thermal stability [10]. However, there is a large mismatch between the   $  W_{F}  $   level of   $  TiO_{2}  $   and perovskite layer in PSCs [11, 12], which results in the serious loss of Voc and hysteresis phenomenon due to the unbalanced charge extraction ability at the both interfaces of ETL/perovskite and perovskite/HTL [13–17]. The interface modification is an efficient strategy to tune the   $  W_{F}  $   of   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer [18]. In our previous work, a self-assembling monolayer (SAM) was incorporated to shift the

$ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  level of  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  nanorod arrays from 4.15 to  $ 3.94\mathrm{eV} $  [19]. However, the in-situ deposition of the SAM takes much time. Normally, wide-bandgap metal oxides were incorporated as intercalation to elevate the  $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  level of  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer, such as  $ \mathrm{MgO} $ ,  $ \mathrm{CaTiO_3} $  and  $ \mathrm{SrTiO_3} $  [20-23]. But the synthesis process is complex and high cost, restricting the large-scale fabrication. Therefore, it is desired to develop simply and efficient method for  $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  engineering of the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer.

$ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  is a metal-free, non-toxic and earth-abundant polymeric semiconductor [24, 25], in which the structure consists of  $ \mathfrak{sp}^2 $  hybridized N and C atoms for covalent tri-s-triazine frameworks [26, 27].  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets with two-dimensional (2D) structure have characteristics of high stability, low cost, easy and high-yield production, which has been widely applied in various fields, such as photocatalysis, sensing and solar cells [28-32]. In previous PSCs,  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets have been incorporated into perovskite precursor to delay the crystallization rate for improving the quality of perovskite films [33, 34]. Significantly, the  $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  level of  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets is higher than that of  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  [35]. Thus, if the  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets were employed to modify  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer, the  $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  level would be improved, which would further decrease the loss of Voc and hysteresis phenomenon in PSCs [36].

In this work, a highly stable ink of  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets was successfully prepared, which was further incorporated into  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ -based planar PSC for enhancing the performance and eliminating the hysteresis phenomenon. The  $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  level of  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer was raised with value from 4.15 to  $ 3.95\mathrm{eV} $ , leading to

# 2 Experimental

# 2.1 Preparation of g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets dispersion

Firstly, 10 g melamine was placed in a ceramic crucible (100 mL), transferred into a muffle furnace, and heated at 550 °C for 4 h with a ramp rate of 5 °C/min. After cooling down to room temperature, the obtained yellow mass was milled into fine powder, which was followed to reheat at 500 °C for 2 h again. The light yellow fine powder was added to ethanol and ultrasonic treated for 12 h and then centrifuged to remove the possible large aggregates. Finally, the supernatant was collected with the final concentration of 1 mg/mL. Here, the obtained g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $  nanosheets dispersion can be stable for more than one year.

# 2.2 Device fabrication

Fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO)-coated glass substrates were etched by Zn powder and 2 M HCl solution, followed by successively ultrasonic cleaning in acetone, deionized water and ethanol for 20 min, respectively. After being dried by   $  N_{2}  $   flow, the FTO surface was treated with ozone for 15 min. For compact   $  TiO_{2}  $   (c-  $  TiO_{2}  $  ) layer, a precursor solution was spin-coated at 2,500 rpm for 30 s, and sequentially annealed at 500 °C for 30 min in muffle furnace. Here, the c-  $  TiO_{2}  $   precursor solution was prepared as our previous works [19]. Typically, 369 μL titanium isopropoxide was dissolved in 2.53 mL isopropanol, 35 μL HCl (2 M) was added in 2.53 ml isopropanol, and the later solution was dropped-wise into the former solution under vigorous stirring. For g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   modification, the as-prepared g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets dispersion was spin-coated on c-  $  TiO_{2}  $   layer at 2,500 rpm for 30 s, and heated at 120 °C for 15 min in air condition (marked as “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}-1  $  ”). To explore the effect of the g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   modification, the above process was repeat once (marked as “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}-2  $  ”).

For perovskite deposition, the precursor solution was prepared by adding 462 mg PbI $ _{2} $  and 159 mg CH $ _{3} $ NH $ _{3} $ I into a mixed solvent of 0.64 mL N,N-dimethylformamide and 0.16 mL dimethylsulfoxide under stirring for more than 3 h. Then the precursor solution was spin-coated on the g-C $ _{3} $ N $ _{4} $ /TiO $ _{2} $  films at 500 rpm for 3 s and then 4,000 rpm for 20 s. During the above second step of spin coating at 10 s, 300 μL chlorobenzene was dropped onto the rotating substrates to extract the solution for enhancing the film crystalline quality. Immediately, the films were annealed on a hotplate at 75 °C and 105 °C for 10 min, respectively. The HTL was spin-coated on perovskite films at 3,000 rpm for 35 s. The HTL precursor solution was prepared as followings: 72.3 mg spiro-OMeTAD powder was first dissolved in 1 mL chlorobenzene under stirring for over 1 h, then 28.8 mL 4-tert-butylpyridine (TBP) and 17.5 mL Li-TFSI

(520 mg/mL in acetonitrile) were added, and continuously stirred for more than 12 h. Finally, 150 nm silver electrodes were thermally evaporated under   $  10^{-4}  $   bar vacuum to finish the device fabrication. The active area of the devices was defined as   $  0.09 \, cm^2  $   by appropriate mask.

# 2.3 Characterization

The crystal structure was analyzed through X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns by Bruker D8 Advance X-ray diffractometer with Cu-Kα radiation (  $  \lambda = 1.5418  $  ). The morphology was obtained by scanning electron microscopy (SEM, FEI NOVA NanoSEM 450) and transmission electron microscope (TEM, FEL Talos F200X). The ultraviolet photoelectron spectroscopy (UPS) measurement was performed with an AXIS Supra. The UV-vis absorption spectra of perovskite films were measured by PerkinElmer Lambda 650 S spectrophotometer. The photoluminescence (PL) spectra were obtained by an Edinburgh Instruments spectrometer (FLS980) with excitation source of 450 nm pulsed laser (< 100 ps, 1 MHz, Wuhan Yangtze Soton Laser Co., Ltd, China). The current density-voltage (J-V) curves were collected by Keithley 2400 Source under standard AM 1.5 G (100 mW/cm²) illumination from a solar simulator (Newport 91150 USA). The incident photon-to-electron conversion efficiency (IPCE) spectrum was recorded by Keithley 2400 Source under 150 W tungsten lamp with a 1/4 m monochromator (Spectra Product DK240). The electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) was acquired with an electrochemical workstation under dark condition at a forward bias of 0.2 V with amplitude of 0.01 mV in a frequency range from 1 Hz to 1 MHz.

Importantly, the Voc in PSCs is theoretically decided by the splitting of quasi   $  E_{F}  $   levels between ETL and HTL under illumination, which was increased via g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets modification. Therefore, the g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets could enable the less offset of energy level at the interface of ETL and perovskite layer, and reduce the loss of   $  V_{OC}  $   in   $  TiO_{2}  $ -based PSCs [40].

Figure 7(a) shows the comparison of J-V hysteresis for various devices. The bare   $  TiO_{2}  $  -based device present severe hysteresis, which is the common phenomenon in the   $  TiO_{2}  $  -based planar devices [16]. However, the “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $  -1”-based device has negligible hysteresis. Usually, the J-V hysteresis of PCSs were induced by charge collection and transport related to charged defects and interfacial charge accumulation [50, 51]. As demonstrated above, the “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $  -1”-based device exhibited well-aligned energy level between ETL with perovskite due to tune the   $  W_{F}  $   level of c-  $  TiO_{2}  $  , which cause the charge transfer more efficient between the interfaces. Moreover, the grain size of perovskite films were increased by g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $   nanosheets modification. Thus, the hysteresis is negligible in the “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $  -1”-based PSCs.

The stability of the unencapsulated PSCs with and without g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $  modification were further surveyed in ambient air at room temperature and 40%–65% relative humidity. From Fig. 7(b), it can be seen that the PCE of  $  TiO_{2}  $ -based PSCs exhibits sharp degradation with dropping to 40% after 240 h, while the “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $ -1”-based device retain about 90%. In general, the  $  TiO_{2}  $  surfaces contain abundant oxygen vacancies, which would cause deep sub-band gap trap states, decompose the perovskite film and increase carrier recombination at the interfaces [52, 53]. The results reveal that “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $ -1” modification can efficiently cover the trap states at the interface between  $  TiO_{2}  $  and perovskite layers. Furthermore, the PCE of “g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $ -1”-based device still retain 85% after 320 h. Therefore, the g-  $  C_{3}N_{4}  $  nanosheets are expected to be a novel effective interfacial modification material with high performance and superior stability for  $  TiO_{2}  $ -based planar PSCs.

# 4 Conclusions

In summary, the 2D polymeric  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets were deposited on  $ \mathrm{c - TiO_2} $  layer to improve perovskite quality and energy level alignment for enhancing the Voc and diminishing the hysteresis of the enhanced planar PSCs. After  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets modification, crack-free and pinhole-free smooth perovskite film with large grain size was obtained. Moreover, the  $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  level of modified  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer was raised, resulting in a larger Voc and better energy level alignment. Meanwhile, the charge recombination was inhibited at the ETL/perovskite interface. Therefore, the optimized PSCs boosted PCE from the initial  $ 15.81\% $  to  $ 19.55\% $  without obvious hysteresis. This work provides an effective simple strategy for the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $   $ W_{\mathrm{F}} $  engineering by  $ \mathrm{g - C_3N_4} $  nanosheets intercalation to enhance the performance and diminish the hysteresis phenomenon in planar PSCs.
