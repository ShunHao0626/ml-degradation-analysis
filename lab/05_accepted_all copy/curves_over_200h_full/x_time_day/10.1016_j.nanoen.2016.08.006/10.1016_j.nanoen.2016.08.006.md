# Abstract

One of critical issues for practical applications of perovskite solar cells (PSCs) is how to enhance the photocurrent and air stability of the peovskite materials. Here, we report an air-stable, hole-conductor-free (HCF), high photocurrent PSC based on  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ -NiO nanoparticles ( $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs) composite. The composite solution contains highly dispersed NiO NPs in solution of  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{I} $  (MAI) and  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  in dimethylformamide. By introducing the  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ -NiO composite into the active layer, the HCF-PSCs with FTO/c- $ \mathrm{TiO}_2/\mathrm{mp}-\mathrm{TiO}_2/\mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO/Au architecture have been fabricated under ambient conditions. This composite-based HCF-PSCs show a high photocurrent density ( $ J_{sc} $ ) of  $ 26.41~\mathrm{mA/cm^2} $ , which is  $ 97\% $  of theoretical maximum (i.e.,  $ 27.2~\mathrm{mA/cm^2} $ ). Compared to the power conversion efficiency (PCE) of  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -based HCF-PSC (i.e.,  $ 5.43\% $ ), the  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs composite-based HCF-PSC showed a high PCE of  $ 12.14\% $ . This increase in PCE is mainly due to a remarkable increase in photocurrent density, suggesting that the  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO composite enhances the charge carrier generation via improving photo-absorption and fast hole-conduction by NiO NPs. More interestingly, the composite-based HCF-PSCs without encapsulation showed remarkable air stability with retaining  $ \sim 90\% $  of its original PCE and  $ \sim 94\% $  of both  $ J_{sc} $  and FF for 60 days under ambient environment. XPS and IR spectra analysis revealed that Ni-O, Pb-O, C-O, Ni-N and N-NiO bonds attributed to strong chemical interaction between NiO and  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $  molecules enhance the air stability of  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO composite based HCF-PSCs.

# 1. Introduction

There has been an unprecedented rise of power conversion efficiency (PCE) exceeding  $ 20\% $  that was reported by Seok et al.[1] Such success is mainly due to the fabulous properties of perovskite materials such as direct band gap, low exciton binding energy, long diffusion length and carrier lifetime, broad absorption range from visible to near infrared spectrum, etc.[2-7] However, the poor stability of both the perovskite and charge-carrier materials has been a bottleneck in the commercial development of high-performance and long-term stability PSCs which can withstand sustained operation in ambient environment [8-13]. The air-induced instability is attributed to degradation of perovskite and organic hole-transport materials (for example, spiro-OMeTAD and PEDOT:PSS) caused by chemical interaction with moisture [14-19] and oxygen [10,20]. To solve the air-induced degradation problem of perovskite materials, different methods have been studied; introduction of hydrophobic buffer layer on the top of perovskite layer [16,19], utilization of water-resistant materials [10,17,18] and mesoporous  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  and  $ \mathrm{Al_2O_3} $  layer to reduce superoxide  $ \mathrm{O}_2^- $  which leads to deprotonation and decomposition of  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $  [14,20].

Compared to the expensive and chemically degradable organic hole-transport materials (HTMs), p-type inorganic HTMs have received considerable attention because of low cost synthesis, compatible energy level relative to perovskites, efficient hole transfer attributed to relatively high conductivity, and device stability. We fabricated an HCF-PSC using the  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs composites, synthesized by blending  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $  and NiO NPs. By embedding the composite film in an inverted device with the configuration of glass/FTO/c- $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $ /mp- $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $ / $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs/Au, we attained a PCE of  $ 12.14\% $  with  $ J_{\mathrm{sc}} = 26.41 \mathrm{~mA/cm}^2 $  which is  $ 97\% $  of theoretical maximum current density (i.e., 27.2 mA/ $ \mathrm{cm}^2 $ ) [46], superior to the values reported so far for any perovskite-based solar cell (i.e.,  $ 20 \pm 2 \mathrm{~mA/cm}^2 $ ). # 2. Materials and Experimental

# 2.1 Preparation of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $

$ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{I} $  (MAI) was synthesized by reacting methylamine (40 wt% in water, purchased from Sigma-Aldrich) and hydroiodic acid (57 wt% in water, purchased from YAKURI) with the molar ratio of 1.2:1 in an ice bath for  $ 2\mathrm{h} $  under continuously stirring, and followed by rotary evaporation to remove the solvents and crystallize MAI [47]. The as-synthesized MAI was washed in ethyl alcohol and precipitated with diethyl ether with several times. Finally, the white

# 2.2 Preparation of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}-NiO  $   composite solutions

$ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  precursor was formulated by dissolving titanium diisopropoxide bis (acetylacetonate) in butanol with weight percentage of  $ 16\mathrm{wt}\% $  , and then stirred for  $ 12\mathrm{h} $  yielding to a clear and transparent solution, and followed by filtering with a  $ 0.2\mu \mathrm{m} $  syringe filter prior to spin-coating. For mp- $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer, the solution of  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  NPs (30 wt%) was prepared by diluting NPs (25-30 nm, purchased from Solaronix, Switzerland) in ethyl alcohol under ultra-sonication for 30 min, followed by stirring overnight. To prepare a solution of  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs composite, NiO NPs (< 50 nm, Sigma-Aldrich) dispersed in dimethylformamide (DMF) are mixed with as-synthesized MAI and  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  (Sigma-Aldrich) in dimethylformamide (DMF) with molar ratio of 1.2:1 and ultra-sonicated for  $ 1\textrm{h} $  . As-prepared composite solution is then homogenized for 20 min in a homogenizer (THINKY Mixer System, K-CORPORATION), followed by stirring overnight. To examine the effect of constituent of  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs on the device performance, two sets of composite are prepared with varying the loading amount of NiO NPs from 0 to 8 wt% at constant  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $  concentration of  $ 40\mathrm{wt}\% $  and varying the  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $  concentration from 38 to  $ 45\mathrm{wt}\% $  at constant NiO NPs loading of  $ 5\mathrm{wt}\% $ .

# 2.3 Device Fabrication

All the fabrication processes of the PSCs based on MAPbI $ _3 $ -NiO NPs composite were carried out under ambient conditions. FTO substrates were thoroughly cleaned by using detergent, diluted water, and acetone and ethanol. To prepare a compact TiO $ _2 $  (c-TiO $ _2 $ ) layer, the TiO $ _2 $  NPs solution (16 wt%) was spun on the FTO substrate at 3000 rpm for 30 s and heated at 250 °C on a hot plate. The mp-TiO $ _2 $  layer was subsequently deposited on the c-TiO $ _2 $  layer by spin-coating the TiO $ _2 $  NPs solution (30 wt%) at 4000 rpm for 60 s, followed by annealing at 500 °C for 1 h in a box furnace. The MAPbI $ _3 $ -NiO NPs composite solution was then spin-coated on the FTO/c-TiO $ _2 $ /mp-TiO $ _2 $  at 1500 rpm for 30s, followed by two-step annealing, i.e. at 130 °C for 2 min and at 100 °C for 1 h. Finally, Au electrodes were deposited through a shadow mask in a thermal evaporator (JV15EVA-15K2P).

# 2.4 Characterizations

Structural morphology of the device configuration was examined by field emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM, Hitachi S4700, and SUPRA 40VP). Crystallinity of materials was examined by multi-purpose high performance X-ray diffraction (XRD, Rigaku) with Cu-Kα radiation,  $ \lambda = 1.540598 $  Å in the range of  $ 10 - 60^{\circ} $  and  $ 10 - 90^{\circ} $ . The absorption properties of the composite was analyzed with an ultraviolet-visible (UV-vis) spectroscopy. # 4. Conclusions

We found that the  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO composite enhances the charge carrier generation via improving photo-absorption and fast hole-conduction by NiO NPs. # Highlights

- Air-stable, hole-conductor-free (HCF), high photocurrent perovskite solar cells based on  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ -NiO nanoparticles ( $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs) composite

- Remarkable increase in photocurrent density due to enhanced light harvesting and fast hole-conduction by the  $ \mathrm{MAPbI}_3 $ -NiO NPs composite

- Remarkable air stability with retaining ~90 % of its original PCE and ~94% of both   $  J_{sc}  $   and FF for 60 days under ambient environment.

- Strong chemical interaction between NiO and  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  molecules and formation of multiple heterojunctions
