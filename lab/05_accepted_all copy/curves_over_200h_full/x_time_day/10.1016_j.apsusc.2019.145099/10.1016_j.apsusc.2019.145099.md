# Abstract

In this work, Graphene quantum dots (GQDs) was decorated on the  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  electron transport layers (ETLs) to boost the performance of perovskite solar cells (PSCs). The power conversion efficiency (PCE) of  $ 21.1\% $  was acquired with the combination of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  and GQDs. Compared with the  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $ -only ETL devices ( $ 18.6\% $ ), the PCE of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2 / GQDs} $  based devices is greatly enhanced in PCE by the value of  $ 13.4\% $  as well as in the stability. Investigation reveals that with the combination of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  and GQDs, the open circuit voltage ( $ V_{OC} $ ) and the short circuit current density ( $ J_{SC} $ ) could increase. Various advanced optical and electrical characterizations were carried out to explore the action mechanism of GQDs. The experimental results convinced that the introduction of GQDs leads to the better carrier transportation and extraction by increasing the electronic coupling and matching the energy levels between the perovskite and  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  ETL. Also, the perovskite film quality deposited on the GQDs decorated  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  ETL is better compared with the  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  only. Furthermore, the device stability based on the GQDs modified  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  is evidently improved contrast to the  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $ -only device. Its performance kept above  $ 80\% $  of initial value after  $ 720\mathrm{h} $  storage.

Key words: perovskite solar cells; electron transport layer; graphene quantum dots;  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $

# Introduction

Organic-inorganic hybrid lead halide perovskites have attracted considerable research interest for its superior photovoltaic properties such as excellent carrier transport, high absorption coefficients, long electron and hole diffusion length [1-3]. Depending on these superior material properties, PSCs have achieved great success in the photovoltaics society and their PCE has advanced from  $ 3.8\% $  to over  $ 24\% $  in the past few years. In the structure of PSCs, the ETL is regarded as one of the most important parts which is used to transport and extract photogenerated electrons and simultaneously block holes [4-6]. Accordingly, to obtain high performance PSCs, appropriate ETL is the essential part. Traditionally, the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  is used as the ETL because of the low cost, eco friendliness, ease of fabrication and suitable energy levels. However, the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer suffers from low electron mobility [4], sensitivity to ultraviolet illumination [5] and high temperature-sintering process. Especially, the high-temperature process is accompanied by more time-consuming process and higher energy consumption, which is not expected in commercial products. To solve this problem, low-temperature processed PSCs attract wide attention with efforts focusing on preparing new ETLs.

In comparison with the conventional  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  ETL, the  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  is deemed one of the most competitive materials in PSCs with the excellent electrical qualities of wider bandgap, higher electron mobility and deeper conduction band.[6] And, the outstanding optical feature is another advantage for the  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  ETL used in the photovoltaic devices [7].  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  as the ETL was firstly used in desensitized solar cells (DSSCs), but their corresponding PCE was not as satisfying as  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ -based DSSCs [8-10]. While in the PSCs, the application of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  ETL acquired great success. In 2015, a number of groups selected  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  as ETL in their PSCs around the same time. In Dai's report, mesoporous  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  nanoparticle was used as ETL with the further treatment of  $ \mathrm{TiCl_4} $  in PSCs devices, which acquired the PCE of  $ 10.18\% $  [11]. Ma group combined the condense  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  layer with the mesoporous  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer as the ETL in the PSCs architecture with the efficiency of  $ 7.43\% $  [12]. From then on, the investigation of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  ETL took off and achieved a lot [13-15]. Various methods to fabricate low-temperature  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  samples such as spin

coating deposition [16-18], chemical-bath deposition [14], and atomic layer deposition (ALD) [13] have been reported. In all these methods, spin-coating method is one of the simplest ways. Recently, the planar n-i-p structure PSCs based on the low-temperature spin-coated  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  films have realized a very high performance, which is in touching distance with the highest level PCE [19-20]. However, there are still existing some surface issues between the  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  and perovskite layers due to the interface charge recombination [21]. As many of the  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  ETLs are prepared with low temperature process, there are often numerous trap states existing in the  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  aroused by the oxygen vacancies. The nonideal interface will cause the negative effect on the device performance. Metal ions doping is one common approach to decrease the trap states in  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  and improve the corresponding device efficiency. And lithium (Li), yttrium (Y), and stibium (Sb) have been successfully used to perfect the  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  quality. And, interface engineering [14,19,22] is another active avenue to further improve the device performance. By the interface engineering, ETLs could acquire better performance such as better matching conduction band, higher carrier mobility and less charge accumulation as well as more efficient electron collection. Carbon materials ( $ \mathrm{C}_{60} $ , graphene,) [23-24] with many magical properties have been applied into modify  $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $  or  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  ETL with about  $ 10\% $  improvement in device efficiency [25]. Especially GQDs can not only enhance the conductivity but also intermediate energy levels which acquired more attention in previous reports [23]. Their researches mainly focus on the enhancement electronic properties of  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  ETL [31]. However, the influence of GQDs act on the crystallization of perovskite film was not well studied. Therefore, it is quite necessary to further investigate the action mechanism of GQDs from this angle.

In this work, a combination of  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  and GQDs nano-colloid is adopted to form a superior ETL for high performance PSCs with the planar structure of ITO/ $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $ :GQDs/MAFAPbI $ _3 $ Cl $ _{3-x} $ /spiro-OMeTAD/Ag framework. GQDs act as one new class of carbon nanomaterials have gained much interest for its unique competitive characteristic compared to other semiconductor QDs [26]. In this article, we demonstrate that the introduction of GQDs between  $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  and perovskite can lead to an apparent enhancement of electron extraction and suppression of charge recombination

And the device stability based on the  $  \mathrm{SnO}_2  $ :GQDs ETL is also greatly improved compared to the  $  \mathrm{SnO}_2  $ -only device for the improvement of perovskite film quality.
