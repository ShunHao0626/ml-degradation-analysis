# Low-temperature processed  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  compact layer for efficient mesostructure perovskite solar cells

# HIGHLIGHTS

- Low-temperature processed 70nm cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  device exhibits maximum PCE.

- Champion PSC after  $ \mathrm{SnCl}_4 $  treatment acquires PCE of  $ 15.07\% $ .

# ABSTRACT

$ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  nanoparticle film has been synthesized via low-temperature  $ (\sim 180^{\circ}\mathrm{C}) $  solution-processing and proposed as compact layer in mesostructure perovskite-type solar cell (PSC). Low-temperature processed  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  compact layer  $ (\mathrm{cl - SnO_2}) $  brings perfect crystal-lattice and band-gap matching between electron selective layer and FTO substrate and close interface-contact between  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  and mesoporous  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer  $ (\mathrm{mp - TiO_2}) $ , which contributes to suppressing carrier recombination and optimizing device performance. In varied thickness cells,  $ 70\mathrm{nm} $   $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  device exhibits maximum power conversion efficiency (PCE). In order to further restrain photoelectron recombination and improve the photovoltaic performance, the surface modification of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  by  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  aqueous solution has been carried out. The recombination behavior in the cell interior is greatly retarded via  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment and champion PSC after  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment has acquire PCE of  $ 15.07\% $ , which is higher than PCE of  $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $  based PSC fabricated with same  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  and perovskite procedures  $ (13.3\%) $ . The stability of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  PSC via  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment has also been measured and its PCE reduces to  $ 13.0\% $  after 2 weeks in air.

Keywords:   $  SnO_{2}  $   compact layer; perovskite solar cell; low-temperature processed

# 1. Introduction

Since organometal trihalide perovskite matters (CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbX $ _3 $ , X-Cl, Br and/or I) were first incorporated into dye-sensitized solar cell as visible-light absorb agent in 2009 [1], perovskite-type solar cell (PSC) has intrigued a great deal of research interest due to its unique opto-electrical features such as superb light absorption coefficient (1.5×10 $ ^4 $  cm $ ^{-1} $  at 550 nm), suitable band-gap(~1.55eV), large carrier mobility (~20cm $ ^2 $  V $ ^{-1} $  s $ ^{-1} $ ) and long diffusion length(100-1000nm) [2-9]. So far, the power-conversion-efficiency (PCE) of PSC has been swiftly raised from ~4% to more than 20% within the last seven years [10-12]. There are two typical kinds of cell

architectures in PSCs: mesostructures and planar heterojunctions [12-17]. Planar -geometry cells are easily manufactured at low temperature and have the potential in flexible field, but smooth surface of electron selective layer (ESL) required in this kind of PSCs disables perovskite material uniformly cover the whole surface of ESL and microholes may exist in perovskite layer. These factors would facilitate charge recombination and deteriorate the device's stabilization [18-19]. Mesoporous PSCs commonly employ  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  compact layer ( $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $ ) as hole-blocking layer and mesoporous  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer ( $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $ ) as infiltrate scaffold layer together to constitute electron transporting layer (ETL) [11, 20]. EBIC characterization has suggested that extracting holes is more efficient than drawing electrons form  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ , which is the main reason  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  devices require mesoporous ETL [21]. Otherwise, I-V hysteresis is weakened with the existence of  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  [22]. In fabricating  $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $  and  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  process, high-temperature annealing (above  $ 450^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $ ) is needed to enable organic Titanium precursor decompose to anatase  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  phase and remove -OH residues in  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer, which may cause the decomposition of perovskite [20, 23].  $ \mathrm{Cl - TiO_2} $  after high-temperature annealing has high crystalline quality and difficult to contact tightly with  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $ , which is demonstrated from an obvious dividing line, even crack appearing between  $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $  and  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  in cross-sectional SEM image of  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  PSCs [23-24]. These would make  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  split from FTO substrate and affect the photovoltaic properties of mesoporous PSCs. Seeking a kind of low-temperature processed compact layer is a feasible route to boost the cell PCE and suppress charge recombination.

Recently,   $  SnO_{2}  $   as a promising n-type semiconductor has been employed to PSCs as ESL in either planar or mesostructure type because of its excellent transmittance and high electron mobility [15, 25-29]. Several groups have reported on low temperature processed   $  cl-SnO_{2}  $   (below   $  200^{\circ}C  $  ) and its planar- structure PSCs, which exhibits high efficiency [15, 25]. For example, Ke et.al have presented a planar-type PSC with   $  SnO_{2}  $   ESL, in which   $  SnO_{2}  $   ESL was deposited by spin-coating   $  SnCl_{2}\cdot2H_{2}O  $   ethanol solution and heating at   $  180^{\circ}C  $   1 hour [25]. The average PCE of champion   $  SnO_{2}  $   based cell is 16.02%, which is obtained from the values via both forward and reverse bias

scans. In order to weaken hysteresis behavior caused by a band misalignment in   $  TiO_{2}  $   based planar PSC, Baena et. al have proposed   $  SnO_{2}  $   layer as electron selective contact [15]. This barrier-free charge transport PSC achieved high voltage of 1.19V and exceeding 18% PCE. Ke et.al also have compared the performance of PSCs with low-temperature processed   $  SnO_{2}  $   (LT-  $  SnO_{2}  $  ) and high-temperature processed   $  SnO_{2}  $   (HT-  $  SnO_{2}  $  ) ESLs [29]. The results have shown that LT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   film has better coverage, wider band gap and higher electron mobility than HT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   film, which results in PSCs with LT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   ESLs have better performance than the cells with HT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   ESLs. From above examples, it is seen that LT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   planar PSC is an opportunity for high efficient solar cell and a promise in flexible area. For solving possible low voltage in   $  SnO_{2}  $   Planar PSC caused by deep conductive band edge of   $  SnO_{2}  $ , surface passivation of   $  SnO_{2}/CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   interface was carried out to maintain   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   crystallinity. For example, Song et. al fabricated a low temperature planar PSC, in which   $  SnO_{2}  $   ESL was prepared by spin-coating   $  SnO_{2}  $   paste [28]. Meanwhile, the photovoltaic performance of the PSCs has been further optimized by solvent vapor annealing. The final PSC has achieved PCE of 13% and it even can be durable exceed 30 days in air ambient environment. Complex manufacturing process is acquired to ameliorate the crystallinity of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   material and PSC stability. In this respond,   $  SnO_{2}  $   based mesostructure PSCs have some attempts. For example, Dong et. al have demonstrated   $  SnO_{2}  $   based mesoporous PSC, in which together HT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   and mp-  $  TiO_{2}  $   are introduced as ESL[26]. The PCEs are 7.43% for reverse voltage scan and 5.91% for forward voltage scan, respectively. Mesoporous   $  SnO_{2}  $   film has been proposed as electron transporting and scaffold layer to replace the traditional mp-  $  TiO_{2}  $   in mesoporous PSC and this cell via   $  TiCl_{4}  $   treatment has PCE of 10.18% [27]. HT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   based mesostructure PSCs still show low efficiency [26-27]. Above all, combining a LT-  $  SnO_{2}  $   with mp-  $  TiO_{2}  $   may be a viable avenue to improve PCE for cl-  $  SnO_{2}  $   based PSCs.

Herein,  $ \mathrm{LT - SnO_2} $  based mesostructure PSCs have been prepared and the effects of different  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  thickness on surface morphology and PSC performance have been investigated. To further improve the cell's performance and its stability, surface

# 2. Experimental section

# 2.1 Fabrication of samples

FTO glass substrates (  $  \sim14\Omega/sq.  $  ) were ultrasonic cleaned in deionized water, acetone and ethanol for 10 min, respectively, blow-dried by   $  N_{2}  $   flow, and treated with UV-ozone for 15 min for preparing cl-   $  SnO_{2}  $  .

$ \mathrm{Cl - SnO_2} $  was prepared by spin-coating precursor solution of  $ 0.1\mathrm{mol} / \mathrm{L} $   $ \mathrm{SnCl}_2\cdot 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} $  in ethanol by 2000rpm at 30s on FTO substrates. The thickness of  $ \mathrm{Cl - SnO_2} $  was manipulated by adjusting spin-coating times. The film was heated at  $ 180^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $  for 60min on hot plate. As for  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment, the FTO substrate with  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  has a bath in 0.04M aqueous solution of  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  for 30 min at  $ 70^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $ .

As comparison,   $  cl-TiO_{2}  $   was spin-coated on FTO substrate using a solution of titanium (diisopropoxide) bis(2,4-pentanedionate) (75% in isopropanol) in ethanol at 1:9 volume ratio, and dried at   $  450^{\circ}C  $   for 30min on hot plate.

Mp-  $  TiO_{2}  $  film was deposited on compact layer according to previous report [20, 24]. Before fabrication of the solar cells, the films were heat-treated at 450°C for 30 min. Perovskite  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $  and spiro-OMeTAD hole-transporting material (HTM) were deposited onto the mp-  $  TiO_{2}  $  layer following literature report [14, 24]. High concentration of  $  PbI_{2}  $  (1M) and  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $  (30mg ml  $  ^{-1}  $ ) can ensure large infiltration of perovskite material and maintain complete conversion from  $  PbI_{2}  $  to perovskite  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $  [4, 20]. Finally, 60 nm-thick Au layer was thermal-evaporated at 0.5 Å/s rate. Except for Au evaporation, the procedures of device fabrication were almost completed in air ambient.

# 3. Results and discussion

The schematic diagram of mesoporous PSC with  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  is shown in Fig.1a, from bottom to top including FTO/compact layer/mp+CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _3 $ /CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _3 $ /spiro-OMETAD/Au, which is similar with the previous mentioned structure of mp-TiO $ _2 $  architectures [20, 24, 30]. The practical cross-sectional SEM image of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  PSC is also exhibited in Fig. 1b. From Fig. 1b, it can obviously been seen that  $ \sim 70\mathrm{nm} $  cl-SnO $ _2 $  adheres well to FTO surface,  $ \sim 300\mathrm{nm} $  mp-TiO $ _2 $ +CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _3 $  layer and  $ \sim 300\mathrm{nm} $  CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ PbI $ _3 $  overlayer are spread over SnO $ _2 $  compact layer in sequence,  $ \sim 150\mathrm{nm} $  spiro-OMETAD and  $ \sim 60\mathrm{nm} $  Au are on the top of the device. The well contact interface between  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  and FTO is due to their same SnO $ _2 $  crystal lattice. Dense solutions of PbI $ _2 $  (1M) and CH $ _3 $ NH $ _3 $ I (30mg ml $ ^{-1} $ ) can guarantee a large amount of absorber capacity and high perovskite crystalline quality [4]. The thickness  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  is calculated using a stylus profiler instrument (KLA-Tencor XP-1) by averaging the thickness of seven different locations in the same sample. The thickness of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  is about 40nm, 70nm and 90nm during spin coating once, twice and thrice, respectively.

Current density-voltage (J-V) characteristics of the best-performance PSCs with different thickness  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  are plotted in Fig. 2a. The inset is SEM image (top view) of the surface of  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  layer. We can see that the  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  grains almost completely cover on the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2 / CH_3NH_3PbI_3} $  layer and the size of  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  grains is about  $ 150 - 250\mathrm{nm} $ . Both the grain size distribution (in Fig.2a) and the thickness of  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  layer (in Fig.1b) are in the range of the best distribution for efficient PSC [31-32]. The Best and average photovoltaic parameters are listed in table 1. From Fig. 2a, we derive short-circuit current density (Jsc), open-circuit voltage (Voc) and

IPCE of PSC champions with varying cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  thickness is presented in Fig.2b. PSCs are obviously observed to display maximum IPCE in the visible region (350-750nm) and IPCE values are largely improved with increasing cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  thickness. The calculated Jsc values are  $ 15.6\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $ ,  $ 19.0\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $  and  $ 21.1\mathrm{mAcm}^{-2} $  for  $ 40\mathrm{nm} $ ,  $ 70\mathrm{nm} $  and  $ 90\mathrm{nm} $  cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  cells, which is coincidence with the measured Jsc values. Statistical distribution of photovoltaic parameters is shown in Fig. 3. From these figures, it can be found that Jsc increase and Voc and FF decrease with increasing cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  thickness, which results in maximum PCE obtained for  $ 70\mathrm{nm} $  cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  cell.

To further explore the effect of the  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  thickness on the surface morphology and PSC performance, we make a SEM observation in Fig.4 for  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  layer. The  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  with  $ 70\mathrm{nm} $  duplicates the surface morphology of FTO substrate and almost completely covers on conductive substrate, which suggests that  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  has tight interface-contact with FTO substrate and effective blocking FTO and perovskite layer. The  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  with  $ 40\mathrm{nm} $  covers incompletely the FTO surface and some large FTO grains are exposed on the outside and the surface of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  with  $ 90\mathrm{nm} $  is smooth and obviously has pinholes, which would facilitate photocurrent recombination. From SEM images of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  surface,  $ 70\mathrm{nm} $   $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  is the best candidate as hole-blocking layer in  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  PSCs.

Electrochemical impedance spectrum (EIS) is employed to investigate the effect of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  thickness on device's performance. Representative EIS spectra (Nyquist plots) for  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  PSCs are depicted at  $ 0.7\mathrm{V} $  bias under dark condition in Fig. 5a. The inset is a simple equivalent circuit. The fitting results include two irregular arcs, where the first smaller arc at high frequency is related to spiro-OMeTAD/Au interface and the second larger arc at middle frequency is owing to the combination of cell interior characterized with recombination resistance  $ (\mathbb{R}_{\mathrm{c}}) $  and chemical capacitance (C1) [26, 33-34]. At  $ 0.7\mathrm{V} $  bias,  $ \mathbb{R}_{\mathrm{c}} $  values are ranked in the order:  $ 70\mathrm{nm} > 90\mathrm{nm} > 40\mathrm{nm} $ , which

indicates that the recombination process in the 70nm is restrained. Thus, the 70nm PSC represents a higher Voc due to lower carrier recombination.

Fig.5b describes the transmittance spectra of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  with different thickness. The transmittances of  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  are slightly stronger than that of FTO substrate, which is owing to the antireflection of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  nanoparticle film. The transmittance of  $ 40\mathrm{nm} $   $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  has maximum value in the visible region. The transmittance is weaker with increasing the  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  thickness, which is the result of the number of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  nanoparticles increasing and scattering strengthening with thickness increasing.

High transmittance and high recombination for 40nm based cell leads to its lowest Jsc of 15.53 mA cm  $  ^{-2}  $  at average. The decrease of Rc for 40nm cell may blame to incompletely coverage on FTO surface. 70nm and 90nm thickness cl-SnO  $  _{2}  $  may cover whole FTO surface, which leads to a good barrier between lighting absorb agent and FTO substrate. However, the SnO  $  _{2}  $  nanoparticles would grow and some pinhole would appear with cl-SnO  $  _{2}  $  thickness increasing, which is responsible for high photocurrent recombination and low FF and Voc for 90nm cell. These quite agree with the SEM results (Fig.4).

XRD characterization is taken to analyze the crystal structure of   $  SnO_{2}  $   via heat-proceeding. In consideration of conductive substrate contain tin element, the   $  SnO_{2}  $   film is deposited onto Si (100) substrate for XRD measurement. As shown in Fig 6a,   $  SnO_{2}  $   film is poor crystallinity during   $  180^{\circ}C  $   thermal annealing and the sample is tetragonal   $  SnO_{2}  $   crystal after   $  450^{\circ}C  $   sintering process. The characteristic peaks are compared to standard tetragonal   $  SnO_{2}  $   (JCPDS No.41-1445). X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) result shown in Fig.6b further indicate that   $  cl-SnO_{2}  $   via   $  180^{\circ}C  $   annealing is   $  SnO_{2}  $  . From Fig.6b, Sn   $  3d_{5/2}  $   and Sn   $  3d_{3/2}  $   peaks at binding energy of 487.0 eV and 495.4 eV can be observed, respectively, which is in accord with the Sn3d peaks in the literature [25].

For the  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  cell using  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  nanoparticle film as compact layer, we found that the performance of the device is unsatisfied, which exists the recombination caused by the pinholes at  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  surface. In order to make up the pinholes, we propose to optimize the surface via  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  aqueous solution.  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment has been proposed

to modify the substrate in dye-sensitized solar cells for boosting the device performance [35-36]. There is no relevant report on  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment for PSCs and similar  $ \mathrm{TiCl_4} $  treatment can greatly improve the solar cell performance and inhibit photocurrent recombination [27, 37]. Photovoltaic performances of  $ 70\mathrm{nm} $  cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  devices with and without treatment by  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  are described in Fig.7a and Table 2. After treating in  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  solution, PCE of cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  cell is substantially improved from  $ 12.23\% $  to  $ 15.07\% $  ( $ 23.2\% $  increment), which is mainly due to significant increase in FF from  $ 59.9\% $  to  $ 65.8\% $ . As comparison, PSCs with  $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $  compact layer (cl- $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $ ) are fabricated with same sequential deposition method and mp- $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $  layer. PCE of cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  device via  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treatment ( $ \mathrm{SnCl - SnO_2} $ ) is higher than PCE of cl- $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $  cell. SEM images of cl- $ \mathrm{SnO}_2 $  before and after  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  treating are shown in Fig. 8. From Fig. 8, it is observed that there are more nanoparticles exist on the surface and the film is more compact after  $ \mathrm{SnCl_4} $  modification.

Finally, the stability of  $ \mathrm{SnCl - SnO_2} $  and  $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $  cell placed in air ambient environment is preliminarily examined. Fig.9 shows the durability result of the devices under air ambient. The PCEs of PSCs with  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  and  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  compact layer decrease significant in air ambient within 2 weeks. However, the  $ \mathrm{SnCl - SnO_2} $  cell is more stable than the  $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $  device. This slight durability of  $ \mathrm{SnCl - SnO_2} $  PSC may originate from the better crystal match with  $ \mathrm{mp - TiO_2} $  and the weaker hygroscopicity of low-temperature  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  than  $ \mathrm{cl - TiO_2} $  [28]. In addition, the defects such as oxygen vacancies existed in the surface of nanoparticle film caused by solution process would induce decomposition of perovskite material and reduce the stability of photovoltaic devices [37]. In the present research, spiro-OMeTAD is commonly used as the hole selective and electron blocking material. The durability of cells is deteriorated by spiro-OMeTAD.

# Conclusion

In summary, a low-temperature processed  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  nanoparticle film has been introduced as compact layer in PSC. High transmittance of  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $  film and good band-gap match enable  $ \mathrm{cl - SnO_2} $  PSC have excellent light harvesting and repressed

recombination. 70nm cl-  $  SnO_{2}  $  PSC acquires larger PCE than 40nm and 90nm based cells. Surface modification of cl-  $  SnO_{2}  $  cell by  $  SnCl_{4}  $  aqueous solution is an effective way to inhibit charge recombination and boost cell PCE. The highest PCE of cl-  $  SnO_{2}  $  cell via  $  SnCl_{4}  $  treatment is 15.07%, which is higher than PCE of cl-  $  TiO_{2}  $  device fabricated with same mp-  $  TiO_{2}  $  layer and sequence deposition. The slight stability of  $  SnCl-SnO_{2}  $  cell than cl-  $  TiO_{2}  $  device may be owing to better crystal match and weaker hygroscopicity of the SnCl-  $  SnO_{2}  $  film than cl-  $  TiO_{2}  $ .
