# Hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  spheres as mesoporous layer for better efficiency and stability of perovskite solar cells

Yeming Mei  $  ^{a,b}  $  , Hongli Liu  $  ^{a,b}  $  , Xianggao Li  $  ^{a,b}  $  , Shirong Wang  $  ^{a,b,*}  $

a School of Chemical Engineering and Technology, Tianjin University, Tianjin 300354, China.

b Collaborative Innovation Center of Chemical Science and Engineering (Tianjin), Tianjin 300072, China.

ABSTRACT: Hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  spheres were synthesized by template method, with the diameters was  $ 200~\mathrm{nm} $  and the thickness of shell about  $ 30~\mathrm{nm} $ . The high-quality perovskite films fabricated on the hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  mesoporous layer demonstrate smooth surface and large grain size, resulting in the better scattering light ability and fewer defects. The efficiency of the perovskite solar cells prepared on hollow mesoporous layer can reach  $ 17.60\% $ , which is an approximately  $ 14\% $  higher than the PCE of conventional mesoporous layer devices under the same condition. Importantly, the solar cells with hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  can exhibit better stability than the cells with mesoporous  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  after aging 30 days in  $ 65\% $  relative humidity under dark conditions. The results indicate that the hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  mesoporous layer will be a candidate for better efficient and stable  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  perovskite solar cells, and this structure provides a direction for the design of novel mesoporous layers for perovskite solar cells in future.

# 1. Introduction

Organic-inorganic halide perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have attracted more and more attention because of simple structure and low process cost, as well as good

photovoltaic performance[1-4]. Over past several years, the power conversion efficiency (PCE) has increased to  $ 25.2\% $  rapidly [5-8]. Up to now, the most widely used architecture of PSCs is mainly made up of conventional mesoporous n-i-p configurations, where the electron transport layer (ETL) mainly consists of n-type mental oxides, such as  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ ,  $ \mathrm{Al_2O_3} $ ,  $ \mathrm{ZrO_2} $ ,  $ \mathrm{In_2S_3} $ ,  $ \mathrm{SnO_2} $ ,  $ \mathrm{Nb_2O_5} $  and  $ \mathrm{WO_X} $  nanoparticles [9-18]. Among them,  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ , which was first used as ETL in dye-sensitized solar cells (DSSCs) [19,20], it has been widely used in perovskite solar cells to transport electrons due to its excellent optical properties. Mesoporous  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer plays an important role in facilitating extraction by the improvement of interface contact between the perovskite layer and the electron transport layer [21-26]. The size and crystal of the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  are crucial in determining PSCs performances. Controlling these features of the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  not only improve the perovskite infiltration contact between the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  mesoporous layer and perovskite light absorbing layer, but also affect the charge transfer dynamics at the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ /perovskite interface [27-30].

The conventional preparation of mesoporous   $  TiO_{2}  $   layers reported in the literatures mainly uses titanium dioxide nanoparticles as precursors, then dispersed in solvents for films deposition by spin-coating method [31,32]. The mesoporous   $  TiO_{2}  $   nanoparticles prepared through this method always demonstrate 18-30 nm in shape, along with irregular in shape, which is disadvantage to light scattering and absorption. However, too large solid particle mesoporous layer can induce the reduction of surface area, which is disadvantage for the deposition of film. In addition, the PCE the efficiency is also significantly reduced, respectively. Therefore, novel   $  TiO_{2}  $   with suitable nanoparticle size and outstanding charge extraction ability is essential for better efficiency and stability of PSCs [33–37].

In this article, hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   microsphere films as mesoporous layer to obtain for better efficiency and stability of PSCs. The hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   was synthesized by template method, the diameter of hollow microspheres was 200 nm. Compared to the control film, the better optical performance can be improved due to the light scattering ability and deduction of defect density. In addition, the PCE based on hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer is 17.60%, which is 14% higher than control   $  TiO_{2}  $   mesoporous layer. Meanwhile, the devices exhibit excellent reproduction and less hysteresis. Moreover, compared with control devices, PSCs based on

# 2. Experimental

# 2.1.Materials

FTO substrates was obtained from Nippon Sheet Glass Co., PbI $ _2 $ , spiro-OMeTAD was obtained from Xi'an Polymer Light Technology Corp. PVP was purchased from Aladdin.  $ N $ -butanol and dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO),  $ N $ ,  $ N $ -dimethylformamide (DMF) and chlorobenzene (CB) were purchased from J&K chemical Corp. PMMA polymer was laboratory self-made. All the materials were used as received without any other purification unless other-wise specified.

# 2.2. Synthesis of hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   microspheres

The hollow microspheres were prepared by template method [38]. The process can generally described as below: First, the PMMA polymer template was prepared and the polymer template was dispersed in the solution of absolute alcohol. The core-shell structure was prepared by using butyl titanate as precursor, it separated by centrifugation and freeze-dried to obtain hollow spheres. Hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  spheres were prepared after template removal by calcination at  $ 500^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $ . The prepared hollow microspheres were mixed with ethylcellulose and terpineol in a certain proportion to prepare mesoporous paste. The detailed preparation process is described in support information.

# 2.3. Synthesis of methylammonium iodide (CH₃NH₃I) and perovskite solution

15 mL HI solution (57 wt% in water) was reacted with 16 mL of methylamine (40 wt% in water) in a 100 mL round flask at 0 °C stirring for 5 h. Rotary evaporator was used to remove the solvent and obtain the precipitate, followed by washing several times with diethyl ether and drying in a vacuum oven for 20 h to obtain the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $  .

The   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   perovskite solution was prepared by dissolving MAI and   $  PbI_{2}  $   in the mixed solvent of dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO)/N, N-dimethylformamide (DMF), the volume ratio of DMSO/DMF is 1:9, the 1.5 M solution was stirred for 4 hours. The resulted solution was filtered with a polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) filter with 0.45   $  \mu  $  m pore size before use.

# 2.4. Solar cells fabrication

FTO glass electrodes were washed through a sonication method with detergent acetone, isopropanol, and water for 30 min, separately. The compact layer spin-coated on FTO with   $  TiO_{2}  $   solution at 2000 rpm for 30 s, and then annealed at 500 °C for 30 min in ambient air. A mesoporous layer was deposited using spin coating D18-NRT with ethanol (1:5.5, weight ratio) made the   $  TiO_{2}  $   nanoparticle paste, the hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   spheres paste diluted in ethanol (1:5, weight ratio) was spin-coated on the compact layer. Fig. 1 shows schematic of the device of the different   $  TiO_{2}  $   mesoporous layer of PSCs. The perovskite films were deposited by spin-coating at 2000 rpm for 10 s, then followed 5000 rpm for 30 s, during the process of 5000 rpm high speed, 180 μL amount of chlorobenzene as anti-solvent was dried onto the substrate then annealed at 100 °C for 30 min in ambient air to form   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   perovskite films [39,40]. The hole-transporting layer was spin-coated using 1 mL chlorobenzene solution including 72 mg of spiro-OMeTAD, 27 μL of tert-butylpyridine (TBP), and 17.5 μL of 520 mg mL  $  ^{-1}  $   lithium bis(trifluoromethylsulphonyl)imide acetonitrile solution. The spiro-OMeTAD hole-transporting layer was deposited by spin-coating at 3000 rpm for 30 s. Finally, an Au electrode was obtained by thermal evaporation.

# 2.5. Characterization

TEM images were collected using JEM-2100F with an accelerating voltage of 200 kV. The image of perovskite films and cross section of PSCs were characterized with a scanning electron microscope (SEM S4800). XRD patterns were recorded on the Mini Flex 600. Ultraviolet–visible (UV-Vis) absorbance spectra were obtained with a Thermo Visible-spectrometer (Evolution 300UV). Steady-state photoluminescence (PL) spectrum

# 3.1 Characteristics of hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  sphere

As shown in Fig. 2(a-b), the TEM images of synthesized hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  spheres, the obtained Hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  spheres have uniform size distribution with an average diameter of about  $ 200\pm 10~\mathrm{nm} $ . Meanwhile, in Fig. 2(c-d), SEM images of synthesized hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  spheres, the surface of hollow microspheres is composed of many  $ 10\sim 15 $  nm nanoparticles. The breakage of some microspheres in the SEM image further confirmed the hollow structure. This kind of structure can increase light scattering and provide longer light path length, it helps to improve optical beneficial for perovskite applications.

To evaluate the efficiency of charge injection from perovskite to  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  and the influence of the hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  mesoporous, steady-state was presented in Fig. 3(c). the  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  layer in contact with different layers showing different PL intensities, for perovskite thin films based on hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ , the PL intensity was reduced more obviously than that base on common  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ . The order of  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 > \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ -Control  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} > \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ -Hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ , the ability of electron extracting is in reverse order. Due to the existence of more recombination resistance, the strongest PL quenching can be observed in  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ -Hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ , it suggesting that hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  can extract charge carrier more efficiently.

Meanwhile, time-resolved photoluminescence (TRPL) was shown in Fig 3(d). According to the formula,  $  \mathrm{Y = A_1\exp(-t / \tau_1) + A_2\exp(-t / \tau_2) + y_0}  $ ;  $  \tau = \tau_{1} + \tau_{2}  $ ;  $  \mathrm{A_1}  $  and  $  \mathrm{A_2}  $  are relative amplitudes, while  $  \tau_{1}  $  and  $  \tau_{2}  $  represent the lifetimes of the fast and slow recombination, respectively [42]. The PSC devices show that the fastest TRPL decay occurs in the PSC device based on Hollow  $  \mathrm{TiO_2}  $ . The film with hollow  $  \mathrm{TiO_2}  $  shows phase lifetime of 51.16 ns, while the perovskite with nanoparticle exhibits phase lifetime of 74.61 ns. The observation that the TRPL decay becomes faster based on hollow  $  \mathrm{TiO_2}  $ . It indicates that hollow  $  \mathrm{TiO_2}  $  mesoporous is more advantageous over made of nanoparticle to achieve efficient electron injection from perovskite to  $  \mathrm{TiO_2}  $ .

The electrochemical measurements were carried out with an electrochemical workstation (Fig.S1). Nyquist plots for perovskite solar cell were measured in dark condition. The recombination resistance  $ (\mathbb{R}_{\mathrm{rec}}) $  in simplified equivalent circuit reflects the charge recombination. It could be observed that the PSC based on hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  layer exhibits the larger semicircle, the higher  $ \mathbb{R}_{\mathrm{rec}} $  means the lower recombination rate arising from the high-quality films of the  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ .

Meanwhile, the hollow mesoporous surface reduces the loss electron recombination, resulting in the greater  $ J_{sc} $  and FF.

Hysteresis phenomenon can usually observe in the perovskite solar cell due to defect state, ion transport, interface, and many other factors. For the device with common  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  mesoporous layer, the reverse and forward value is relative different. In contrast, the devices based on hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  mesoporous layer show less hysteresis, which can be due to the decrease of spontaneous radiative recombination between trap.

The reproducibility of the solar cells is also carried out by characterizing about 10 cells. The cells demonstrate excellent reproducibility by the narrow statistical distribution shown in Fig. 5(b). Thus, it was an effective path to produce highly efficient.

Long-term stability is essential for development of PSCs. In order to investigate the influence of different mesoporous layer on the stability, the corresponding device stability was carried out at room temperature in ambient environment. As shown in Fig.6, the XRD peaks located at  $ 13.2^{\circ} $  assigned the  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $ , the intensity of the peak can represent the ratio between the peak correspond to the  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  and the peak of the perovskite. In the film with control  $ \mathrm{TiO}_2 $  layer, the intensity of the peak of  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  gradually increased and finally higher than reference peak located at  $ 14.6^{\circ} $ . # 4. Conclusions

In this paper, hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   microspheres were successfully prepared and then used as mesoporous layer for perovskite solar cells. This novel mesoporous layer can help to obtain high-quality perovskite thin film with less pinhole, along with large grains. The enhanced charge carrier extraction with reduced trap density can explain the improved PCE for the hollow mesoporous based perovskite solar cells.

As a result, the champion perovskite solar cells based on hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   show a PCE of

17.60%, which is 14% higher than that based on control   $  TiO_{2}  $   mesoporous layer. At the same time, the devices exhibit excellent reproducibility and less hysteresis. Moreover, the devices based on hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   layer demonstrate better stability compared to control devices when aged under ambient air of   $  65\pm5\%  $   relative humidity. It will be a potential material for perovskite solar cell.

# Highlights

1. Hollow   $  TiO_{2}  $   synthesized by template method shows diameters about 200 nm, along with shell thickness about 30 nm.

2. The perovskite films fabricated on the hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  demonstrate good morphology and crystallinity, resulting in better scattering light ability and fewer defects.

3. PCE of cells based on hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  can reach  $ 17.60\% $ , which is  $ 14\% $  higher than that based on conventional  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ .

4. The PSCs based on hollow  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  demonstrate better stability under ambient atmosphere with a relative humidity of  $ 65\pm 5\% $  for 30 days.
