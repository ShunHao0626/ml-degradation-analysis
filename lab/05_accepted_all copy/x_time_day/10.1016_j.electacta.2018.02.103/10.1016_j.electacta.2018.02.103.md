# ABSTRACT

Poor long-term stability of perovskite solar cells (PSCs) in ambient air has hindered the PSC commercialization. In this work, we introduce an ionic liquid (IL) of 1-ethyl-3-methylimidazolium hexafluorophosphate ([EMIM]PF $ _6 $ ) as an inter-layer between the ZnO and perovskite layers. The devices are fabricated in air without any atmosphere controlling. Solar cell with a power conversion efficiency of  $ 13.50\% $  is obtained, and a small  $ J-V $  hysteresis is accomplished. More importantly, after 90 days ( $ >2000\mathrm{h} $ ) storage in air, our device without encapsulation retains  $ 88.17\% $  of its initial efficiency. Our work provides a simple valid strategy to develop air-processed, stable and efficient PSCs for future applications.

# 1. Introduction

Since Kojima and co-workers first reported perovskite hybrid sensitized solar cells in 2009, perovskite solar cells (PSCs) have received worldwide attention because of their excellent photovoltaic performance and low-cost manufacture [1, 2]. Simple processability and long-term stability are essential properties to accelerate the practical application of PSCs [3]. In device architectures, electron-transport layer (ETL) plays a key role in achieving efficient and stable PSCs. Nowadays, as a kind of classical n-type inorganic semiconductors,  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  has been widely devoted to the ETL in most state-of-the-art PSCs [4-6]. However, in general, the high-crystalline  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  has to be obtained through the  $ 400 - 500^{\circ}\mathrm{C} $  high temperature annealing process, which increases the cost and limits its further application [7-11]. To avoid high temperature annealing process, the ZnO deposited by low-temperature spin coating has been introduced into the PSCs as the ETL [12-15]. Moreover, theoretically, it is well accepted that the n-type ZnO semiconductor possesses higher electron mobility in comparison with the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  [16, 17]. Considering above advantages, the ZnO is expected to be a desirable alternative of  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $  to achieve the extraction of photo-generated electrons while avoiding the high temperature annealing process. Since it was first reported, many sequential efforts have been done to develop the

PSCs based on ZnO ETL [18-21]. Despite some promising results, severe decomposition of perovskite films onto the ZnO substrates has been revealed due to the proton transfer of methylammonium cation caused by the nature of ZnO surface [22]. In addition, this degradation process of perovskite can be accelerated by hydroxyl groups or the residual chemicals on the ZnO surface [22, 23]. To improve the ZnO ETL and ZnO/perovskite interface, the modified interlayers and composited ETLs have been successively developed, which significantly suppress this decomposition of perovskite films. For example, two-dimensional carbon materials such as graphene, graphene oxide, and reduced graphene oxide have been successfully equipped with the ZnO as composited ETLs to eliminate residual organics and restrain degeneration reactions [24-26]. Zuo and co-workers introduced a 3-aminopropanoic acid monolayer to modify the ZnO/perovskite interface in PSCs, which enhanced the ZnO substrate wettability and electronic coupling between ZnO ETL and perovskite [27]. Cheng et al. added a polymeric buffer layer of poly(ethylenimine) to avoid the physical contact between the ZnO and perovskite layer achieving a best PCE of  $ 10.02\% $  [23]. Moreover, it has been proved that the ZnO ETLs with aluminum doping show superior electron extraction properties in PSCs, on which the perovskite layers exhibit better thermal stability [28-30]. Especially, the ionic liquids (ILs) owning to the remarkable electrochemical and thermal stability, and various functional ligands have been regarded as a promising modifying material to enhance the adhesive effects between layers and modify interface states [31]. Yang

and Zhou et al. employed the IL of 1-butyl-3-methylimidazolium tetrafluoroborate into the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ -based PSCs improving the electron transport and suppressing ion migration [32]. The group of Yang also demonstrated that the IL of 1-ethyl-3-methylimidazolium hexafluorophosphate could both modify the  $ \mathrm{TiO_2} $ /perovskite interface and play the role of independent ETL [33]. However, to our knowledge, there are fewer works paying attention to the modification effect and mechanism of IL in ZnO-based PSCs.

Although, in the most recent, a PCE record of PSCs is as high as 22.1 %, the long-term environmental stability is still an important issue in PSCs. Unfortunately, the performance and stability of low-temperature PSCs are inferior to that of high-temperature PSCs. In addition, until now, most preparation techniques for high-quality perovskite films still have to rely on a well-controlled glovebox. It is very desirable to propose a new strategy for realizing air-processed efficient PSCs with long-term stability.

Here, we report an effective interface passivation method for long-term stability PSCs. By applying a representative IL of   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}  $   as a ZnO-modifying interlayer, the planar PSC with a structure of FTO/ZnO/  $  [EMIM]PF_{6}/MAPbI_{3}/Spiro-OMeTAD/Au  $   has been fabricated in ambient condition. High-quality perovskite films have been obtained onto the   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}  $   modified ZnO substrates. The champion device yeilds the highest PCE of 13.50 %, which is much better than that of devices without a   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}  $   interlayer (10.08 %).

# 2. Experimental

# 2.1. Reagents and materials

$ \mathrm{Zn(CH_3COO)_2\cdot 2H_2O} $ , KOH, 1-ethyl-3-methylimidazolium hexafluorophosphate ([EMIM]PF6) and all anhydrous solvents were obtained from Aladdin. Lead iodide  $ (\mathrm{PbI}_2) $ , 4-tert-butypyridine and lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (Li-TFSI) were purchased from Kanto. Methylammonium iodide  $ (\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{I}) $  was obtained from Youxuan Trade, 2, 2', 7, 7'-tetrakis-(N,N-di-4-methoxyphenylamino)-9, 9' spirobifluorene (spiro-OMeTAD) was obtained from Toronto. All chemicals and solvents were employed without any further purification.

# 2.2. Solar cell fabrication

The preparation of ZnO precursor solution and two-step sequential solution deposition method are based on our previous works [34, 35]. In typical process, the   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}  $   methyl alcohol solution (5 mg mL  $  ^{-1}  $  ) was spin-coated at 4000 rpm for 20 s on the ZnO film, and annealed at 70 °C for 15 min. Afterwards, the PbI  $  _{2}  $   solution in N, N-dimethylformamide (DMF) (460 mg mL  $  ^{-1}  $  ) was first spin-coated on the ZnO/IL

After cooling to room temperature, the hole transport layer solution containing 76 mg of spiro-OMeTAD, 28.5 μL 4-tert-butypyridine, 17.5 μL Li-TFSI (520 mg of Li-TFSI in 1 mL of acetonitrile) all dissolved in 1 mL chlorobenzene was deposited by spin-coating at 3000 rpm for 30 s. Finally, about 60 nm thick Au counter electrode was deposited via vacuum thermal evaporation at rate of 1.0 Å s  $  ^{-1}  $ . The 0.12 cm  $  ^{2}  $  active area of the PSCs was determined through a non-reflective metal mask. All processes were operated in air (the average temperature was 25 °C, average relative humidity was 47.5 %).

The inefficient interfacial charge transfer and excess electron trap-state density result in charge accumulation, which causes hysteresis. It may be associated with the fact that the IL leads to chemical binding between ZnO and perovskite, which provides an electron transfer bridge, reducing the charge accumulation and hysteresis effect [32]. As discussed above, the long-term air stability of ZnO-based PSCs is attributable to the effective interface passivation effect of IL. In ambient air, moisture in the atmosphere will affect the air stability of PSCs. To achieve a highly chemical stable device, high-quality perovskite film is of crucial importance. The hydrophilicity of IL is helpful to effectively spread the  $ \mathrm{PbI}_2 $  precursor on the IL-passivated ETL (Fig. S2), forming a high-quality  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  film. Relatively flat surface and pinhole-free can take the lead in preventing moisture penetration to suppress the decomposition of perovskite materials, which resulted in long-term air stability of PSCs. On the other hand, ion migration in PSCs may be a crucial factor affecting stability [3]. It is known that mobile ions and charge accumulations within devices are main origin of hysteresis and degradation in PSCs [55]. In our case, the introduction of IL can form a special imidazolium salt at the perovskite interface (Fig. 6), limiting the migration process and charge accumulation between perovskite layer and ETL as well as improving stability and reducing hysteresis [32, 45, 56]. To further enhance the long-term stability, the formation mechanisms and passivation effect of imidazolium salt will need more studies in the future.

# 4. Conclusions

In summary, a high-efficiency and environmentally stable planar PSC has been fabricated in ambient condition by applying   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}-IL  $   as a ZnO-modifying interlayer. The quality of perovskite films has been promoted with a two-step spin-coating method through introducing the   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}-IL  $   onto ZnO ETL as a modification layer. With a device structure of FTO/ZnO/  $  [EMIM]PF_{6}/MAPbI_{3}/Spiro-OMeTAD/Au  $  , the planar PSCs yeilds the highest PCE of 13.50 %. It is much better than the device without a   $  [EMIM]PF_{6}  $   interlayer, which merely reaches the highest PCE of 10.08 %. We demonstrate that the outstanding performance is attributed to the passivation effect of IL. Optimizing choice of a special IL is expected to improve the performance of ZnO-based PSCs.
