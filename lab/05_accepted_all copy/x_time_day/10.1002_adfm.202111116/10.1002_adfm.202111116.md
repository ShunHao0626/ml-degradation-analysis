# In Situ Stabilized   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   for Air-Fabricated Inverted Inorganic Perovskite Photovoltaics with Wide Humidity Operating Window

Sheng Fu, Xiaodong Li, Juanyong Wan, Wenxiao Zhang, Weijie Song, and Junfeng Fang*

cells (PSCs) have rapidly developed with the highest efficiency of over 20.3% and their air fabrication for future low-cost production has been a hot spotlight.  $  ^{[7-12]}  $   However, all the high-efficiency devices are fabricated with the regular structure (n–i–p), while their stability is significantly limited by the Spiro-OMeTAD layer with hygroscopic and thermophobic dopants.  $  ^{[13-16]}  $   In addition, the regular structure is mis-matched with the tandem photovoltaic. In comparison, the inverted (p–i–n) structure can not only avoid the use of undesirable transport layers, but also features a compatible process with the tandem structure.  $  ^{[17-21]}  $   Unfortunately, the inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs are rarely reported and their highest efficiency is only 16.67%, which is far-lagged from the regular. Inverted triiodine cesium lead (CsPbI $ _3 $ ) perovskite solar cells (PSCs) are promising in photovoltaics owing to their ideal light absorption, non-volatile active layer, and avoidance of fragile Spiro-OmeTAD, especially as the top cell in tandem devices. However, they still exhibit far-lagging efficiency, and must be processed in a strictly controlled environment due to water-fearing CsPbI $ _3 $ . Here, a novel strategy to convert the harmful water erosions into an in situ stabilizer for efficient inverted CsPbI $ _3 $  PSCs fabricated with a wide humidity operating window, is proposed. During air fabrication, maleic anhydride (MAAD) can react with water molecules in air to reduce moisture erosions, while the hydrolysis products (maleic acid, MAAC) control grains growth. After annealing, MAAC strongly binds to CsPbI $ _3 $  grains as a shield to hamper phase transition and moisture penetration. A champion efficiency of 19.25% is obtained, which is the highest efficiency among the inverted inorganic PSCs. In parallel, the authors' optimized devices present efficiency of 18.39% even fabricated in relative humidity 60% condition. Moreover, the stability against various ages is improved, and the optimized devices remain at 96.8% of its initial efficiency after maximum power point tracking at 65 °C for 850 h.

As is widely known, the photovoltaic phase of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   is thermodynamically unstable and the lead iodine octahedral   $  [PbI_{6}]^{4-}  $   would spontaneously distort into the non-photovoltaic phase (  $  \delta  $  ) due to the mismatched ionic radius.  $  ^{[4,22]}  $   Water molecules can drive the surface   $  Cs^{+}  $  ions out of their initial lattice sites and accelerate the   $  [PbI_{6}]^{4-}  $   distortion.  $  ^{[19,23]}  $   which results in the undesirable phase transition of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films during their air fabrication. Additionally, the water erosions would leave massive iodine vacancy (  $  V_{1}  $  ) in   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films due to the large solvation enthalpy of halogen anions in water.  $  ^{[21,23]}  $   The   $  V_{1}  $   traps would accelerate the phase transition by reducing the balance ionic bonds and act as the non-radiative recombination to capture carriers. Furthermore, the crystallization of air-deposited   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films would be further deteriorated under the rampant erosion of water molecules.  $  ^{[24]}  $   The above undesirable issues may be the reasons why a strictly controlled humidity is necessary for the reported air-fabricated   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs. Strategies focused on growth adjustment and hydrophobic additives have been developed to ameliorate the air fabrication of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films.  $  ^{[11,19,24-29]}  $   Despite considerable ameliorations, they are all obtained based on the regular structure and need strictly control the relative humidity (RH). Here, we propose a novel strategy to turn the harmful water erosions into an in situ stabilizing facilitator to fabricate high-performance inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs even in the high-humidity (RH 60%) ambient condition through incorporation of maleic anhydride (MAAD). As illuminated in Figure 1a, MAAD molecules can consume water molecules in air through the hydrolysis reaction during the films deposition. In addition, the hydrolysis product of maleic acid (MAAC) owns two carboxylic groups, which can control crystal growth with effective passivation, and hamper   $  [PbI_{6}]^{4-}  $   distortion though the strong Lewis acid-based interaction toward   $  CsPbI_{3}  $  .  $  ^{[30-32]}  $   Density functional theory (DFT) calculations further verify that the MAAC molecule is more strongly absorbed on   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   surface than water molecules, which builds a solid protection from moisture erosion during the air process. The resulted films feature stable photovoltaic phase (  $  \beta  $   phase) with promoted photoelectric properties. Encouragingly, we realize a champion efficiency of 19.25%, which is the first time that the efficiency of inverted inorganic PSCs is comparable to that of regular ones (Table S1, Supporting Information). The optimized devices feature wide humidity operating widows and show champion efficiency of 18.37% even fabricated in RH 60% condition. Apart from high efficiency, the resulting PSCs exhibit good stability, maintaining almost 100% of initial efficiency in the glovebox for 100 d and 89.8% after storage in RH 10% for 1000 h. What's more, the devices reveal excellent operational stability under a high temperature (65 °C) and maintain 96.8% after maximum power points (MPP) tracking for 850 h while the control ones (ref) almost degrade completely within 450 h.

# 2. Results and Discussion

As is widely known, the moisture sensitive MAAD can consume water molecules and be hydrolyzed into MAAC (Figure S1a, Supporting Information). The PH test paper dripped with MAAD solution reveals the acidification and rapidly turns into red when exposed to RH 40% ambient within 4 min. In addition, the PH paper with MAAD powders reveals the red in humidity condition (Figure S1d and Video S1, Supporting Information) within 7 min and presents no change even in glovebox for 2 h. To observe the molecule change, the H¹NMR spectra (Figure S1c, Supporting Information) are conducted on the aged MAAD powders. It is in good agreement with the hydrolysis that the peak at 7.45 ppm of MAAD is gradually transferred into the peak of MAAC at 6.26 ppm when exposed to the humidity condition. The two carboxylic groups on MAAC molecules can strongly interact with CsPbI₃ and improve the crystallization, trap passivation, and phase stability.[19,21] We conduct the hydrolyzed MAAD and MAAD doped CsPbI₃ films deposited in air (Figure S2, Supporting Information). In comparison with the hydrolysis MAAD, the peak of  $  \nu_{\text{C=O}}  $  is shifted to 1697 cm⁻¹ from the initial 1710 cm⁻¹, and the difference ( $  \Delta \nu_{-\text{COO-}}  $ ) between  $  \nu_{\text{as}}  $  and  $  \nu_{\text{s}}  $  of carboxyl groups is decreased from 135 to 72 cm⁻¹ after incorporating into CsPbI₃ films. It indicates that the MAAC is non-volatile and have been salinized after high-temperature annealing, which would gift the strong ionic bonds along with the coordination bonds to CsPbI₃. The interaction between MAAC and CsPbI₃ is further investigated by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, Figure S3, Supporting Information). In addition, the theoretical simulations from density functional theory (DFT) calculations (Figure S4, Supporting Information) point out that MAAC molecules feature a larger adsorption energy ( $  \Delta E_{ad}  $ ) of -0.72 eV on the CsPbI $ _3 $  surface than water molecules (-0.55 eV). The salinized carboxyl groups would gift more secure adsorption strength on the surface with ionic bonds, which would build solid shield to insulate moisture erosions during the air process. In addition, the hydrophobic groups of MAAC molecules would be an outward orientation on CsPbI $ _3 $  grains to protect grain boundaries (GBs) from moisture erosion. $ ^{[34]} $  The high resolution transmission electron microscope (HR-TEM) image (Figure S5, Supporting Information) of the MAAD doped CsPbI $ _3 $  exhibits the uniform amorphous at the GBs for solid protection. In good consistency, the water contact angle measurements reveal that the angle is increased from 40.5° to 71.5° after incorporating MAAD (Figure S6, Supporting Information). As presented in Figure 1b; Figure S7, Supporting Information (XRD patterns of different MAAD dopant concentration), the ref film shows a peak of non-photovoltaic δ phase in the grazing incidence wide-angle X-ray scattering (GIWAXS) image and no δ is observed after incorporating MAAD. It directly reveals that MAAD incorporation can address humidity-induced phase transition of air processed CsPbI $ _3 $  film. Moreover, the doped CsPbI $ _3 $  films display the stronger diffraction intensity of (110) and (220) crystal planes than the ref, which indicates the crystallization promotion of MAAD incorporation. And, 4 mg mL $ ^{-1} $  is the optimized concentration with the highest diffraction intensity among all the samples (Figure S7a, Supporting Information). In addition, when enlarging the peaks of (110) planes, the peaks present the shift toward wide angles after MAAD doping (Figure S7b, Supporting Information), which indicates that the MAAD would induce a compressive strain in the CsPbI $ _3 $  lattice. According to the reports, $ ^{[35-37]} $  the compressive strain could increase the formation energy of V $ _1 $  defects and stabilize the black phase of CsPbI $ _3 $ . The induced-compressive strain by MAAD incorporation would further hinder phase transition for long-term stability. It has been widely proved that CsPbI $ _3 $  perovskite always features poor quality for the larger nuclear energy and uncontrolled crystallization. $ ^{[7,23,38]} $  Meanwhile, the scanning electron microscope (SEM) images of the ref film show inferior quality with non-dense morphology and small grain size (Figure S8a, Supporting Information). Whereas, the morphologies are gradually densified with large grain after MAAD doping (Figure S8b,c, Supporting Information), corresponding to the growth control of MAAD. Besides, the optimized films offer better interface contact and the surface roughness is flattened from the initial 18.6 nm to 9.45 nm. Long-term phase stability is also taken into comparison via accelerated age under RH 30% conditions for 14 d (Figure S9, Supporting Information). As shown in X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns (Figure 1c), the ref film suffers from serious phase transition and shows strong δ phase peaks after aging. In comparison, the MAAD

doped film remains unchanged with pure   $  \beta  $   phase. It proves that the strategy of the in situ stabilizer can significantly improve moisture toleration and phase stability of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films during air fabrication.

The hydrophobia nature of the   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   seriously limits the humidity operating window of the air-fabricated photovoltaics for future low-cost production. The MAAD can consume the water during the air process and form the solid shield in   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films against water erosions, which would expand the humidity operating window. We fabricate the   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films in various conditions (from 0% to 60%) to observe the tolerance of the operating humidity. As shown in Figure 2a, the ref film fabricated in glovebox shows the pure   $  \beta  $   phase and the peak of non-photovoltaic phase is gradually stronger with the RH increase, corresponding to the humidity-caused phase transition.  $  ^{[19]}  $   In sharp contrast (Figure 2b), the MAAD doped films present no phase transition even operating in high humidity (RH 60%). In consistence with XRD results, the morphology of the ref films would be worse and present the enlarged pinholes with the humidity increasing (Figure 2c). Interestingly, the films fabricated from MAAD incorporated precursors are gradually densified with the RH increase and the high humidity (RH 60%) also reveals the better morphology than the ref, which further proves the effectiveness in eliminating water erosion of MAAD incorporation for air fabrication of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $  . The stable photovoltaic phase associating with the dense morphology in a wide humidity operating window, further supports the feasibility of the in situ stabilizing strategy via MAAD incorporation.

Along with the crystallization promotion, salinized MAAC ions with strongly coordinated carboxylate and solid protection from the moisture erosions, can effectively passivate the fragile grain boundaries (GBs) and suppress the formation of water-induced   $  V_{1}  $   defects, which would improve the photoelectric properties with less defects in   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films. An obvious evidence can be found on the Pb 4f XPS spectra (Figure S3c, Supporting Information). The metal lead signals as the deep-energy-level traps in the ref films are removed after MAAD incorporation. We conduct the space charge limited current (SCLC) measurements based on the hole-only structure (Figure 3a inset) and the trap density (  $  N_{t}  $  ) can be simulated with the trap-state filling voltage (  $  V_{TFL}  $  ) in SCLC curves (Figure 3a).  $  ^{[39-41]}  $   The   $  N_{t}  $   is reduced from   $  5.46 \times 10^{15}  $   cm  $  ^{-3}  $   to   $  3.74 \times 10^{15}  $   cm  $  ^{-3}  $   with MAAD incorporation (Table S2, Supporting Information). The photoluminescence (PL) measurements in Figure 3b reveal that the PL intensity of MAAD doped film is double-fold of ref. Similarly, the time-resolved PL (TRPL) spectra in Figure 3c show the prolonged lifetime of photo-generated carriers from the initial 6.25 ns to 18.28 ns with MAAD doping. Thus, the humidity-induced stabilizer can address the inferior crystallization and gift superior carriers transport with less non-radiative recombination for air-fabricated films.

# 3. Conclusion

In summary, we report a novel and effective strategy by in situ stabilizing   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films to address the moisture erosion during air fabrication of   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   films, and realize the high-performance inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs with a wide humidity operating window. During operation in humidity air, MAAD molecules can convert harmful water erosions into a stabilizer to regulate the crystallization and suppress phase transition of the   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   film. As a result, a champion efficiency of 19.25% is realized with high   $  V_{oc}  $   of 1.160 V, which is the first time that the efficiency of inverted inorganic PSCs is comparable to that of regular ones. Whereas, the optimized devices feature high efficiency of 18.39% even fabricated in RH 60% ambient condition. Besides, the target devices show good duration both in glovebox and outside, and even remain at 96.8% after MPP tracking at 65 °C for 850 h. Our work breaks the vacancy of air-fabricated inverted   $  CsPbI_{3}  $   PSCs and provides the novel perspective for air fabrication of the inorganic perovskite photoelectric devices.
