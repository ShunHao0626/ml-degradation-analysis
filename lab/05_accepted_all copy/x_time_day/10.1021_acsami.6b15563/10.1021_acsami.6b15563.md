# Facile Face-down Annealing Triggered Remarkable Texture Development in   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   Films for High-performance Perovskite Solar Cells

ABSTRACT: Herein, we demonstrate that the facile face-down annealing route which effectively confines the evaporation of residual solvent molecules in one-step deposited precursor films can controllably enable the formation of (110) textured  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  films consisted of high-crystallinity, well-ordered, micron-sized grains. And all the grains span vertically the entire film thickness. Such microstructural features make dramatically decreased non-radiative recombination sites as well as greatly improved transport property of charge carriers in the films compared with the non-textured ones obtained by conventional annealing route. As a consequence, the planar-heterojunction perovskite solar cells with these textured  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $  films exhibit the significantly enhanced power conversion efficiency (PCE) along with small hysteresis and excellent stability. The champion cell yields the impressive PCE boosting to  $ 18.64\% $ , and the stabilized value of around  $ 17.22\% $ . Particularly, it can maintain  $ 86\% $  of its initial value after storage of 20 days in ambient with relative humidity of  $ 10 - 20\% $ . Our work suggests a facile and effective route for further boosting the efficiency and stability of low-cost perovskite solar cells.

KEYWORDS:  $ \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{NH}_3\mathrm{PbI}_3 $ , texture development, defects, face-down annealing, perovskite solar cells

# 1. INTRODUCTION

Organolead trihalide perovskite (OTP) materials have recently emerged as promising candidates for photovoltaic devices that are known as perovskite solar cells.  $  ^{1-4}  $   In just few years, the power conversion efficiency (PCE) of wet-processed single-junction cells with the OTP polycrystalline films such as   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   has raised rapidly from 3.8% to over 20%.  $  ^{5,6}  $   This unprecedented progress mainly relies on the inherently excellent optoelectronic features of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films,  $  ^{1,4,7,8}  $   as well as the fundamental research efforts focused on device architecture, interface engineering along with materials and deposition routes for each of functional layers.  $  ^{3,4,8,9}  $   Among all the concerns, effective deposition of pinhole-free and uniform   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films has been recognized as the most critical one that enables reliable cell performance.  $  ^{4,8,10}  $   Currently, additive-free, crystallization-engineered one-step routes including fast deposition-crystallization procedure,  $  ^{11}  $   solvent-engineering technology,  $  ^{12}  $   and Lewis base adduct based approach  $  ^{13}  $   have become more and more popular for solution processing of desired   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films.  $  ^{1,14}  $   For each of them, the recipe of timely drop-casting antisolvent such as toluene or diethyl ether during spinning the precursor solution that contains equimolar   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $   and   $  PbI_{2}  $   in N,N-Dimethylformamide (DMF) or γ-butyrolactone/dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO)) is required, since it can speed the nucleation kinetics or decouple the nucleation and growth processes of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   grains thereby avoid the formation of pinholes in the films.  $  ^{1,14}  $   Because of excessively fast crystallization process, the resultant   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films generally consist of small grains with broad size distribution in range of ~100-500 nm.  $  ^{12,15,16}  $   Such microstructure feature unavoidably leads to high density of crystal imperfections such as grain boundaries and intragranular defects in the films.  $  ^{15,17}  $   They can seriously deteriorate the electrical and optical qualities of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films, in turns strictly limit the PCE of cells.  $  ^{15,17-19}  $   In addition, the anomalous photocurrent density-voltage (J-V) hysteresis in perovskite solar cells that makes accurate efficiency determination challenging also intrinsically links to such crystal imperfections.  $  ^{17,20}  $   In attempting to circumvent these problems, some successful strategies have been exploited such as interface engineering of cells,  $  ^{9,21}  $   passivation of grains boundaries,  $  ^{17,22,23}  $   as well as coarsening grains of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films.  $  ^{16,24,25}  $

For polycrystalline films, equally to grain size, the orientation of crystal axis in each grain is also one of important microstructural features that dominate their physical properties.  $  ^{26}  $   Especially,

the ones with aligned crystal axis are so-called textured films.  $  ^{26,27}  $   They possess a single-crystal-like nature along the crystal axis, and hence an enhancement of physical properties is expected.  $  ^{28}  $   Ordinarily prepared polycrystalline films are composed of grains with random orientation. And, the methodology that is utilized to develop texture to enhance the functional properties of polycrystalline films is known as texture engineering.  $  ^{28,29}  $   Specifically, the one-step deposited   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   polycrystalline films are similarly characterized with randomly oriented grains.  $  ^{10,30-32}  $   Recent studies revealed their tremendous intragranular heterogeneities in photoluminescence (PL),  $  ^{33}  $   cathodoluminescence,  $  ^{34}  $   and cell performance parameters.  $  ^{35,36}  $   Therefore, texture engineering is of particularly important to modify the electrical and optical properties of such   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films, and hence further improve the performance of ultimate cells. Nevertheless, state-of-the-art routes for texture engineering of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films inevitably include complex additives,  $  ^{6,17,31,37-39}  $   sophisticated processing control,  $  ^{40-42}  $   or special substrates.  $  ^{43-45}  $   It is thus of interest to advance more facile route to develop texture in one-step deposited   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films.

Herein, a facile face-down annealing strategy exempting the use of any additives or special substrates is proposed to develop texture in   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films. It involves the deposition of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   precursor films via Lewis base adduct based one-step spin-coating method,  $  ^{13}  $   followed by pre-heating the films face-up on hotplate at controlled temperatures and then turning them face-down during annealing. By it, the (110) textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films composed of high-crystallinity, well-ordered, micron-sized grains can be successfully obtained. And, all the grains vertically penetrate the entire film thickness. The planar-heterojunction perovskite solar cells based on these textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films yield the remarkably improved performance as well as small hysteresis and excellent stability, in contrast to the cells based on the non-textured ones obtained by conventional annealing.

# 2. RESULTS AND DISSCUSION

The face-down annealing route for preparing   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films is illustrated in Figure 1, and described in details in experimental section. Briefly, the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   precursor films composed of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I\cdot PbI_{2}\cdot DMSO  $   complex were firstly coated on compact   $  TiO_{2}/FTO  $   glass substrates by the Lewis base adduct based one-step spin-coating method proposed by Park et al.  $  ^{[13]}  $  . Then, they were placed face-up on hotplate and pre-heated in batches at the temperatures of 23 °C.

Even so, it is still relatively low compared with the one prepared by sequential deposition technique.⁵⁰ In addition, a symmetric, Gaussian distribution of heights can be observed for sample CA, signifying that the film surface has a continuously undulated profile with no specific preferred height.⁴⁷ Yet, the height distribution becomes asymmetric with a dominant distribution coupled with an additional narrow distribution for sample FDA-60, FDA-40, and FDA-RT. As to polycrystalline film, the lower regions represent the grain-boundary grooves, and the higher regions represent the top-most profile of grains.¹⁰,⁴⁷ So, we can infer that the grains growth preferentially selects the certain plane in those films, agreeing the texture development in them. All the above observations provide conclusive evidence that the facile face-down annealing route can successfully realize the (110) textured CH₃NH₃PbI₃ film, and the texture degree, average grain size along with surface roughness increase in sequence for sample FDA-60, FDA-40, and FDA-RT. The formation of such textured CH₃NH₃PbI₃ films mainly attributed to the following two aspects. One the one hand, the DMSO molecules will be confined on the surface of CH₃NH₃PbI₃ film during the initial crystallization stage of the film. As proved in pervious works,¹³,⁵¹ the O atom in DMSO interacts strongly with Pb atoms in CH₃NH₃PbI₃.⁵² A strong interaction of DMSO with CH₃NH₃PbI₃ surface atoms will effectively lower the surface energy of (110) crystal facets, which results in preferred growth of CH₃NH₃PbI₃ grains along [110] direction, namely (110) texture in CH₃NH₃PbI₃ film. This situation is similar to the formation (110) oriented CH₃NH₃PbI₃ film by DMSO solvent annealing.⁵³,⁵⁴ On the other hand; the nucleation sites for the CH₃NH₃PbI₃ grains can be reduced due to the slow evaporation of DMOS molecules, which leads to the growth of large-sized grains in CH₃NH₃PbI₃ film.⁴⁸ The above two aspects account for the formation of textured CH₃NH₃PbI₃ film with large grain size.

The optoelectronic properties of resultant   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films were then investigated. Figure 4(a) gives their UV-vis absorption spectra. It can be seen that all the films possess typical absorption character of tetragonal   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $  .⁵ Compared with sample CA, the absorption onset exhibits slight bathochromic shift for textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films, especially sample FDA-40 and FDA-60. Moreover, the absorption intensity of those samples in the range of 550 nm to onset is also higher than that of sample CA. Such features are probably caused by the high crystallinity of grains ²⁵,³⁸ and strong light scattering associated with a rough surface of the films.⁵⁰ It should be noted that the absorption intensity of sample FDA-RT decreases significantly below 550 nm, which mainly results from the pinholes in the film wherein light just passes without being absorbed.¹¹ To probe the recombination dynamics of photo-generated charge carriers in concerned samples, time-resolved PL measurements were conducted, as given in Figure 4(b). Each of samples was deposited on blank glass substrate, and was photoexcited from the side of glass substrate. The curves were fitted with a bi-exponential decay function. The fast component corresponds to trap-assisted recombination, while slow component is attributed to recombination in the bulk of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   grains.²⁴,³³,⁴⁸ Taking the weighted average of the two components, the lifetime of minority carriers is estimated to be 21, 46, 66, and 75 ns for sample CA, FDA, FDA-40, and FDA-RT, respectively. The prolonged lifetime indicates the decrease of non-radiative recombination sites.⁷,³³,⁴⁸,⁵⁵ Therefore, we can infer that the reduced non-radiative recombination sites can be achieved in textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film, and the density of them decreases in turns for sample FDA-60, FDA-40, and FDA-RT.

Compared with sample CA, the larger current densities at same bias voltages can be measured from sample FDA, indicating its better hole conductivity. Moreover, both of the films show ohmic behavior at low electric fields, followed by a nonlinear increase in current at the bias voltages exceed the trap-filled limit (  $  V_{TFL}  $  ), demonstrating that all the trap states are filled. The   $  V_{TFL}  $   is related to trap density:   $  V_{TFL}=en_{t}L^{2}/(2\varepsilon\varepsilon_{0})  $ ,  $  ^{46}  $   where e represents the elementary charge of the electron, L is the thickness of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film,   $  \varepsilon  $   and   $  \varepsilon_{0}  $   is the relative dielectric constant of   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   (  $  \varepsilon=32  $  ) and vacuum permittivity, and   $  n_{t}  $   is trap density. When operating in the trap-free space charge limit current (SCLC) regime above 2.0 V, the dark current well follows the Mott-Gurney law:   $  J=9\varepsilon\varepsilon_{0}V^{2}/(8L^{3})  $ .  $  ^{46}  $   So, the hole mobility can be estimated to be ~23 and ~49 cm  $  ^{2}  $ /Vs for sample CA and FDA-40, respectively. The better hole conductivity as well as lower trap density and higher mobility suggest the more superior charge transport behavior in sample FDA-40. On the other hand,   $  J_{0}  $   represents the thermal emission rate of electrons from valence band to conduction band in cell's light absorber layer, which is directly proportional to recombination rate of charge carriers.  $  ^{55}  $   The decreased   $  J_{0}  $   for the cell with sample FDA-40 indicates the reduced recombination of charge carriers. So, the charge carrier recombination lifetime under real cell working conditions was further quantified by impedance spectroscopy measurement as the product of recombination resistance and chemical capacitance.  $  ^{38,45}  $   One of Nyquist plots of measured impedance spectra for the cells with sample CA and FDA-40 are given in the insert of Figure 6(d). Such desired characters mainly come from the unique microstructural features of the textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film. As revealed in Figure 2 and Figure 3, the textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film is composed of micron-sized grains with excellent crystallinity and uniformly aligned orientation perpendicular to the substrate. One of the one hand, the out-of-plane order of grains means that the transport and transfer of charge carriers along the direction perpendicular to the substrate are proceeded in the single crystal grains, and do without crossing any grain boundaries.  $  ^{11,38,48}  $   Such peculiarity gives rise to the excellent transport behavior of charge carriers in textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film. On the other hand, the large size and superior crystallinity of grains in textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film signifies the low density of crystal imperfections such as grain boundaries and intragranular defects. It is well acknowledged that the crystal imperfections usually act as trap states and can cause serious non-radiative recombination of charge carriers in polycrystalline film.  $  ^{15,16,19,33}  $   In the present case, the decreased non-radiative recombination sites in textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film can be thus ascribed to the reduced crystal imperfections. And, the decrease of crystal imperfections can partially weaken the ion migration in polycrystalline   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film,  $  ^{17,20}  $   which well explains the suppressed J-V hysteresis in the cell with sample FDA-40. Furthermore, it is also beneficial to prevent the diffusion of water molecules from atmosphere into the film, ultimately boost the stability of the cell with sample FDA-40.  $  ^{18,37,40}  $   In short, the textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film can effectively promote the PCE, suppress the J-V hysteresis, and improve the stability of planar-heterojunction perovskite solar cell, which mainly correlate with the improved transport property of charge carriers and reduced non-radiative recombination sites as results of uniformly aligned orientation, large size and superior crystallinity of grains in the textured film.

In summary, we present a facile face-down annealing route to develop texture in one-step deposited   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films. This annealing route without use of any additives and special substrates can controllably realize the (110) textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films. Such films are composed of micron-sized grains with excellent crystallinity and uniformly aligned orientation perpendicular to substrate. And, all the grains can vertically penetrate the entire film thickness. As a result, the planar-heterojunction perovskite solar cells with these textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films show the remarkably improved PCEs with the average one of 17.11% and the highest one up to 18.64%. And, the notorious J-V hysteresis is also alleviated substantially in the champion cell. More importantly, the champion cell can maintain 86% its initial PCE after storage of 20 days in ambient with the relative humidity of 10-20%. Such desired performance features can be mainly attributed to the much reduced non-radiative recombination sites as well as greatly improved transport property of charge carriers in the textured   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films. This work represents an important step toward realizing high-quality   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films for efficient optoelectronic devices especially perovskite solar cells. And, we envision it can be generalized to other OTP films such as   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbBr_{3}  $ ,   $  HC(NH_{2})_{2}PbI_{3}  $ ,   $  CsPbI_{3}  $ , and the mixtures thereof.

Materials and reagents. All chemicals are of analytical grade and used without further purification.   $  PbI_{2}  $   (99.999%), DMF (99.8%), DMSO (  $  \geq99.9\%  $  ), anhydrous acetonitrile (99.8%), 4-tert-Butylpyridine (TBP, 96%), bis(trifluoromethane)sulfonimide lithium salt (Li-TFSI, 99.95%), hydroiodic acid (No stabilizer, 57 wt.% in   $  H_{2}O  $  ), and methylamine (33 wt. % in absolute ethanol) were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich. Chlorobenzene (99.5%) was purchased from Aladdin Reagents. Absolute ethanol, diethyl ether, and acetone were purchased from Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd. (China). Spiro-MeOTAD,   $  \geq99\%  $   was purchased from Shenzhen Feiming Science and Technology Co., Ltd. (China).

$  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $  synthesis. 30 mL Hydroiodic acid was added dropwise to 25 mL of methylamine under  $  N_{2}  $  atmosphere with vigorous stirring at 0 °C for 2 h. The solvent was evaporated with a rotary evaporator at 70 °C, and the resultant product was washed twice with diethyl ether. Then, it was redissolved in ethanol at 70 °C, recrystallized by diethyl ether at room temperature, and dried at 60 °C in a vacuum oven for 24 h. Thus, the  $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $  powder was obtained.

Solar cell fabrication. The   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   precursor film was firstly deposited on the   $  TiO_{2}/FTO  $   glass substrates by the Lewis base adduct based one-step spin-coating method proposed by Park et al.  $  ^{13}  $   Specifically, 50   $  \mu  $  L precursor solution with the premixed 160 mg   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}I  $ , 461 mg   $  PbI_{2}  $ , 78 mg DMSO, and 600 mg DMF was dropped on the   $  TiO_{2}/FTO  $   glass substrates, which were followed by spinning at 4500 rpm for 20 s. During the initial 6 s of spin process, 500   $  \mu  $  L diethyl ether was added dropwise on the substrates. After spinning, the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   precursor films were formed. These films were crystallized to tetragonal   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   by the face-down annealing as following. Firstly, the   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   precursor film coated   $  TiO_{2}/FTO  $   glass substrates were placed face-up on hotplate (the hotplate surface was polished with sandpaper repeatedly prior to use) and pre-heated in batches at the temperatures of RT, 40 °C, and 60 °C for 30 s. Subsequently, all the films were turned face-down, and annealed at 100 °C for 10 min. After being removed from the hotplate, a series of blackish   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   films were thus obtained. To fulfill the solar cell fabrication, 100 nm spiro-MeOTAD layer was coated on   $  CH_{3}NH_{3}PbI_{3}  $   film by spinning of 100   $  \mu  $  L premixed solution of 72.5 mg spiro-MeOTAD, 28   $  \mu  $  L TBP, 17.5   $  \mu  $  L Li-TFSI in acetonitrile, and 1 mL chlorobenzene at 3000 rpm for 20 s. After being stored in dry box for 24 h, the Ag top anode with the thickness of ~100 nm was deposited by thermal evaporation method. Thus, the complete solar cells were obtained.
